**Wondrous Item (Ring), Uncommon**

![[Ring of Eldritch Precision.webp|500]]

This simple silver ring is adorned with a small, glowing sapphire that occasionally sparks with electricity. Created using a powerful Vial of Lightning, it enhances the Warlock's Eldritch Blast.

While wearing this ring, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls with your Eldritch Blast cantrip. When you cast Eldritch Blast, a small sliver of lightning leads the path between you and your target, enhancing the accuracy of the blast.
