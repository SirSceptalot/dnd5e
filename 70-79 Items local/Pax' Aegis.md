*_Armor (half-plate), unique_*

**Description:** This masterfully crafted half-plate armor is designed to reflect the strength and nobility of [[Pax au Telemanus]]. The armor plates are forged from a rare alloy, shimmering with a deep, metallic gold hue, symbolizing the grandeur of House Telemanus. Each plate is meticulously engraved with scenes of valor and unity, echoing the Telemanus family's commitment to justice and honor.

**Properties:**

- **Armor Class (AC):** Being half-plate, Pax's Aegis provides a base AC of 15 + the wearer's Dexterity modifier (max 2).
- **Bulwark of Honor:** While wearing this armor, the wearer gains a +1 bonus to all Charisma (Persuasion) checks, embodying the charismatic leadership of the Telemanus lineage.

**History:** Pax's Aegis was crafted not only as a suit of armor but also as a symbol of the enduring legacy of the House of Telemanus. Wearing it is a statement of one's commitment to the ideals of courage, leadership, and unwavering loyalty.
