_Wondrous item, unique_

**Description:** This golden ring, embedded with a mysterious, ever-shifting gem, possesses the remarkable ability to record and replay events.

**Properties:**

1. **Recording:** The ring continuously records all events within a 30-foot radius of its location.
    
2. **Playback:** The playback feature of the ring is restricted to specific individuals predetermined by the ring's creator. Designated individuals can activate the playback, which projects holographic images of the recorded events in a 10-foot cube area within 5 feet of the ring. They can manipulate the playback with hand gestures to pause, rewind, fast-forward, or zoom in on details.
    
3. **Security Feature:** The ring is magically protected against unauthorized use, tampering, and magical detection methods such as "Detect Magic," ensuring its recordings remain secure and hidden.

Worn by [[Pax au Telemanus]]
