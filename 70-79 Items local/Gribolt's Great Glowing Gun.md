_Legendary Siege Weapon_

This cannon is a marvel of arcane engineering, born of Magister Gribolt's ingenuity and fueled by rare magical components, including Workshop Portal Gems and a Nightmare Beast Focus Gem. Designed to provide unparalleled firepower, it is a weapon of devastating capability, capable of turning the tide of any battle.

---

**Weapon Type**: Siege Cannon  
**Category**: Legendary Artillery

---

#### **Specifications**

- **Damage**: 8d10 force damage.
- **Range**:
    - Normal: 600 feet
    - Extended: 2400 feet (with disadvantage on the attack roll).
- **Ammunition**: None required; the cannon fires bolts of pure arcane energy.
- **Recharge**: After each shot, the cannon requires a brief moment to recalibrate and recharge. This translates to the following mechanics:
    - The cannon can only be fired once per **3 rounds** of combat (approximately 18 seconds).

---

#### **Operation**

- **Crew Requirement**: The cannon requires a minimum of 3 crew members to operate efficiently: one to aim, one to manage power flow, and one to handle targeting adjustments.
    - If fewer than 3 crew members are present, the cannon takes an additional round to recharge after each shot.
- **Targeting**: Attacks made with Gribolt's Great Glowing Gun require a ranged attack roll, using the following formula for attack bonuses:
    - **Attack Roll**: +10 to hit.
- **Area of Effect (Optional)**: Instead of targeting a single creature or object, the cannon can be set to overload, discharging energy in a 30-foot radius.
    - All creatures in the area must make a **DC 18 Dexterity saving throw**, taking half damage on a success.

---

#### **Special Features**

- **Nightmare Infusion**: The Nightmare Beast Focus Gem grants the cannon a faintly pulsating, otherworldly glow. Once per day, the cannon can unleash a **Devastation Beam**, doubling its damage output (16d10 force damage) at the cost of becoming nonfunctional for the next 24 hours.
- **Portability**: Though technically a siege weapon, the Workshop Portal Gems integrated into its design allow the cannon to be transported and deployed via teleportation circles created by the ship's artificers.

---

#### **Durability and Vulnerabilities**

- **Hit Points**: 300 (Siege Weapon, AC 15)
- **Resistance**: Damage from nonmagical weapons.
- **Vulnerability**: Psychic damage (due to the Nightmare Gem’s volatile nature).

---

#### **Lore**

Crafted aboard the Bastion by Magister Gribolt, this cannon is not merely a weapon—it is a testament to the ingenuity of those who dare to blend magic and machinery. Its brilliant, glowing barrel radiates power, a silent promise of destruction to any who would oppose its wielder.
