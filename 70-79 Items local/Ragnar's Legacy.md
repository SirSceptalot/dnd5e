_Greataxe +1, unique_

**Description:** Forged from the iron of [[Ragnar]]'s own armor and infused with his ashes, this greataxe is a somber yet powerful tribute to a fallen warrior. The weapon retains traces of its origin, with subtle, ash-grey streaks marbling the dark metal of the blade. The haft is sturdy, made from reinforced wood and wrapped in leather for a firm grip.

The weapon was commissioned by [[Kavax au Telemanus]], and given to [[Pax au Telemanus]] when he embarked on his journey to the outside world.

**Properties:**

- **Weight:** The greataxe is heavier than a standard one, reflecting its unique materials and construction. It weighs approximately 15 pounds.
- **Sentiment of the Fallen:** While not magical in nature, the enormous weapon seems to resonate with the strength and spirit of its namesake. When wielding Ragnar's Legacy outside of combat, the user gains advantage on Intimidation checks, embodying the fearsome presence of Ragnar himself.
- **Durability:** Due to the exceptional quality of materials and craftsmanship, Ragnar's Legacy is more durable than standard weapons. It has increased resistance to damage and wear.

**History:** This greataxe is not just a weapon but a symbol of the enduring legacy of Ragnar. It carries the weight of his ideals and the memory of his unyielding spirit, inspiring those who knew him to fight with honor and strength.
