Used by [[Tiziano]].

## Command Nature:

You may cast Grasping Vine at will, but does not require concentration

You may cast Plant Growth at will

## Roots of the World:

You may cast Transport Via Plants up to 2 times per day without expending a spell slot.

## Imbue with Nature’s Wrath:

Once per day, as an action, you may enhance a creature’s attacks with elemental energy. All effects (attacks, spells and cantrips) that require an attack roll, when they hit, they deal an additional 1d6 damage. The type of damage may be picked from; fire, cold, lightning, thunder and bludgeoning.

This effect ends when that creature takes a long rest.

## The Cycle Unending:

When you die your body is reduced to a single seed pod, containing your soul.

When planted into fertile ground, it grows into a new body for you in 24 hours. You are resurrected and gain the benefits of a long rest.
