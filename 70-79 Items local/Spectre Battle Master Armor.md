**Armor (plate), very rare (requires attunement)**

This exquisite set of +1 adamantine full plate armor was crafted at great monetary cost by the finest craftsmen and magic users of the city of Amaroth and the elite military organization Paragon. It was specially commissioned for Legion, a Warforged paladin with the rank of Spectre, by the archmage Verras to protect him during a crucial and dangerous mission.

**Armor Class (AC):** 19 (plate +1)
**Weight:** 65 lbs

**Properties:**
- **Adamantine Armor:** While you’re wearing this armor, any critical hit against you becomes a normal hit.
- **Lightning Retribution:** Any creature that hits you with a melee attack while you’re wearing this armor takes 1d4 lightning damage.

**Appearance:** The armor is a stunning blend of white and gold with an exquisite finish, a testament to the skill and dedication of its creators, despite being produced in record time for such a special set of kit.

**History:** 
The Spectre Battle Master Armor was enhanced by Weiss, the first Warforged, using a Vial of Lightning. This enhancement imbues the armor with the ability to retaliate with a jolt of electricity against those who dare to strike its wearer.

**Attunement:** This armor requires attunement by a paladin.

**Notes:** This armor was specifically made for Legion, a Warforged paladin, but can be worn by any paladin who meets the attunement requirements. The legacy and the powerful magic infused within it make it a prized possession for any who don it.

## Modifications

**Stormplate Rebuke**  
_Armor Enhancement (requires attunement)_

This enhancement channels the power from a [[vial of lightning]] into the reinforced frame of the armor, imbuing it with a crackling electric aura. Whenever a creature strikes the wearer of this armor with a melee attack, they feel the sting of the storm. The attacker takes **1d4 lightning damage** automatically as arcs of energy lash out from the armor upon contact.

The lightning energy does not differentiate friend from foe, and this effect triggers regardless of the attack's source. The wearer of the armor feels a faint hum and warmth as the energy courses through the plating, serving as a constant reminder of the storm's latent power.
