## Description
The [[Bell of Saint Solar Macharius]], which used to belong to the Nation of [[Ashein]], must be transported to [[Avandar]].
Besides the actual artifact, a number of decoys will be transported at the same time. 

In return we'll receive an adamantine full plate armor (to fit me).

---
## Scope
We will be given on of the bells, either the genuine article or a decoy. This item is to be transported to [[Ashein]] and be handed over to the [[High Elf]] [[Faynaigen]] in the [[Vanyarin]] district.
