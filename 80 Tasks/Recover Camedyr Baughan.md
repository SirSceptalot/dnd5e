[[0835-09-18]]: The cleric [[Camedyr Baughan]] has gone missing after venturing into [[Nivemer's Stand]] to see what the increased paranormal activity there was.
We take it upon ourselves to try and rescue the devout believer.

[[0835-09-19]]: Entrance into Nivemer's Stand.
We find a glowing rock in a metal stand, which seems like a significant object.
After going to the [[Temple of Pelor in Collegento]] a man called [[Leandros]] joins our mission, but he can't tell what the object is either.

[[0835-09-20]]: We're in the right place, as we find a diary belonging to one of the adventurers hired to protect Camedyr. [[Diary of an adventurer]].

[[0835-09-21 (alternate timeline)]]: An odd ghoul called [[Zorth]] has seen Camedyr around, he calls him "the man with the poetry".

Camedyr chooses to fight against us with a creature known only as [[The Child]]. Despite our attempts to disable him without killing him, he succumbs to his injuries and expires.
We take his remains with us back through the portal to [[Nivemer's Stand]].

[[0835-09-20 (continued)]]: We deliver the remains of Camedyr to the [[Temple of Sarenrae in Collegento]], hereby closing this mission.
