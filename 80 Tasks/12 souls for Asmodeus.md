## Description
In return for his help in our mission to [[Save Nivemera's soul]], [[Varius]] requires me to obtain one soul for [[Asmodeus]] each month for the duration of one year, starting next month ([[0835-10-01]]).

Each time someone dies nearby I can use the [[Soul Cage]] provided to hold the soul. In the next hour, a decision must then be made to either release the soul or send it to [[Asmodeus]].

If I miss delivery during a certain month, my own soul is at stake. If I perish during such a period, it will go to Asmodeus, finishing the mission early.
The next month, two souls must be provided then, and the total number of souls is extended to 13 (one month extra service).

## Scope
