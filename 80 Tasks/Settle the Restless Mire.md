## Description
The [[Restless Mire]] in the north of [[Ashein]] is inhabited by many undead, and has been for as long as people can remember.
For a long time, there has been an unspoken truce between the mire and the rest of the nation. The people leave the undead alone, and they stay in their territory.

Lately however, they're trying to spread beyond the protective barriers.

There is rumoured to be at least one shard of divinity somewhere in the mire, and the suspicion is that the recent trouble is due to a necromancer stirring trouble while seeking for one of these.

---
## Scope
The reason that is driving undead beyond the barriers containing them must be removed.
What the reason is and how the situation is resolved is of no consequence.

Only the reason for the current unnatural behavior (for undead) is relevant here.
Natural tendencies or motivations that any undead inhabitant of the mire may have not related to the recent abnormal activity is not within the scope of this project.

## Updates
### [[0836-02-14]]
We leave [[Esterna]] north, towards the [[Restless Mire]].

### [[0836-02-16]]
Arrival at the mire after dark.
While we watch, a number of zombies make an intelligent and coordinated effort to get a few of their number across the magical barrier keeping them in, so we dispatch them.

### [[0836-02-17]]
We have a few fights against undead and meet a [[Bruxa]], who we have a short conversation with.

### [[0836-02-19]]
We find the necromancer, who turns out to be none other than [[Krisius]] and the [[Shambling Lord]]. The latter was held captive by the former, but after fighting them both this is no longer the case.

### [[0836-02-20]]
We meet the [[Bruxa]], [[Verena]] again twice, once at night and once later that same morning.
She was able to capture [[Krisius]] and was going to give him to the [[Shambling Lord]] as a gift so they could rule the [[Restless Mire]] together.

We manage to convince her to give [[Krisius]] to us in return for killing the [[Shambling Lord]]. Besides giving us [[Krisius]] she will also ensure the creatures inside the [[Restless Mire]] will stay inside and behave.

Late at night, we do indeed manage to kill the [[Shambling Lord]] and [[Verena]] keeps her end of the bargain.

### [[0836-02-21]]
We start moving out the swamp and towards [[Esterna]] again.

### [[0836-02-24]]
Arrival in [[Esterna]] and debriefing by [[Galerin]].

## Conclusion
We have managed to get the creatures that inhabit the [[Restless Mire]] to stop trying to escape.
The necromancer within turned out to be [[Krisius]], who we managed to extract.

In return, we were given the location of a shard of divinity.
It refused to comply, but that's beyond the scope of this mission.
