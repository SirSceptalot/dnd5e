## Description
A ship's captain, [[Gilmer Chatan]], is paying 3000 gold coins for protection during an expedition to an island up north.
He needs to go there to find a magical artifact that can control the weather.

---
## Scope
[[Gilmer Chatan]] must be kept alive from when the ship departs to when the ship returns to [[Jarnhov]].
Keeping the rest of the crew alive is technically outside of the scope, although ideally they should be protected as well.

While on the ship, a certain amount of work is requested.

Additional rewards may be negotiated if further assistance is required during the mission.
