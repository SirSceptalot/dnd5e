## Description
Several individuals have gone missing in [[Iglberg]]. Little else is known at this time.


---
## Scope
Find out what happened to the missing individuals.
If they are held against their will, free them.
If they are dead, return their bodies or indicate their whereabouts.
If they have gone on their own volition, report this.

---
## Updates
[[0835-11-12]]: Departure from [[Amaroth]] to [[Iglberg]].
[[0835-11-17]]: Arrival in [[Iglberg]]. The [[Lady of the Woods]] is the most likely suspect. She has the help of [[Yeth Hound|Yeth Hounds]] and [[Redcap|Redcaps]].
The victims were probably charmed by remotely activated magical devices, several of which were still hidden in the houses of people not yet victimized.
[[0835-11-18]]: We go hunt for the [[Lady of the Woods]] and manage to defeat her, although she's able to escape by turning invisible.
All missing persons are safely returned home.
[[0835-11-19]]: We start the trip back to [[Amaroth]].
[[0835-11-25]]: Arrival in [[Amaroth]]. Administrator [[Dreali]] gives us the promised 1000 Gold coins, thereby ending this mission.

Missing persons have been returned, the [[Lady of the Woods]] of has been driven off. Although she was not killed or apprehended, this was not part of the project scope.
