## Description
During the battle for [[Collegento]], the [[Erinyes]] [[Tisiphone]] fires a magically infused ballista bolt at [[Bastion]], killing him instantly and summoning the [[Pit Fiend]] [[Hadak]] in his place.

He makes bold claims and is likely to seek domination of [[Skogsland]] at least, possible more.


---
## Scope
[[Hadak]] must be stopped in whatever way possible.
Ideally, he must be imprisoned. However, he is a very immediate threat and even killing his current body would be acceptable, even though this would enable him to regenerate in hell and return at a later time.
