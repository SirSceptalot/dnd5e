[[0835-09-20 (continued)]]: We take it upon ourselves to save [[Nivemera]] from eternal torment.
She was cursed by [[Pelor]] because she promised her soul to [[The Gatekeeper]].
She did this because Pelor refused to help her stop Skogsland's army from ravaging her city, where The Gatekeeper had no such issues.

Her soul is encased in a large soul gem, which has been cursed by Pelor to provide eternal unspeakable torment.

We enlist the help of [[Varius]], a cleric of [[Asmodeus]].
He does agree to help, although he wants something in return:
[[12 souls for Asmodeus]].

He gets an underling, [[Runa]], to help us retrieve the soul from her prison.

We manage to free the soul, and it dutifully retreats into the provided silver soul cage.

We'll consider this case closed, as the soul is now safe in our possession.

There is a followup mission to find a permanent container for her soul, as she agrees to accompany us in our adventures.

New mission: [[Safe container for Nivemera]].
