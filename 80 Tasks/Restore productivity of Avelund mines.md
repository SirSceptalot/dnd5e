## Description

Shipments from the mines of [[Avelund]] stopped unexpectedly.
It's said that a new local religion would be the cause.

---
## Scope
Resolve whatever issues are preventing material production and transport from [[Avelund]] to [[Balthazar Gelt]] in [[Amaroth]].

---

## Updates
[[0835-09-28]]: We leave for Avelund.
[[0835-10-07]]: Checkpoint: Velgend.
[[0835-10-12]]: Arrival in [[Avelund]]. A man called [[Behektur]] is the cause of the trouble. The issue is not just that the mines were stopped, the whole city was taken over. He uses a mix of force and addictive substances to keep his people subjugated.

[[0835-10-13]]: Behektur uses a mutated hill giant called Jericho to periodically retrieve a small crate from the mines. We plan to interrupt this supply line by halting production of whatever this is at the mines.
[[Ophelia]] will try to stop outgoing carrier pigeons from reaching their destination.

[[0835-10-14]]: Behektur needs celestial and draconic blood to craft Ambrosia, the magical substance he uses to transform his subjects and keep them under his rule - it's highly addictive.
The celestial blood he gets from an angel he trapped in a mirror in the mines. We manage to free the angel [[Viantius]].
His source of draconic blood is so far unknown.

[[0835-10-15]]: a giant trap is being built by the [[Avelund Resistance]] to help us defeat [[Jericho]].
They're also crafting weapons to arm the masses in the coming uprising.
Jericho kills [[Hershel McLachlan]] and destroys all blacksmith shops in the outskirts after [[Emerson Hale]] turns out to be a traitor and informs the [[Exaltators]] of the resistance's plans. Before his death, [[Hershel McLachlan]] told us to go find a woman named [[Almira Maiden]].

[[0835-10-16]]: [[Tiziano]] finds out that [[Behektur]] ships [[Ambrosia]] off to a red dragon on an island.
This also means that a trade agreement with this red dragon is the most likely source for the dragon blood, which is an ingredient for the drug.
It seems Behektur finds the price a bit steep, which would be why he has sent [[Jericho]] out to capture a young white dragon.
There has also been a shortage of Ambrosia, leading their mage [[Krisius]] to experiment with alternate ingredients.
We reach [[Almira Maiden]] later that day who turns out to be the actual leader of the [[Avelund Resistance]], with [[Hershel McLachlan]] as her cover and main operative.
After another attack from us on a group of [[Exaltators]], all Exaltators retreat to the inner city to hold the line there.

[[0835-10-17]]: Arinya is given information via her patron that something will happen to upset the balance in 2 days. The most likely event is an incursion by a band of orcs.
The [[Exaltators]] kill [[Lala]].


[[0835-10-18]]: The [[Orc|Orcs]] attack the inner city, and basically wipe out all traces of the [[Exaltators]]. [[Behektur]] and [[Krisius]] manage to escape.

[[0835-10-19]]: A Void Dragon named Elterion comes looking for Behektur and Jericho.

[[0835-10-20]]: We deal with the Kobolt infestation in the mines, and take the miners along to restart production. This finishes our mission here.
Final step is to go back to [[Amaroth]] to officially conclude the mission.

---

## Conclusion
We report back to Weezard and are given the promised reward: [[Nivemera's Locket]].
