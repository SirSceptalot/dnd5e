Several farmers have been killed in [[Iglberg]].
We arrive here on [[0835-07-31]] to try and solve this mystery on request of administrator [[Emil]].

The same day we discover the creature is actually a woman named [[Helmine]] that was transformed after a failed ritual given to her by he [[Lady of the Woods]], which also cost the life of her cheating husband [[Volker]].

We confront her and try to talk her into coming with us peacefully, but sadly she declines and falls at our hands.
