## Description

The free city of [[Collegento]] has been overthrown by the [[Temple of Asmodeus in Collegento]], which is not acceptable to either [[Ashein]] and [[Skogsland]].

The temple has slowly been rising in prominence and power until they were strong enough to make a violent rush for absolute power, with the help of a number of forces of hell that they summoned.
Both [[Ashein]] and [[Skogsland]] will send a delegation to handle the situation.


---
## Scope
Power over the city must be removed from the followers of [[Asmodeus]].
The original government must be restored, or an interim government set up.
