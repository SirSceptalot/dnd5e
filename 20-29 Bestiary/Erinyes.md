Legend has it that the first Erinyes were angels that fell from the Upper Planes due to misdeeds or mistakes.
They generally look beyond beautiful and are not afraid to use and abuse their looks to get what they want.

In the third layer of hell is the city of [[Dis]], where they are used plentiful as guards and torturers.
