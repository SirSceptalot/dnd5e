## Description

Verras is a beautiful mature elven woman.
Red of hair and emerald eyes with high cheekbones and a slim build, everything about her appearance is conventionally beautiful.
Even her dark green wizard's robe has been adjusted to fit her body more tightly and show off her body.
Though the look of wisdom beyond her years in her eyes betrays that there is more to this elf than just beauty.

## Occupation
