## Description

He presents the outward appearance of a clown, though his abilities suggest much more beneath the surface. At a minimum, he possesses the power to conjure portals seemingly from nothingness and navigates the enigmatic [[Bibliotheca Aeterna]]—a labyrinthine repository where others often find themselves hopelessly trapped for eternity—with an ease that defies explanation.

Formerly, he served under the [[Whisperer]], a figure controlling the [[Bibliotheca Aeterna|Black Library]]. However, circumstances shifted when we eliminated the Whisperer, and he subsequently assumed control of the library's operations.
