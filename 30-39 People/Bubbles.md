## Race - Bunny Necromancer

As a bunny necromancer, you are a small, fluffy creature with the power to animate the dead and control their bodies. You have a mischievous nature and a love of exploring and experimenting with the limits of your powers.

## Ability Score Increase
You can choose one ability score to increase by 2, and one ability score to increase by 1.

## Age
Bunny necromancers mature quickly and live for up to 10 years.

## Alignment
Bunny necromancers tend towards chaotic alignments, as their powers can be unpredictable and their curiosity often leads them to bend or break the rules.

## Size
Bunny necromancers stand about 1 foot tall and weigh between 1 and 2 pounds. Your size is Tiny.

## Speed
Your base walking speed is 35 feet.
You have a burrow speed of 5 feet.

## Hit points
Your hit die is 2 size smaller than the normal hit die for your class.

## Abilities


### Minor illusion
While piloting a corpse or suit of bones, you can cast [[Minor Illusion]] on yourself for the purpose of appearing like a living, healthy version of whatever race it was in life.

### Lucky feet
When you're not piloting a corpse, enemies have disadvantage on all attacks.

### Languages:
You can speak, read, and write Common and Sylvan
