## Description

Ecbert is stern-faced, lean man entering his late forties. He has short, greying hair and is clean shaven. His nose is slightly hooked and his eyes a dark brown.
He carries himself with an aura of authority and dignity, wearing a robe reminiscent of a roman toga.

## Occupation

Member of the [[Council of Archmages]], Ecbert is currently [[Skogsland|Skogsland's]] Archmage of Defense.

He is also the leader of [[Paragon]], a small group of Military Police founded to combat the worst of war crimes during the war between Skogsland and Ashein.
