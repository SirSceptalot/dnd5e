## Description

Male, human, late 30's
Very nondescript looking, brown short hair, brown eyes
Easily forgettable if not for claw scars running down the right side of his neck.

Paladin that used to live in the [[Sacrosanct Monastery]], now lives in the [[Eternal City]], leading the resistance against the now dominant population of vampires.
