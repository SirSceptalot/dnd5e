## Character description

![Pax au Telemanus](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/red-rising/images/8/80/Pax-au-telemanus-by-magali-villeneuve.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200427004230)
\- Pax au Telemanus with the 185cm tall Virginia au Augustus

Pax au Telemanus, the son of [[Kavax au Telemanus]], is a remarkable figure even among the already impressive Gold caste. He towers at an imposing 2,24 meters and possesses the robust physique characteristics of an Obsidian. While it's true that many Obsidian reach greater heights, typically between 210 and 275 cm, the only *Gold* Pax knows to be taller than him is his 2,26 meter tall brother Daxo.

Pax is a surprisingly energetic talker once he gets going, with a penchant for complimenting both friend and foe in his stories. He is known for yelling out his name as a war cry during a battle, a trait he took from his father.

His entire childhood, Pax would sneak off and join the Obsidians in their training, sparring and fighting with them to test his strength. His parents have raised him to lead Obsidian legions into battle.

At the age of 18, Pax prepares to go to The Institute, a ruthless proving ground for the ruling Gold caste which aims to identify and mold the future leaders of the Society.

Only the best students of their year may earn the title of "Peerless Scarred", and are given the tell-tale facial scar by their father or patron.

## World building

### The Society of Colors

The Society of Colors, hidden from the rest of the world, lies isolated by a formidable and perpetual storm system. Legends whisper of a colossal generational vessel, carrying thousands of men, which once braved these treacherous storms. The vessel met its fate against the relentless fury of nature, leaving its passengers stranded on an unknown archipelago. These survivors, whose job was indicated by their colored uniform on the ship, kept working together on the island as they did on the ship.
Guided by Gold, all colors maintained a strict regime of forced selection through breeding, constantly selecting the most successful of each Color for the production of progeny. Apart from the physical and mental aspects, each Color's culture was groomed as well.

**Red** became more physically enduring, resilient and hardworking, and traditional family values were strongly promoted and valued.

**Whites** were selected for fairness and moral integrity, which, coupled with their culture defines a breed becomes nearly physically ill if they were to try to tell a lie.

**Grey** has unwavering discipline and tactical intelligence, just as the **Violets** have unrivaled artistic creativity, and **Pinks** have unrivaled beauty and stunning grace.

Although all Colors have undergone significant physical and psychological changes, the greatest adaptation are without a doubt in the Obsidian and Gold castes.

While **Gold** is no match for Obsidian in pure physical prowess, they still improved their own physique in order to be able to maintain superiority over other Colors. They selected heavily for stronger, faster, and more resilient specimen, while maintaining a grace, dexterity and elegance that other races can only dream of.

**Obsidians** are the epitome of physical prowess, possessing superhuman strength, durability, and resilience. They can lift incredible weights, endure extreme physical exertion, and withstand injuries that would fell lesser beings. Their agility and reflexes are equally formidable, allowing them to move with devastating speed and precision on the battlefield. Even a Gold spine can be crushed by an Obsidian grip.

### Link to Skogsland

A torn piece of scripture written by the Obsidians, the monstrous Color that has been bred for war and raised to believe the Golds are actual living gods, somehow was lost to sea and eventually, through chance, found it's way into the hands of [[Balthazar Gelt]]. The scripture spoke of "Gold Gods," beings of immense power and majesty. Misinterpreting these writings, Gelt embarked on a transformative journey, altering his very essence to become a creature wrought from living gold, believing this would elevate him to god-like status.

It was only after his transformation that Gelt uncovered the origin of the scripture and teleported across realms to the Society of  Colors. Here, he encountered House Telemanus, one of the ruling Gold families.

Gelt's arrival sparked curiosity and intrigue. After negotiations, an unprecedented arrangement was struck: Pax au Telemanus, the youngest Telemanus son, would accompany Gelt back to his world. The purpose was threefold: Pax would embark on a journey of discovery and adventure, seeking to earn honor and recognition akin to becoming a Peerless Scarred. His deeds and actions would be meticulously recorded by a special ring that Gelt would craft, ensuring that his journey would be remembered and judged upon his return. In exchange, Gelt would be granted the chance to study Pax's unique physiology, and House Telemanus would gain access to the knowledge and magical insights Pax gathered.

Thus, Pax arrived in Gelt's world, finding himself in a towering edifice of gold, the wizard's sanctum. After Gelt studied Pax for a few days, Pax set forth to explore this new and wondrous land, filled with magic and diverse beings unlike any he had known. His first great challenge would be to join a hunt organized by one of the land's archmages, a quest that would lead him to cross paths with other adventurers, each with their own tales and destinies.

#### Rage
**Level 1**
On your turn, you can enter a rage as a bonus action. Lasts for 1 minute, or until unconscious or haven't attacked or taken damage for 1 round.

While raging, you gain the following benefits if you aren’t wearing heavy armor:

- Advantage on STR checks and saving throws.
- Bonus dmg with STR melee weapon. See [[#Character Progression Plan]]
- Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

#### Unarmored Defense
**Level 1**
While you are not wearing any armor, your Armor Class equals 10 + DEX mod + CON mod.

#### Gold Superiority
**Level 1**
You get a +1 constitution bonus

#### Gold Rush
After STR, DEX or CON ability or skill check, +1d4 once per long rest.

#### Feat: Great Weapon Master
**Level 1**
- When you score a critical hit with a melee weapon or reduce a creature to 0 hit points with one, you can make one melee weapon attack as a bonus action.

- Choose -5  to attack roll for +10 to damage. you make a melee attack with a heavy weapon that you are proficient with, you can choose 

#### Reckless Attack
**Level 2**
On your first attack, you can decide to take advantage on STR melee attack rolls, but attack rolls against you have advantage until your next turn.

#### Danger Sense
**Level 2**
You have advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects that you can see, such as traps and spells.

#### Primal Path 1
##### Frenzy
**Level 3**
You can go into a frenzy when you rage. 1 melee weapon attack as a bonus action on each of your turns after this one. When your rage ends, you suffer one level of [exhaustion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/compendium/rules/basic-rules/appendix-a-conditions#Exhaustion).

#### Feat: Mobile
**Level 4**
- Your speed increases by 10 feet (to 40)
- When you use the Dash action, difficult terrain doesn't cost you extra movement on that turn.
- When you make a melee attack against a creature, you don't provoke opportunity attacks from that creature for the rest of the turn, whether you hit or not.

#### Extra Attack
**Level 5**
Gain a second attack.

#### Fast Movement
**Level 5**
Speed increases by 10 (to **50**) feet while not in heavy armor.

#### Primal Path 2
##### Mindless Rage
**Level 6**
You can’t be [charmed](https://www.dndbeyond.com/compendium/rules/basic-rules/appendix-a-conditions#Charmed) or [frightened](https://www.dndbeyond.com/compendium/rules/basic-rules/appendix-a-conditions#Frightened) while raging. If you are charmed or frightened when you enter your rage, the effect is suspended for the duration of the rage.

#### Feral Instinct
**Level 7**
Advantage on initiative rolls.

#### Brutal Critical 1
**Level 9**
Roll one additional weapon damage die for a critical hit with a melee attack.

#### Primal Path 3
##### Intimidating Presence
**Level 10**
Action to frighten a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you, DC 8 + proficiency + CHA mod. if the creature can see or hear you, until the end of your next turn. On subsequent turns, action to extend the duration of this effect.
Ends if the creature ends its turn out of line of sight or more than 60 feet away from you.

If the creature succeeds on its saving throw, you can’t use this feature on that creature again for 24 hours.

#### Relentless Rage
**Level 11**
While raging, DC10 CON save to drop to stay at 1 hit point instead of dropping to 0 hit points.

DC increases by 5 per use until a short or long rest.

#### Increase STR by 2 (20)
**Level 12**
Increase Strength from 18 to 20.

#### Brutal Critical 2
**Level 13**
Roll two additional weapon damage dice for a critical hit with a melee attack.

#### Primal Path 4
##### Retaliation
**Level 14**
When you take damage from a creature that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to make a melee weapon attack against that creature.

#### Persistant Rage
**Level 15**
Your rage ends early only if you fall [unconscious](https://www.dndbeyond.com/compendium/rules/basic-rules/appendix-a-conditions#Unconscious) or if you choose to end it.

#### Increase CON by 2 (18)
**Level 16**

#### Brutal Critical 3
**Level 17**
Roll three additional weapon damage dice for a critical hit with a melee attack.

#### Indomitable Might
**Level 18**
If your total for a Strength check is less than your Strength score, you can use that score in place of the total.

#### Increase CON by 2 (20)
**Level 19**
Increase Constitution from 18 to 20.

#### Primal Champion
**Level 20**
At 20th level, you embody the power of the wilds. Your Strength and Constitution scores increase by 4. Your maximum for those scores is now 24.
