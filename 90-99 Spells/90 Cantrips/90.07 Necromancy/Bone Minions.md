_Necromancy cantrip_

- **Casting Time:** 1 round per minion
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of bone dust or a collection of tiny bones)
- **Duration:** 10 minutes

As you cast this spell, you summon from your materials tiny skeletal creatures no taller than a sitting bunny. These skeletal minions are fragile and poorly suited for any thing on a scale larger than the minute.

You can create up to 1 minion per wizard level. If you try to create any beyond that, the oldest existing minion crumbles to dust.

**Minion Statistics:**

- **Hit Points:** 1
- **Armor Class:** 9
- **Speed:** 5 ft.


- **STR** 2 (-4)
- **DEX** 8 (-1)
- **CON** 3 (-4)
- **INT** 1 (-5)
- **WIS** 3 (-4)
- **CHA** 1 (-5)

|              | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| ------------ | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Score**    | 2   | 8   | 3   | 1   | 3   | 1   |
| **Modifier** | -4  | -1  | -4  | -5  | -4  | -5  |

- **Attacks:** If equipped with a tiny weapon, they can make an attack roll with no bonus to hit, dealing 1 point of damage on a successful hit. They must occupy the same space as their target to attack.

The minions follow your mental commands, performing tasks like fetching small items, activating simple mechanisms, or assisting with minor distractions in combat. They can operate within 30 feet of you; beyond this range, they can continue the last command given but cannot receive new commands.



**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 1st level or higher, the duration increases to 10 minutes for each level of the spell slot, and the number of minions you can create and control increases by an amount equal to your wizard level for each level of the spell slot used.
