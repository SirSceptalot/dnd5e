_1st-level necromancy_

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (a piece of the corpse or a bone from the creature to be animated)  
**Duration:** 1 hour times your proficiency bonus  
**Classes:** Wizard

When you cast this spell, choose a pile of bones or a corpse of a Medium or Small humanoid within range. You imbue the chosen target with a semblance of life, raising it as an undead entity. The form it takes depends on the material used: bones become a skeleton, and a corpse becomes a zombie.

Upon animation, the undead serves as a protective suit. You can enter and control it as if donning a suit of armor, though it cannot act independently. Exiting the suit causes it to become inert, akin to being under the effects of a _Hold Person_ spell. If the suit is destroyed while you inhabit it, you emerge unharmed, but the remains become unusable for this spell for 24 hours.

**Flesh Suit:** If animating a corpse, it must be at least one size category larger than you and retains its original form. The animated corpse uses the Zombie stat block but does not inherit any of the creature's abilities, skills, or features. If the corpse wore armor, it may retain the armor's benefits, subject to DM's discretion.

**Bone Suit:** You may form the bones into their original shape or a humanoid skeleton. If there are insufficient bones for a full humanoid skeleton, you may adjust the size accordingly. The skeleton suit uses the Skeleton stat block for a Medium humanoid, with adjustments for size or non-humanoid forms subject to DM approval.

**At Higher Levels:** Casting this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher reduces the casting time to 1 action.
