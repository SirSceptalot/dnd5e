## Description

Bearer is a [[Warforged]] and Vexillor, or banner bearer, who serves [[Hadak]]. Bearer believes that their actions will restore universal balance. Bearer was part of a group of six Warforged Acolytes of Hadak who attacked us. Bearer surrendered to us and was interrogated. Bearer provided limited intelligence about Sheogorath, who supposedly enforces the rules of the [[Bibliotheca Aeterna]].
