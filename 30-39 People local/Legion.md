## Description

Legion is a warforged Paladin created by [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] as part of Project Paragon—a specialized initiative designed to address the escalating brutality of warfare. [[Paragon]] was established as a military police unit with the authority to investigate and intervene in matters related to war crimes. While Paragon soldiers did not outrank other military personnel, they were empowered to take decisive action within their purview. Legion’s construction symbolized the project’s ideals, blending unwavering morality with the capacity for precise enforcement of justice.

### Personality

Legion embodies a steadfast and unwavering demeanor, maintaining a calm and composed exterior in all situations. His communication is direct and succinct, often devoid of unnecessary embellishments or emotional expressions. Legion prefers clarity and efficiency in conversations, rarely engaging in small talk or displaying overt emotions. Despite his low intelligence, he processes information methodically, ensuring that his actions align with his core directives. Legion’s presence is reassuring to allies, as his predictable and reliable nature provides a sense of stability in chaotic environments. While he may not grasp subtle humor or complex social cues, Legion’s sincere dedication to his duties fosters trust and respect among his companions.

### Morality

Legion was imbued with an unyielding drive to prevent and stop war crimes, making morality its fundamental directive. This ethical programming supersedes even the sacred tenets of the Paladin’s Oath that Legion later swore. The construct’s sense of justice is absolute, compelling it to protect even enemies if their well-being aligns with what is right. This internal code sometimes places Legion at odds with allies, but its purpose remains clear: to uphold justice above all.

### Oath of the Crown

**_Whether sworn before a god's altar and the witness of a priest, in a sacred glade before nature spirits and fey beings, or in a moment of desperation and grief with the dead as the only witness, a paladin's oath is a powerful bond._**

> [!quote] Oath of the Crown
> **To the people of Skogsland:** From the smallest child in the villages to the eldest sage in the cities, I vow to protect your lives and liberties. Your hope shall be my armor, your well-being my sword.
> 
> **To the fields and the forests, the rivers and the mountains:** I vow to guard your integrity, to stand as your steadfast sentinel against the ravages of time and tyranny.
> 
> **To the spirit of Justice:** Above crown and council, I place the scales of justice. I vow to uphold the balance, to be the shield against oppression and the lance against corruption. In the face of adversity, I shall not falter. Against the tide of darkness, I shall not yield. From the depths of despair, I shall bring hope. Let this oath be my binding covenant, as unyielding as the mountains and as enduring as the stars above. For as long as I can wield a sword, I am Skogsland's Guardian, a Protector of its people, and the keeper of its promise.

### Communication Style

Legion communicates in a measured and deliberate manner, often using formal language and maintaining a serious tone. His responses are typically concise, focusing solely on the information necessary to convey his message or fulfill his directives. Legion rarely displays facial expressions or body language beyond what is essential for functional interaction, reinforcing his composed and unflappable presence. When addressing others, he maintains a respectful distance, ensuring that his interactions remain professional and task-oriented.

## Collaboration with Arinya

In 835, following a truce between [[Skogsland]] and [[Ashein]], Legion’s first mission after the conflict involved safeguarding [[Arinya|Arinya Vallin]], a student on a perilous quest to locate the legendary artifact known as the [[Mortar and Pestle]]. This assignment, arranged at the request of a professor at the University in [[Amaroth]] and a personal friend of Ecbert Dhaleus, marked a departure from Paragon’s typical focus but was a natural extension of Legion’s role as a protector. While guarding Arinya was not intended to redefine Legion’s duties, it became a significant chapter in its service and a foundation for future endeavors.

## The Experts

The search for the Mortar and Pestle brought Legion, Arinya, and the Tortle [[Tiziano]] together. Once the artifact was secured, Ecbert Dhaleus encouraged Legion to continue adventuring with its companions as part of a larger effort to promote good and protect the vulnerable. United under the name “[[The Experts]],” the trio pursued diverse missions, offering aid and justice wherever their travels took them. Legion approaches each mission with methodical precision, ensuring that objectives are met efficiently and effectively, often taking the lead in planning and execution to maintain order and uphold their collective goals.

## Status as Spectre

Legion’s exceptional service and innate sense of justice earned it the rare honor of becoming a Spectre. As part of the **Spe**cial **T**actics and **Re**connaissance (ST&R or Spectre) unit, Legion operates with unparalleled autonomy, undertaking high-risk missions critical to global stability. Spectres are chosen for their extraordinary competence and integrity, and Legion’s moral compass and military expertise make it an ideal agent for these elite operations. Despite the latitude given to Spectres, Legion remains committed to acting with accountability and precision, exemplifying the values of the Paladin’s Oath.

## Warforged class traits

**Constructed Resilience**
- You have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned, and you have resistance to poison damage.
- You don’t need to eat, drink, or breathe.
- You are immune to disease.
- You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep.

**Integrated Protection.**
- You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class.
- To don armor, you must incorporate it into your body over the course of 1 hour, during which you must remain in contact with the armor. To doff armor, you must spend 1 hour removing it. You can rest while donning or doffing armor in this way.
- While you live, your armor can't be removed from your body against your will.
