#race/variant-human/gold 

![Gold Sigil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/red-rising/images/5/50/Gold.jpg)

The Golds are the result of a long line of selective breeding. 

They have golden irises, gold/blond hair, and are generally over 2 meters tall. To be under 2 meters is considered short among Golds.

Due to their breeding, they tend to live longer than other humans, many easily exceeding 150 years. This also means that even Golds considered to be elderly are still fearsome in battle.

## Gold superiority

A Gold's bone density is higher, as is the tensile strength in their tendons, ligaments and muscle fibers.
Even an untrained Gold can hold their breath for at least 5 minutes.

You get a +1 constitution bonus

## Non-magical heritage

The Gold, as the other members of the Society of Colors, have a distinct heritage that renders them incapable of wielding magic. Due to the many generations of selective breeding without awareness of magic, they lack the innate connection to the arcane and divine energies that other races possess.

You can't cast spells or activate scrolls.

## Gold rush

The Golds, with their extraordinary physical capabilities, have the ability to push their bodies beyond normal limits in moments of need, a testament to their superior physical conditioning and genetic prowess.

**Feature:** Once per long rest, after you make an ability check or a skill check that relies on Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution, you can choose to invoke the "Gold Rush." Roll an additional 1d4 and add the result to the check. This surge represents a momentary peak in your physical abilities, allowing you to overcome challenges that might otherwise be beyond your reach.

**Usage:** You can decide to use Gold Rush after you see the result of the d20 roll. Once used, this feature cannot be used again until you complete a long rest.
