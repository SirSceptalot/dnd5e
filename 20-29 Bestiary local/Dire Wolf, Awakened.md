As a member of the dire wolf race, you are a powerful and fearsome predator with a strong connection to the natural world. You are larger and more imposing than a typical wolf, and your senses are heightened, allowing you to detect threats and prey with ease.

**Ability Score Increase**: Your Strength and Constitution scores increase by 2.

**Darkvision**: You have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

**Keen Senses**: You have advantage on Perception checks that rely on hearing or smell.

**Natural Athlete**: You have proficiency in the Athletics skill.

**Bite**: You can use your action to make a bite attack, dealing 1d6+your Strength modifier piercing damage.

**Pack Tactics**: You have advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

**Languages**: You can speak and read Common. You can communicate with wolves as one of them.

**Size**: You are a Large creature, standing about 4 feet tall at the shoulder and weighing around 500 pounds, and occupy a space of 10 feet by 10 feet. Your reach is 5 feet.

**Speed**: Your base walking speed is 50 feet.
