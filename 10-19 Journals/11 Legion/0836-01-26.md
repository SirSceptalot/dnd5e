[Location:: [[Avandar]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Galguth]]]

We're off to the [[Tellery]] district to find [[Galguth]]. Somehow, [[Tiziano]] actually manages to spot him. [[Galguth]] notices he's been spot and comes over to us to talk.

When it's clear the conversation isn't going anywhere, he takes off again upriver.
We follow him with the guards in tow, and find a cave at the left riverbank where 4 elven girls are eating pick-nick.
They refuse to let us enter the cave, and a fight ensues. Their beautiful appearance quickly drops and they reveal themselves to be [[Sea Hags]].

The cave reveals itself to be quite the complex.
We find numerous things in our exploration:
- Symbol of Kord
- Documents on transporting artifacts via the river
- Documents describing smuggle routes in and around Avandar, written in code
- Healer's kit
- 6 Longswords
- 2 Greatswords
- 1 Platemail armor
- 5 wheels of cheese
- an amount of beef jerky
- a number of dried sausages
- +1 elven chainmail
- Clank Be Gone
- A vault, without the matching key

During our exploration we're forced to fend off a number of [[Yeth Hound|Yeth Hounds]].
At a certain point we exit the cave into the [[Gribenwald]], so the cave connects at two ends it seems.

We encounter 5 bedrooms as well, seemingly belonging to a party of smugglers.

In another part of the cave we go into ever deeper waters, where 3 dead bodies are floating. They clearly had a violent end, as they clearly have wounds and even lungs full of water.
Besides the bodies, we also see a pretty gilded frame, which most likely is some sort of magical portal.

At this point, we're attacked and defeated by [[Galguth]] and several of his minions.
We manage to escape with our lives.

After returning to the city, we report to the guards, and spend our evening in the [[Cracked Goblet]].
