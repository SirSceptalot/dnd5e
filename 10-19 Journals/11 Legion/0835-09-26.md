[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]

We travel onward despite the dreary weather.
The road itself looks safe, with occasionally patrolling [[Warforged]].

We arrive at [[Amaroth]] in the late afternoon. Arinya and I agree that it feels good to be in a familiar and safe location again. It does look like there are fewer Warforged than usual though.

My first stop is [[Paragon]] to report for a debriefing.
I report to [[Reckoning]], who takes me to Corporal [[Hammer]].
They invite me for a night at the [[Metal Toad]] to catch up.

After the debriefing, I ask how my old friend Balthazar Gelt is doing. Quite well it seems, he gradually worked his way up to a wizard of quite high standing, and has transformed his tower into one of pure gold.

We spend the night in [[The Wicked Jester]].
