[Location:: [[Far Realms]]]
Arinya tells of an unsettling dream she had of an infant and a woman telling her to take care of it.
In the dream, she was leached upon by a black tentacle where she was previously holding the child.
There were also a number of emotions involved in the dream which I'll not attempt to understand.

[[Leandros]] professes to a similar dream about the same child.

We return to the mansion where the ghostly figure that previously escaped us is patrolling the inner courtyard. He summons a gigantic warrior, and another battle to remember ensues.

From the [[Dread Knight]] we pick up his sword, a (or the?) [[Dreadblade]].
We can find another unusual weapon in the room, a [[Tentacle Rod]], as well as 39 gold coins.

Arinya finds another note from the adventurers that precedes us: "I do not know anymore from where I came and that gives me comfort. In knowledge lies madness".

There's also a script about a race called Mi-Go, a type of intelligent fungus that originated (and hopefully lives) on another planet.
This was all written in some strange language that Arinya could only understand by performing her magic ritual that lets her understand all languages.

I cut off the head of the [[Dread Knight]] to take with us, it might fetch a fair price in the right market. This wouldn't usually interest me, but Arinya seems addicted to coin, and at least this is a legal way to earn some.

When Tiziano looks through one of the other doorway, where he sees a cook preparing a meal.
The cook throws spices at him, seemingly in an attempt to convince him that he would be a good meal.

He's quite friendly for a strange cook in such a hostile environment, and asks us if we would mind giving him the corpses of the men we just killed, so he can add them to his dish. His name is [[Zorth]].

He's seen Camedyr Baughan around, and calls him "the guy with the poetry".
There are a few more individuals in the kitchen, of the same type as the ones we fought the day before. They seem to be preparing themselves to be cooked.

The cook gives Arinya a note which was apparently written by the cleric, in which he says he's no longer a priest of Sarenrae, but a priest of [[The Gatekeeper]].

In another room stands a desk with a number of papers. Some are on the topic of a race named [[Illithid]], creatures connected to a sort of "elder brain". It looks like the creature Arinya saw through Fiona's eyes was a member of this race.

Another page has some information on how the transition to a lich works.

Beyond another door in the mansion, a loud sorrowful moaning emanates.
The room beyond the door is in severe disrepair. An extremely long limbed humanoid creature appears from behind the corner, and emits the most pitiful moan I ever heard.
The creature walk towards me, not attacking but moving for an embrace.

As it holds me, the sheer sorrow that flows from it devastates and frightens me.
The creature does attack in a way, but we're able to defeat it. My friends… I've never used that word to describe them before... My friends know that this type of creature is a [[Sorrowsworn]].
In its room we find 250 gold coins and a beautiful little box with gilded edges. It contains a small bundle of feathers on a wooden plate shaped like an oak leaf. Later identified as a [[Feather Token]].

We take some time to rest here, we've done quite a bit of fighting already today. Arinya places her tiny hut so we have a safe place to rest, and inside she and I each attune to the new weapons we found. Arinya to her Tentacle Rod and I to the Dreadblade.

When we're ready, we carry on to the final door in the building. A chamber with a large coffer a nothing else lies before us. Tiziano tries to open the trunk, but immediately notices that he fell into the trap set by a [[Mimic]].

After defeating it, we continue to the next room where we are faced with a man and a horrific looking creature which turns out to be [[The Child]] everyone keeps rambling about.
Tiziano recognises the man as [[Camedyr Baughan]].
The fight was extremely strange though, as soon as you look away for an instant, you forget the creature is there. This made combat unsettling and difficult.

It was not made easier by the fact that we actually didn't want to kill Camedyr.
We prevailed, but did not manage to keep Camedyr alive.

The room contains a scroll and 2 glass vials. One contains a potion of longevity, the other an elixir of health.

Arinya identifies the scroll as being one of Summon Greater Demon.

There are also three red soul gems, which are likely shards from Nivemera's soul gem.

We go back to the portal to return to our own world, where the tentacle faced creature awaits us.
He introduces himself as [[Vuoldussk]], his race is the [[Illithid]].

We have a conversation about where the portal leads, and where this is.
He tells us that we're in the [[Far Realms]], the farthest we could possibly be from home.

According to his tale, their species recently had to flee here from their home on the Prime Material Plane because of a war with the creatures we saw him transport yesterday. He says he has no idea why the two species are at war, but this is very clearly a lie. Evidently, they started something that backfired.

The other species is called [[Gith]].

We say our (untrusting) goodbyes, after which we wait for the portal to open in Arinya's tiny hut.
When the portal opens, we go through.
