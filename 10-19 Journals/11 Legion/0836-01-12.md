[Location:: between Amaroth and Velgend]
[Mission:: [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]]]

There's a knock at the door of the [[Dancing Hut]] at night, which is odd since we're traveling.
There's a man from the [[Judicators]], and asks us nicely to return the [[Bell of Saint Solar Macharius]] to Ashein.

We politely decline, and let him warm up a bit in our hut before he takes off again.
I wish him the best of luck, and we part amicably.

During the day there aren't any events, we make good headway with the Dancing Hut.
