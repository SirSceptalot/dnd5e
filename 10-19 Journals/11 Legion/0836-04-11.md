# Encounter with Vestalius
In the green glow of dawn, the abbey lay shrouded in mystery. As we approached, the sound of banging and shouting filled the air. A misshapen man, [[Vestalius]], stood at the gates with a group of warriors, demanding to be let in. He claimed to have encountered "The Dark," an experience that had broadened his horizons but also left him deformed.

The paladins within the [[Sacrosanct Monastery]] were convinced that Vestalius meant them harm and were preparing to fight him and his warriors. [[Arinya]] used her magic to communicate with the paladins and also informed me of the situation through her Message spell. I kept Vestalius occupied for a few more minutes until the paladins burst through the doors and the battle began.

Despite putting up a valiant fight, Vestalius and his men were no match for me, Arinya, Tiziano, and the paladins.

# The monastery
We were then led into the devastated monastery by [[Tirell Dawnbringer]]. A large hole gaped in the ground, and Tirell told us that the abbey had been attacked by Hadak's forces. When we asked about Aurocondor, Tirell said they had few allies there, but offered to put us in touch with a contact in the Eternal City who might be able to help us.

## Trial of shadows
Our true purpose in coming to the abbey was to retrieve the [[Guiding Stone]], which lay at the bottom of the hole filled with magical darkness. We were given a magical lantern to help us navigate through the shadow demons that lurked within. Upon finding a glowing crystal at the bottom of the pit, the three of us each touched it in turn and were teleported back to the surface. To our surprise, we found ourselves each holding a piece of the crystal, which when joined together, transformed into the Guiding Stone we had been seeking.

As we sit with [[Tirell Dawnbringer]], he warns us about the dangers of the Eternal City, also known as the First City or the Cradle of Civilization. He advises us not to venture too deeply into the city, as it becomes increasingly dangerous the further in we go. On the outskirts, we may encounter small groups of people, but Tirell cautions us not to trust them. These individuals are twisted by history and possess unnaturally long lifespans, a sign of their cursed or blessed bloodlines. He does, however, have a contact in one of these groups, an Aasimar named Enes Mournshield with pitch black hair who always carries the emblem of the Sacrosanct Abbey. He advises us to be vigilant in the Eternal Forest as well, as it is full of hostile creatures.

[[Arinya]] asks about Tsukahara Bokuden, and Tirell tells us that he is a self-proclaimed emperor of a new city called the Thrice-blessed Empire in Aurocondor. In reality, he is a successful warlord of the area. Tirell gives us a silver paste that will help protect us against vampires and their kin, and advises us to rub it on our weapons. With that, we say our goodbyes and return to the boat.

Captain [[Filaurel]] greets us upon our return and asks if we were successful. We tell her that we retrieved the Guiding Stone and present it to her. She has a sailor attach it to the front of the ship, and we set sail for Aurocondor, fortified by the magical barrier the Guiding Stone will provide.

As the ship sails, Arinya decides to perform a ritual to seek answers from the gods. I use my powers to strengthen her resolve and bless her mind, and the ritual is a success. She is able to ask five questions and receives the following answers: Verras and Hadak do not work together, we cannot be certain that Salvatore will not betray us, Hadak does not currently have Anaire Tyualzara, Hadak does not have Tsukahara Bokuden, and Hadak does not have Morrigan.

After this, we each spend some time on our own projects - I write a scroll of the "Protection from Evil and Good" spell, Arinya creates a healing potion, and Tiziano works on his "Belt of Fire Giant Strength." With that, we call it a night.
