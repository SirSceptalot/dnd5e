# Expedition Log: Final Day of Travel (Day 8.5)

Our final half-day of air travel brought both expected challenges and unforeseen encounters. The culmination of our journey was marked by adversity, but also by resourcefulness and camaraderie.

---

## Morning Observations

As dawn broke, the distant silhouette of [[Mors Magicae]] came into view. The land surrounding it was shrouded in ominous storms, with vivid purple sand and soil beneath. The air itself seemed thick with unstable magic—though decidedly not the enchanting kind.

---

## Unfinished Business Remembered

Amidst the morning’s contemplation, I recalled an unresolved matter: the lone [[Warforged]] soldier who leapt from Hadak’s ship, burdened with cargo, during an earlier encounter. Curious about his fate, I asked [[Arinya]] to investigate. She obliged, casting [[Scrying]]. Her vision revealed him chained and rowing as a slave aboard an [[Orc]] vessel. His plight, though unfortunate, affirms that his mission—whatever it was—met an ignoble end.

---

## Ongoing Efforts and Provisions

- **Belt Upgrade**  
    [[Tiziano]] and I devoted further labor to completing the [[Belt of Fire Giant Strength]]. Each moment invested in this project inches us closer to a tremendous enhancement of strength and capability.
    
- **Potion Crafting and Replenishment**  
    [[Arinya]] crafted another [[Potion of Healing]], which I used to replenish my personal reserves. With nine potions from her stock, I now hold an even 20, ensuring ample resources for the days ahead.
    

---

## Emergency Landing and Investigation

A sudden and severe storm disrupted our progress, with high winds and lightning making the ship unstable. [[Magister Gribolt]] enacted an emergency landing, sparing the vessel from greater damage but leaving us grounded. Upon inspection, Gribolt hypothesized that the lightning was attracted to the magical crystals powering the ship’s flight capabilities.

Taking initiative, we ventured to locate replacement crystals nearby.

### **Crystal Discovery and Nothic Encounter**

Our search yielded a deposit of magical crystals. However, our approach provoked an attack by a group of [[Nothic]]. Though victorious, the battle proved unexpectedly grueling, underscoring the dangers of the area.

To transport the crystals effectively, [[Tiziano]] cast [[Call Lightning]] to shatter the largest crystal into manageable pieces. We secured these fragments in the [[Portable Hole]] and returned them to [[Magister Gribolt]].

- **Crystal Usage**  
    The smaller shards may serve as upgrades for our [[Warforged]] crew, while the larger segment is sufficient for constructing a second [[Gribolt's Great Glowing Gun]].

---

## Repairs and Evening Discussions

We chose to delay our land trek until the following day, instead dedicating the remaining hours to aiding [[Magister Gribolt]] with ship repairs. Our teamwork ensures the vessel will soon be airworthy again.

At the dinner table, [[Arinya]] shared her musings on a collection of half-finished [[Dunemancy]] spells recovered long ago from a defeated adversary. Intrigued, [[Magister Gribolt]] volunteered to study and complete them, potentially unlocking their secrets. His enthusiasm was palpable, as was our shared anticipation for what these spells might achieve.

---

## Final Reflections

The day, though fraught with difficulties, exemplifies our adaptability and collective strength. We face each challenge head-on, finding solutions where others might falter. Tomorrow, we begin the next phase of our journey, fortified by our progress and prepared for what awaits.
