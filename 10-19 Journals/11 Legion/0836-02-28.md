[Location:: [[Workshop]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

We insert all crystals in the pillar, after which it starts glowing and spinning into the ground where another portal is revealed.

Through the portal and down a hallway, we find a warforged by the name of [[Ronin]]. Beyond him is [[Weiss]], who's lower extremities have been replaced by some sort of mobility platform consisting of 8 spidery legs.
She immediately strikes me as a maternal figure. Despite not having any of the reproductive capabilities any of the non-artificial creatures would deem a requirement, there is something distinctly maternal and female about her.

After introductions, some talks and preliminary negotiations, we exit the "Mansion", leave the Omnissiah inside, and remove all crystals from the pillar after exiting the portal.

We exit the facility to find [[Malek]] dead. He was likely killed by the group of gunners we found inside.

After burying him, we start the return trip.

Some distance later, we can see plumes of smoke and sounds of violence.
There are [[Abishai]] flying around. Before long, one of them spots us, and we find ourselves in a fight.

As [[Hadak]] joins them, we decide to abort and flee.
While on the run, the sending stones come in handy to request an expedited extraction. This is confirmed, and [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] is soon able to provide escape to his tower in [[Amaroth]].

During the debriefing, he tells us the conversation with Illion has gone well. He can have his tavern and an arms deal, together with a few [[Warforged]] in a harmless pact to protect them from Hadak's mind control.
He's also give 2 demilitarized Warforged to house the souls of 2 of his servants.

Sadly though, Magister Gribolt has been kidnapped by Hadak's forces, despite their precautions. They suspect he's been taken by Hadak's Erinyes [[Tisiphone]].

From the conversation it becomes clear that Hadak is likely after the Omnissiah, so we should go right back from whence we came to retrieve him.
To think we could have simply taken him with us in the first place...

We will scry on the room, teleport in, retrieve the Omnissiah and then teleport back out with a scroll of Plane Shift.
To aid us, we're given a potion of haste, one of invisibility and several types of healing potions.

In the city, [[Arinya]] buys a scroll of Protection for 60 gold coins.
She also sells her Alchemy Jug and her Wind Fan.

Shortly after midday, we return to [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] who's working on retrieving a scroll of Plane Shift.
While he's still occupied, I pay [[Weiss]] another visit to talk about my [[Leg upgrade]].
She has little trouble in working out a plausible design, and gives me finished schematics to be implemented another time.

Since we're still waiting for Ecbert Dhaleus, we go see my friend [[Balthazar Gelt|Weezard]] to ask if he could perform the upgrades.
After being let in by his shocked looking servant, we find him in his workshop in a pool of blood with his 2 arms replaced by pure golden arms.

He agrees to perform the procedure at a later date and will procure the required materials himself.

Only shortly before midnight does Ecbert Dhaleus return with a scroll, after which we're teleported to the [[Workshop]].

There's a cart loaded with machinery from inside parked there, so obviously there are intruders again.
We're met by [[Reckoning]] and a number of other [[Warforged]] inside.

During the battle I try to convert [[Reckoning]] back to our cause to no avail.

He escapes, and we make our way back to the Mansion inside to retrieve the shard of divinity.

The [[Omnissiah]] is willing to give us a reward to free him, which [[Arinya]] eagerly accepts.

We use the scroll to plane shift back to [[The Towers]] and to safety.

Back in Ecbert's tower, [[Weiss]] is also present, having been negotiating for the [[Warforged]] people to have their own island and autonomy after the war with Hadak.

New objectives in the mission:
Retrieve Magister Gribolt
Retrieve a dominated [[Warforged]]

Magister Gribolt was kidnapped from Heidelburg. Why we want him back is obvious: any magic user with his own tower is potent enough becomes a hazard in the hands of Hadak.

Taking one or multiple dominated Warforged back is mostly so they could be used as pawns, or at least gain intelligence about Hadak's operation.

We go to the armory to retrieve 5 sets of manacles, in case we get luck and manage to capture that number of warforged.

Since we have some time now to decompress, I go to the [[University in Amaroth]] to ask about [[Henry]], the man who was captured by the [[Wererat]] a while back.
He showed no symptoms of the disease and was released.

[[Arinya]] tries to scry on Magister Gribolt, and sees him tied and gagged. Due to his rocking motion, he's likely on a ship, but she can't learn more than that.

I go back to [[Paragon]]'s armory to talk about weapons, and they give me a variant of a frostbrand axe, which I was due since my promotion to Spectre.

I meet [[Illion]] there, and have a chat with him. I end up inviting him for drinks that evening.

After we all meet back up at the [[Demon and Toad]], he shows up as well and we have a good time.

We spend the night in Ecbert's tower.
