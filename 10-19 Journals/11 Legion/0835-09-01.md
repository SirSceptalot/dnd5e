[Location:: [[Ermedes]]]
The prince has already heard of our defeat the previous night, but does not want to attack the mercenary camp outright because of political reasons. What he is willing to do is have "a third party" (himself) sponsor us to take him out without it leading to him.

At the moment, we believe the best strategy would be to hire mercenaries from each of the camps in order to help us [[Destroy the Black Marauders]].
This would also generate more work for them, since it takes out one of the competing bands.

After the meeting, we endeavor to find the house of the mystery woman in the [[Copperbank]].
We buy a few potions of healing on the way there.

The house where the pseudodragons saw the mystery woman enter turns out to be another brothel which doubles as a tavern: [[Sultry Stare]] .
We try the same ploy we did in the [[Swift Finish]], letting Arinya go in as a potential customer.

She uncovers that the woman we seek was employed there, but was fired last night because she vanished in the middle of her shift, only to reappear a few hours later.

Being in the [[Copperbank]] already, we make a short trip to the home of Ennio and Luziana to hear if she recognizes the girl.
She doesn't, but two of her servants do. What's more, they know where she lives and point out her house on a map.

When we get to the house, [[Tiziano]] notices that we're being followed. We hatch out a small plan to surround our pursuer, but this quickly devolves into a chase. After a short while Tiziano, who changed into a wolf, manages to catch up and stop the figure.
When I catch up, I manage to grapple and pin the figure, clamping down on its lower jaw to stop it from casting spells.
My grasp was sadly not firm enough, and when my hand is shaken off, a spell is cast and the figure vanishes.

In an incredible feat of perception, the wolf Tiziano is able to somehow detect where our opponent teleported to, and the chase continues across the river.

After a frantic dash across half the district we lose sight when our target leaps in the river.
With some clever tracking, we're able to pick up the trail in the river and follow it back to the house where we started.

Once we've forced our way inside, Arinya spots a trap door going into the old mining tunnels, which clearly have been used very recently. Inside we run into an [[Otyugh]] which won't let us pass without a fight.

Past it, the tunnel ends and we come across [[murderer|our target]] - another [[Warforged]] as expected - tossing a last few papers into a fire. When seeing us, it immediately surrenders. It's wearing some sort of flesh suit to give it the appearance of a human female.
Arinya dashes forward and manages to rescue a few scraps from the fire while I bind the wrists of our opponent.
One of the notes seems to be from the employer ("V"), while the other is a threat to the next victim, [[Katarina Ullman]].
Arinya also makes it abundantly clear that she does not appreciate being denied the right to kill this Warforged, but she'll get over it.
Tiziano removes the flesh suit from it, which we now have to carry along.

After that, Arinya searches her belongings, which include the cloak ([[Cape of the Mountebank]]) it's been using to teleport away, a set of thieves tools, some alchemist supplies, a bundle of crossbow bolts, a set of studded leather armour and a small box with some gold.

There are also 2 vials with an unknown substance which we'll need to identify later.

While marching our prisoner to the castle, I determine that we should have enough evidence to prove this was the [[murderer]]. At the castle, prince [[Prassede Altomari]] contacts [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] by magical means on our request.

I use the time we have before his arrival to compare notes with [[Abenzio Iacovone]] in order to build an even stronger case.
Having some time left, we look at the skin suit, which turns out not to be made of real flesh.
It looks to be a very impressively made disguise, which must have set someone back a large sum of money.

We discuss who "V" might be ass well, but the only possible suspect is the Archmage of Diplomacy, [[Veras]].

When [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] arrives, we report to him. He goes to the dungeon to see the Warforged for himself, but doesn't answer my question about whether or not it's one of ours.

I nominate Ephemera for a medal of Valor for her bravery in the fight the other day where Bruto Manganiello got murdered.
After going in the city to purchase a suitable attire for Ephemera's ceremony of the following day, we go back to the castle.
