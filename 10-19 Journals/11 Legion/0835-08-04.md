[Location:: [[Heidelburg]]]
In the morrow [[Arinya]] finds an impressive looking female [[Halfling]] mercenary - [[Triska]] - and hires her to join us in the raid, while I do likewise with a female [[Dwarf|Dwarven]] mercenary: [[Trinan]].

During the day, [[Tiziano]] insist on hiring [[Randal]] the milkman as mascot for some reason, despite my protest.

In the evening we all advance towards the farm, and await the patrol of the day before.
We luck out, as the patrol arrives almost as we do.

We were able to defeat the group, although [[Trinan]] is gravely wounded. Seeing her pride get in the way of her good judgment, I order her to guard the cart/horses/Randal.

We let Randal take the valuables from the corpses as we carry on towards the farm.

After failing to lure the 2 guards out, we circle round the camp to attack from the sides and rear, preceded by a beast-shaped Tiziano.

At the camp, I try to lure one of the guards out by throwing a pebble. The sound prompts a guard to lean out the first floor window to investigate.
Seizing the opportunity, I immediately follow up with a rock to his head. It causes him to lose his grip and fall to his death.
Likely my easiest kill yet.

This in turn lures a second guard out the building, on which Tiziano (beast-shaped into a tiger) pounces. Sadly, due to poor timing the guard evades the attack and is able to call for help, starting the battle in earnest.

The bandits easily outnumber us two to one, so this fight is something of a challenge, almost killing Tiziano at a certain point.

Fortunately, we all survive, albeit none unscathed.
After the battle we fetch [[Trinan]] and the horses.

We take 90 gold, a greatsword and a pile of leather armour, scimitars and light crossbows from the corpses of the bandits, and spend the night in the farm.

I stand guard as usual.
