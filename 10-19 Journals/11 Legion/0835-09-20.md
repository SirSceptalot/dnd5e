[Location:: [[Collegento]]]
From the ruins of a building I spot translucent glowing figures moving towards the large soul gem.
As they're setting up around the stone, one of them spots me, after which they attack.
In the chaos of battle, two of them manage to escape through a portal.

After the fight we scout the vicinity in search of Camedyr Baughan - perhaps he was in a state where he was unable to come out during the day, like in one of these portals?
After being attack by a [[Banshee]], we return to the camp from the previous party where [[Arinya]] performs a ritual to set up another protective magical shelter in which we take a short rest, before finally agreeing to go through the portal ourselves.

We emerge in a nightscape of dark grey desert, but with a path lined with torches running to a large mansion. Strange note: the torches have a green flame.

We go to the mansion, knock on the door and are greeted by an emaciated male who likes to know if we are flesh.
Since I'm not, this doesn't strike me as a particularly odd question, so I answer truthfully.
I have made better decisions.

The creature yells "flesh for the child" and attacks us.
It was a good fight.

Inside is sort of rock spike tree altar with corpses hanging from it. We don't recognize any of the corpses, but do find a short [[Diary of an adventurer]].

When we check, we do indeed find the portal inactive, so we decide to remain in Arinya's magical tiny hut to regain our strength after the exhausting battle in the mansion.

During this period, I can spot humanoids in the distance, so we send out [[Fiona]] to have a look - the fact that Arinya has used her magic to enhance her link with Fiona along with Fiona's abilities really pays off.

Fiona can make out 5 humanoids, one of which floats. This specimen has purple skin, claw- or talon-like hands, and tentacles on his face. He drags the others behind him on chains.

The 4 others have short stubby noses, yellowish skin with browns stripes. They look emaciated and have an elongated skull, unusually long arms and wear no armour or other gear.

The floating character spots Fiona and telepathically asks "Who are you", after which Fiona manages to quickly escape.
