[Location:: [[Collegento]]]
We bid the family and rest of the settlement farewell, and make our way back to [[Collegento]].
My Aquan lessons are working out nicely today.

Back in Collegento, we start running down the list of posted jobs we were going to look into, the first one being an escort mission.
There were no contact details on the note, but after asking around a bit we found that there was a daughter of a rich man that needed to be transported to her family.
Upon meeting these people however, Arinya could not bear the young girls attitude, so we went to find other employment.

The next mission was the merchant, [[Caleb Arianwen]], that needed an armed escort to the [[StormMaw Tribe]] so he could negotiate a trade deal for the liquor they distribute. No danger was expected at the endpoint, but since the journey goes through a lot of Giant territory, there is likely to be conflict.

Notes on this mission can be found under the following mission log: [[Expedition to StormMaw Tribe]]

[[Caleb Arianwen]] would like to depart the day after tomorrow.

We end the night in the [[Jester and Mug]].
