# Journal

[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: ]

Today is Tiziano's turn to get a constitution enhancing tattoo.

In the meantime, [[Arinya]] and I head over to [[Yaamsus' Quil]] to find a scroll of Mending.

The shop is owned by a small red [[Dragonborn]] who welcomes us heartily.

He doesn't have one, but can make or purchase one.
What he does have, however, is a scroll of Mass Healing Word.

More importantly though, he has a [[Manual of Bodily Health]]. This is an amazing find, and we can't believe our luck. Despite the price of 10.000 gold, we don't hesitate to purchase this tome.

Arinya still wants a few blank scrolls, which he throws in for free.

After this we return to the Tattoo shop, where I start my next important mission: [[Read Manual of Bodily Health]].

I manage to get 8 hours in today.
During these hours, Verras sends us a message to inform that she heard about the Dancing Hut, and says she'll take care of the particulars.

[[Arinya]] didn't stick around, and sold her Ring of Fire Resistance for 400 platinum, which gets added to our wallet.

She also orders 2 potions of flying, which should be ready inside of the next 2 days.

During the evening we go back to [[Verras]], who confirms there will be no costs relating to the Dancing Hut.
She also gives us our wage of 50 platinum pieces each. Tiziano and Arinya hang on to their wages, I just add mine to the collection.

She's also still looking into the function of the small flying ship we found in Tùr Draoidheachd, Magister Gribolt's tower in Heidelburg.

They've spent the last few days debriefing Render, and he's given some solid information on Hadak.

Hadak wants five shards of divinity there, remnants of "the calamity" in Aurocondor, one from each of the following gods:

- Morrigan, handmaiden to the Raven Queen
- Lady of the Vines, First Branch Witch of Melora
-  The Whisperer. Location of this artifact is known to be the Bibliotheca Aeterna
- Anaire Tyualzara, the queen of scales
- Tsukahara Bokuden, self-proclaimed divine emperor, located in the thrice-blessed empire.

Hadak is now rumored to be on his way to the Sacrosanct Monestary to gain access to Aurocondor.
She suggest we get on the boat and try to beat him there.

Arinya is somewhat reluctant because she ordered 2 potions of Fly which will only be ready in 2 days, but Verras surprises us by giving us 2 of her own stock and saying she'll pick up the ordered ones when they're done to keep for herself then.
We also get a receipt for a 20% discount for our next order at the forum, to be used before our departure.

We make our way there at once, and trade in her Rod of the Pactkeeper +1 for an improved +3 version.

We also purchase a few greater and superior healing potions, along with one supreme.

Teleportation to the harbour is scheduled for tomorrow morning.
We spend our evening and night at the [[Demon and Toad]]
