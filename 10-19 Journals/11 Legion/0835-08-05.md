[Location::[[Heidelburg]]]
During the morning hours, we discuss how we will proceed with the profit earned from this venture.
After a very short discussion, we agree that our cooperation so far has been quite successful, and worth continuing.

To this end, we decide to pool our resources so we can better invest in methods to makes us less vulnerable as a group.

While riding our wagon back to the city, we pay our due to the two mercenary ladies, with a sizable fee.
[[Triska]], the halfling, lasted the longest of the two in the fight and also consumed one of her healing potions, so she's paid 65 gold on top of the 20 she was owed.
She told us she'll be in town a few more days before moving on to [[Amaroth]] and later one of the free cities.

[[Trinan]], the dwarf woman that was heavily wounded during the fight with the patrol, gets 35 gold plus the 19 that was agreed upon up front.

When reentering [[Heidelburg]], the guards were not too keen on us taking the corpses straight to the office for administration, but directed us to the graveyard instead, where we were given a signed receipt.

Upon displaying this, [[Emil]] gives us the agreed upon 750 gold coins, but reminds us that we must still fulfill another job in the city.

During some haggling and shopping in both [[Forging Ahead]] and [[Trigeraian's Used Armours]], we manage to sell my chain mail and all the crap we took off the corpses of the fallen, buy a splint armour for me and come out with a descent profit.

Looking for a second job to perform as agreed with [[Emil]], we spend our afternoon leisurely going back to [[The Singed Hare]] to ask about † Horace Ringmann, who had a notice up on the board looking for adventurers.

[[Eliorod]] tells us that † Horace Ringmann was indeed there the night before, and that it doesn't look like he found someone. We're given directions to his house.

First though, we got to "the tower" to see what the job offering there is.

The mage that lives there either has quite a bit of time on his hands, or really makes an effort to appear powerful and versatile.

The tower is hidden from view until one comes within a certain radius. The door is "manned" by an animated knocker in the shape of a fish head, which announces the presence and names of any visitors to the mage.

When the door opens, it turns out to be a portal instead of a hole in the wall as is custom for doors.
The other side of the portal ends in a lovely green field with a fancy table and chairs, where a relatively young mage sits.

He introduces himself as [[Magister Gribolt]], and in need of components to make his tower levitate.
To be exact, he'd need to get a hold of the eyes of a Beholder, or at least the lesser Beholder - a Spectator.

He's willing to pay 500 gold for its main eye, and an extra 100 gold for each of its 4 secondary eye stalks.

He has tracked one of them into an elven crypt, which we may be able to access of by teleportation which he would provide. The Spectator is guarding the crypt, or something therein.

We agree to inform him of our decision regarding this task tomorrow, and head for [[Horace Ringmann's mansion]], which turns out to be an impressive and lavishly designed and decorated building.
The property is surrounded by walls, a fountain stands central in the courtyard, which in turn is flanked by a beautiful garden.

No response greets us here, and after several attempts of attracting the attention of any potential residents, we head back to the centre of administration to inform [[Emil]] of this development.

He agrees to send a patrol with us tomorrow to search the premises and see if something has befallen the merchant.

The night is spent in [[The Singed Hare]] where I wait for Mr. Ringmann to return, without luck.
