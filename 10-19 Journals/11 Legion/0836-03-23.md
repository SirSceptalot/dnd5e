[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

Long before we've been able to complete our rest there's a rumble in the distance.
The others are hesitant to see, but I worry it might be Hadak attacking the dragon, and don't want to miss a tactical opportunity.

We go observe, and see an enormous amount of dust clouding the entrance to the dragon's lair.

Entering carefully, we find no trace of Hadak or his allies, and start to wonder if it might have been a cave collapse, irrespective of the past events.

We're soon attacked by a [[Hell Hound]] or five, summoned by what seems to be some type of dwarf.

After the fight, we head back towards the dragon's lair, and are hindered again by the same summoner. We had a better look at him this time, and it turns out he's not a dwarf but an [[Azer]].
He summons 6 [[Water Elemental|Water Elementals]] this time, but [[Tiziano]] and I manage to convince them not to attack.

Shortly afterwards, he sends a number of [[Salamander|Salamanders]] after us, and faces us himself as well with a ballista. He soon saw the error in his ways though, as he took his last breath.

We take a short rest after this, and head further towards the dragon's lair once more.

We take, reload and prepare the ballista, and fight the dragon. I manage to drive my sword into his skull just as he prepares to launch a stream of flame at [[Arinya]] and [[Tiziano]], ending his life.

WE WON.

After taking a minute to recuperate, we decide to bite our teeth (figuratively in my case) and search the place first, so we can leave this place behind before anyone else comes here.
The second scroll of Decompose is set to work on the body of the dragon, the first one having already been used to decompose the father's body.

I start smashing both skeletons into shreds, to ensure Hadak's mission of using the skeleton to craft another warrior fails.
Right after doing this it comes to mind that parts of a dragon's skeleton might be sold for a nice price, so I try to fish out what remaining intact parts I can.
I did my work properly, because all that's not in pieces so far is about five teeth, two finger bones and one horn.

We collect the gold and platinum, and manage to collect roughly 2700 platinum pieces and 4000 gold pieces.

After that grueling slog, we finally go outside to attempt to get some descent sleep - you'll recall our sleep had been interrupted last night.

A good eight hours later when [[Tiziano]] wakes up, he hears the distant sounds of battle drums, although he can't quite pinpoint the direction.

I use the sending stones to inform Verras of our victory and to request extraction.
Tiziano saw a nearby settlement nearby while flying, so I let Verras know we'll be waiting there.

As we approach by Air Tizi once more, it all looks relatively young. There are some children playing outside, all looks calm.
The only downside is the gigantic mural of the dragon we just killed being worshiped.

We land nearby and obviously draw immediate attention.
We strike up a conversation with an old man who says we're the second strange group today to arrive here, while they usually don't see any outsiders.

They looked like me, so probably a contingent of Warforged sent by Hadak.

As we're talking to this man (Chule is his name) a mage sent by Verras teleports in, saying he's ready to take us back.
We have other plans however, and convince him to come with us as we search for the Warforged party that has precedes us (Chule let us know they were headed towards the dragon's lair).

Since we'll be flying again with Air Tizi and we need to take the mage with us, I go into the portable hole.

When I'm let loose from the portable hole, we're in the midst of combat with a party of [[Orc|Orcs]] (where did they come from?).
In a flash, I see Render's corpse laying between them, torn in half.

No time to reminisce about this though as the [[Orc]] warriors keep us plenty occupied.

After a mighty battle, I'm still able to revive [[Render]] with magic.
He's no longer under the influence of Hadak, but still detests Skogsland and Paragon for some reason.
Since he's still hostile, he does accepts his status as prisoner.

He's also angry with Hadak for forcing him though, so I figure we might still be able to count on him for now.

Presently, the mage from Amaroth teleports us back there, and we deliver [[Render]] into custody.

We go have a conversation with [[Weiss]] about this, and I suggest to have Render serve us until Hadak is defeated, in return for a place in our new land.

We also have a conversation about the fact that flight would be nice to have, and about the possibility of modifying my frame to accommodate it.
Weiss says she'll look into it.

She can report that they're getting close to completing a mental firewall for Warforged by installing the part of the soul that is supposedly missing in all of us.
They're currently pulling Warforged away from all front lines to ensure their safety until this has been implemented.

She says Illion's contribution is now mostly a backup, as the presence of Weiss and the Omnissiah make him mostly redundant.
He's putting his bar plans on hold for the time being, using his location he's been given mostly as an arms trade.

When we return to the [[Dancing Hut]], there's a large black feather in the kettle that drives the hut.

After questioning the people in the neighborhood, an old lady across the street says she did see suspicious people loitering, and a beautiful women dressed in black robes entering our hut.

It can only be Tisiphone.
