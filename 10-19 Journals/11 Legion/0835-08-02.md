[Location:: [[Iglberg]]]
Despite the fact that she gave everyone a bad feeling and we had accomplished our reconnaissance mission from the day before, both my companions wish to visit the [[Lady of the Woods]] AGAIN because they had a dream about her.

Moreover, once we get there, [[Arinya]] fights [[Legion|me]] tooth and nail to take the amulet the "lady" wants transported to the location of the [[Mortar and Pestle]] set that Arinya seeks.


As the "lady" (I should really find something better to call her) gives the amulet, Tiziano - since things weren't shady enough yet - decides to simply throw it into the lake.

The hag goes back inside with a sigh, as Arinya fishes it out of the water and Tiziano feigns innocence.

After knocking on the door again, the foul creature opens again and the encounter continues.
She informs Arinya that the [[Mortar and Pestle]] set are at the source of one of five rivers.
All five rivers have a power of their own, we are to find the one that heals.

We will find the flow of the river going through the mortar and pestle (somehow?).

For a change, we make it back to the village without having to fight our way back. The strange amulet does nothing - for now.

Back in [[Iglberg|town]], we find ourselves with some time to spare which I use to teach a number of the more enthusiastic youths the basis of self-defence with a weapon. This should help them defend better against the gnolls the coming winter.

The rest of the day passes without notable events.
