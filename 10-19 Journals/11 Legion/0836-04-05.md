[Location:: aboard the [[Dauntless Explorer]]]
[Mission:: ]

[[Tiziano]] says that today is the birthday of his brother, Donatello.

We also decide to have a look at the deck of cards today.
[[Arinya]] can identify it as a [[Deck of Many Things]], which she says has positive or negative, slight or severe effects on the persons drawing cards from it.

We decide to chance it, although [[Tiziano]] draws more cards than [[Arinya]] or I do.

In summary, the lasting effects are:
[[Arinya]] knows 2 new cantrips: Light and Save the Dead, has lost some bodily strength, and has a new familiar, [[Edgar]], a raven.
She also gets answer to one question.
Her question: "Can we trust Verras?"
Answer: No.

[[Tiziano]] has his staff boosted with 3 extra charges, and a sum of money.
Furthermore, he speaks jibberish for the time being, and will have luck on his side for a few days.

I grow a pair of cat ears, and have a small one-time use extra healing pool (8 hitpoints).
I also feel slightly smarter (+1 INT).

The rest of the day is uneventful.
