First task of the day is to have [[Cleve Pabst]] scry on Jericho.

Cleve tells us he can see [[Jericho]] traveling alone, ascending along a mountainside.
He's wounded and there's blood on his weapon. There's frostbite on various spots on his body.

After this we go back to the inner city to look at what's left, which is virtually nothing.
While we're there, a [[Void Dragon]] come flying from across the sea, shouting for Behektur.

He sees us and demands food and drinks, which we fetch in order to avoid a fight. The beasts name is [[Elterion]].
After a bit he's off to try and find Jericho, who he says is his.

One task that still needs to be accomplished is to deal with the Kobolts in the mines.
After some searching and talking to people, we gather up a crew of miners that will return to the mines to work and possibly advise us in dealing with the kobolts. They claim the only way to deal with them is to kill them, but I'd prefer to find a more peaceful solution first.

After going back to Cleve Pabst to ask him to scry on Behektur tomorrow, we start our journey to the mines.
