[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Investigate the disappearances in Iglberg]]]

We arrive back at the gates of [[Amaroth]] roughly midday, where we leave the [[Dancing Hut]] at the gates. We go in the city with our horses and cart which we plan to sell here, and head to administrator [[Dreali]], who pays us the promised 1000 gold coins for completing the mission.

There's one final mission that was on the notice board which we inquire about here.
There's a captain assembling a crew for the expedition in Jarnhov, if we're interested we should go there.
New mission: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]

Next stop right now though, is [[Paragon]] HQ for a debriefing.
[[Render]] is at the desk to debrief me.

We also talk about the Titan project, which seems to be going well.
The first Titan is under construction in the new [[Titan production hall]] situated in [[Silverton]].

Render takes me there for a tour, passing by the tower district and into the new production hall.
These are some of the largest manufacturing processes I've ever seen.

From the parts that have already been finished, it's clear that the [[Warforged Titan]] won't just be somewhat large, but huge.

One of the frames is nearing completion. The arms are under construction as well, but there seem to be spiked balls instead of hands attached.

After the tour of the place is finished, I meet back up with [[Arinya]] and [[Tiziano]] in the tavern.
Tiziano says he's still in need of some components for my Belt of Hill Giant Strength, so we go to the office of the Archmage of Magic, Rebis Rumerach.

His office is in his tower - obviously in the tower district. It's a very different style of tower to that of Ecbert Dhaleus, this one is very open.

We're greeted by a [[Tabaxi]] (Catfolk) that can help [[Tiziano]] with his search for magical components.
He needs 6 Earth Elemental cores, which they sell for 100 gold each.

Another 2000 gold goes to components for the Belt of Hill Giant Strength that he's crafting for me.
Finally, some coin goes to Cranium Rat brains for his headband of intelligence.

We sleep outside the city in our [[Dancing Hut]].
