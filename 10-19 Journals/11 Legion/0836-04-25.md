#### **Day Two of Seven**

The expedition to recover the **Holy Avenger** progresses with diligence. My time was spent assisting **[[Tiziano]]** in crafting the **[[Belt of Fire Giant Strength]]**. Though the belt remains unfinished, the effort is a testament to the meticulous labor required for such an artifact.

Both **[[Tiziano]]** and **[[Arinya]]** employed scrying to locate the **Holy Avenger**. Their success revealed the weapon embedded within the torso of a formidable **infernal centaur-like creature**. This entity wielded a spear and resided in a terrain best described as arid, rocky, and ominously purple. Such a setting evokes an air of grim finality—apt for a devil’s lair.

#### **Revealing the Nature of the Enemy**

Utilizing her **[[Shard of Divinity - Knowledge]]**, **[[Arinya]]** identified the creature as an **[[Agaros]]**, an elite enforcer among the devils of the Hells. These entities serve as the ultimate instruments of arch-devils, dispatched only in the direst of pursuits. Her analysis revealed several critical details about its capabilities:

- **Weakest Save**: Dexterity, yet still formidable at **+9**.
- **Resistances**: Immune to non-magical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage, alongside significant resistance to cold.
- **Weaknesses**: None discernible.

Such revelations cast the retrieval of the **Holy Avenger** as a task fraught with peril, one that demands both precision and overwhelming force.

**[[Arinya]]** attempted to deepen her knowledge via **[[Contact Other Plane]]**, though the spell failed, an unfortunate setback that reminds us of the tenuous nature of such divinations.

#### **Seeking Aid**

Deeming it prudent to prepare for confrontation with the **Agaros**, I used my **[[Sending Stones#Verras]]** to communicate with **[[Verras]]**, requesting strategic insight or resources to incapacitate the fiend.

> **Message to [[Verras]]**  
> "Considering retrieving Holy Avenger from body of Agaros in **[[Mors Magicae]]**. Would appreciate if you could find a solution to keep it incapacitated."

Her response, relayed through the **[[Sending]]** spell, was swift:

> **Message from [[Verras]]**  
> "Understood. How soon would you need this, and where?"

I replied with the logistical details:

> **Reply to [[Verras]]**  
> "We will arrive no sooner than five days on the **[[Bastion (Ship)|Bastion]]**. No precise destination available yet."

#### **Conclusion of the Day**

The remainder of the day passed without notable incident. As the sun set, we secured our rest, aware that each day brings us closer to a confrontation that will test the limits of our resolve and skill.
