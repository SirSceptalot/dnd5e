[Location:: [[Divine Empire]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

[[Tiziano]] readies his own scrying spell, which is empowered by [[Tsukahara Bokuden]]'s men. He discoveres that Hadak uses his airship to attack from above. He weakens the defences from a safe distance first, and then jumps from the ship to the forces below, bypassing the entire perimeter defence.

[[Arinya]] also does some scrying, and sees a Horned Devil on the ship. She also sees a number of the new Warforged that Hadak has created. They obviously outrank the regular troop, and she overhears that they will be in the strike force. She can see at least 5, but there are bound to be plenty more.

We estimate the capacity of the ship at roughly 300 men.

We relay this information to [[Tsukahara Bokuden]], but at a certain point during the discussion we manage to insult his honour and we're asked to leave.

My [[Javelin of Lightning]] is returned to me, and we vacate the city.
We set up camp nearby, hidden from sight, and spend the night in [[Arinya|Arinya's]] [[Tiny Hut]].
