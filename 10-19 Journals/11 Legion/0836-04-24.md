This morning in the [[Bibliotheca Aeterna|Black Library]], we discovered a note that had been discreetly placed beneath a door. It bore a message offering a sword in exchange for our departure. Attempting diplomacy, we responded with our own note, inquiring if the author was [[Vecna]] or if a direct conversation was possible. However, the reply repeated their desire for our absence and declined all overtures.

Undeterred, we resumed our exploration of the library, noting the peculiarity that every door led to a new room. [[Tiziano]], ever the experimentalist, hypothesized that each door functioned as a portal. Repeatedly opening and closing one confirmed this, as the destination changed with every attempt.

After a few repetitions, the clow [[Sheogorath]] appears, demanding we show him each a card before he grants us further passage.

After several iterations, the enigmatic clown [[Sheogorath]] appeared, demanding that each of us present a card to gain passage. His standards were surprisingly lax, accepting unrelated library cards from both [[Tiziano]] and [[Arinya]]. Bereft of a card, I, alongside our prisoner [[Bearer]], took refuge within the [[Portable Hole]] concealed by our companions.

In the next hallway, [[Bearer]] and I emerged from the [[Portable Hole]] and resumed our investigation. [[Arinya]] sought information on the Shard of Divinity rumored to be within the library, while [[Tiziano]] researched the library’s structure. He discovered that its layout resembled a celestial system, with a central hub connecting transient rooms via ephemeral magical portals.

Our efforts drew the attention of the [[Whisperer]] when [[Arinya]] opened a book that ominously repeated "Stop looking." Seizing the opportunity, I attempted to enlist his aid against [[Hadak]], arguing that the latter’s disregard for library returns should disqualify him from favor. The [[Whisperer]], unimpressed, countered with an exhaustive list of books Hadak had borrowed and returned punctually.


> [!Quote] The Whisperer
> Hadak is of no concern to me. He can try to reach me and, like his little minions, run circles, and so will you.

In a last ditch attempt to reach the [[Whisperer]] I try a new tactic: anger him.
Instead of writing a response on a note I write it directly on a book. A few books pelt at me, so it seems to be working. [[Tiziano]] takes it up a notch and sets a number of books on fire.
This works, as a wormhole opens next to us, sucking us in and spitting us out in the same room as the [[Whisperer]].

It's not so much a room, as it is an empty dark void where we can see stars and celestial objects in the distance. It looks as if we're in outer space, but there is air, gravity and old stone flooring.
We see stack up stacks of books everywhere

The Whisperer sits on a large throne, een lantaarn in de hand, demonisch gezicht, 4 benen, 5 armen, een zeis, gewaad over zich waar stekels al doorkomen.
He's surrounded by some sort of ghosts or spectral beings, who start attacking us on his command
He joins the fight with magic, but tries to keep us at a distance.

we kill the whisperer with some good teamwork in a heavy fight. As he dies, his body withers to dust, leaving the [[Shard of Divinity - Knowledge]] laying on the ground before us.
We shackle [[Bearer]] after he tries to snatch it and run before us.

Besides the Shard, he leaves behind [[The Whisperer's Scythe]] and a special magical rod that [[Arinya]] identifies as being called "[[Apotheosis]]". It seems to be an improved version of the [[Rod of the Pact Keeper]] that she currently carries, which will enable her to cast [[Modify Memory]], [[Arcane Gate]] and [[Hex]].

In any case, we take a short rest to heal and recover from the fight at this point.

During our rest, we see [[Sheogorath]] walk through the door. He tells us that the [[Holy Avenger]] is in [[Mors Magicae]].
After this, he opens an imaginary door like a mime player, and asks us to step through. As we do, we're transported back to the library in the [[Eternal City]] where our search began.

Back here, we decide to look up a few more facts:
- The Sword of Caz is in the "Vampire Dungeons".
- [[Anaire Tyualzara]] is a 5-headed dragon and the eldest of Tiamath's offspring. Each of her heads is a different colour. All of the chromatic dragons are Teamath's offspring. Anaire is a [[Linnorm]].

After this, [[Arinya]] scries on [[Hadak's Horned Devil]]. She sees him and a few warforged near Hadak, who is walking alongside a large white serpentine creature. This creature must be [[Anaire Tyualzara]].

As [[Anaire Tyualzara]] has or is a [[Shard of Divinity]], we are clearly too late for this one. Hadak beat us to it, and has joined forces with Anaire.

We decide we absolutely need to get to [[Morrigan]] before Hadak does, as losing that Shard to him as well would be disastrous.

We need to go talk to [[Salvatore]], so we go back to the vampires in the palace, and show them our letter of passage that [[Ludwig Grivor]] gave us. The guards lead us to Salvatore.
We ask [[Salvatore]] if he's making any headway find us an army to help fight Hadak and his army. He says the vampires are willing to field one, on the condition that we kill [[Morrigan]]. Since [[Morrigan]]'s death would give the vampires and undead completely free reign over the realm, we decide we need to find an alternative solution.

Salvatore tells us that ever since [[Lauden Brashaer]] imprisoned [[Morrigan]] in the [[Dusk meadow]], the vampires have been running rampant and things have been getting worse for everyone else. An magical barrier with a constant influx of magical power prevents anything from entering or exiting the [[Dusk meadow]].

We ask him about the [[Holy Avenger]]. He says it used to be wielded by the most famous hero of [[Pelor]], but doesn't know the hero's name, nor where exactly it is now, except that it's rumoured to be somewhere in [[Mors Magicae]].

About [[Mors Magicae]]: [[Salvatore]] has bad experiences there. One can choose to increase the power of one's magic there, at a certain cost to one's own health. Magic also has a tendency of taking on a life of it's own, so your fireball could decide to go find something more interesting to do. All in all, he calls it an "interesting" place, which is home to a number of magic eating creatures.

He says he can get us a [[Ratfolk]] guide to take us there, because he'd rather not go back himself.

After this we go find [[Enes Mornshield]]. After some trouble locating him, we find someone that can help. A woman seems to know something, but is reluctant to talk or assist. After some talking and offering of a pot of honey to literally sweeten the deal, she says she can take us to meet him.

She leads us to a place where she knocks a code on the door, after which it opens with nary a squeak. As we enter into the darkened room, holy water is splashed on us as archers take aim from the balcony above. After a tense few seconds, they stand down as they see that the holy water does not harm us.
Lanterns are lit, and a hooded figure reveals himself. He turns out to be the man we were looking for, and we lay out our situation. He supports our cause and wishes to help us, if we help him with the plague of vampires instead. He can rally the people of the city, but needs them to be able to walk freely again without constant fear of the mosters (vampires) openly terrorizing the streets.

On the [[Holy Avenger]], he can tell us that many have tried to retrieve it, and just as many have failed. It's said to be impaled in the heart of a legendary Devil who was hand crafted by [[Asmodeus]], but defeated by a hero of the god [[Pelor]], confirming what we have heard before.
The devil would not die, so the hero had no choice but to leave without his sword.
The devil is said to enjoy combat, and even fight with honour.

Once the sword is removed from the devil, he is said to return to full power again.

On [[Mors Magicae]], he can tell us that it was formed when the [[God of Magic]] was killed by the Prince of Hell, [[Asmodeus]].

[[Enes Mornshield]] reiterates what [[Salvatore]] said earlier: [[Lauden Brashaer]] found a way to capture her in the dusk meadow.
So now she's captured in the dusk meadow, and nobody can find a way into the dusk meadow, nor can she find a way out. She's confined to the graveyard.
The location of the graveyard is known, and a constant magic source supplies the power to keep the barrier active.

As we discuss, the more magically inclined of the group agree that we would be able to use the [[Find the path]] spell to find the location of the [[Holy Avenger]] sword.

We depart and make our way back to our air ship [[Bastion]], using [[Tiziano|Tizi's]] [[Transport via Plants]] ability to quickly cover a large distance.

As we get back aboard, we're greeted by [[Weiss]] who declares she's been able to manufacture three of her [[Weiss Grenade|Weiss Grenades]], and hands us one each.
[[Magister Gribolt]] hasn't been dillying around either, as he finally finished his modifications to our magic ship, which will now travel at twice it's former speed.

We ask him if the ship will function properly around [[Mors Magicae]] due to the weird magic effects there. He says it will, as long as he can first isolate a few systems.

[[Arinya]] still has the empty scroll that can be charged by simply casting a spell into it, so I cast [[Revivify]] into it. This is a great spell to have in case one of us is killed, as long as we can then activate the scroll within the first minute. [[Arinya]] agrees to hold the scroll on her person. As I'm able to cast it myself without scroll, and [[Tiziano]] has another spell to bring one of us back from the dead, we are once again a step closer to feeling ready to face Hadak.

I decide to finally cast [[Summon Steed]], so I finally have a trusty steed for faster movement in and out of battle. As I focus on the spell, a horse appears.

The horse is armored or made of intricate metallic plates. Blue glowing symbols and geometric shapes, such as hexagons and circles, are embedded in its body, suggesting magical energy.
The overall color scheme combines bronze-like metallic tones with glowing electric blue highlights.
 Its mane is flowing, white, and ethereal, almost resembling light or smoke, adding a magical quality to the otherwise mechanical body. The horse's eyes glow with a vivid blue light, reinforcing its mystical nature.
The horse stands tall and proud.

I can feel an instinctive bond with it that will allow us to fight as a seamless unit.

As it is for transport, I decide to name it [[Transport Unit]], or [[Transport Unit|Unit]] for short.

[[Tiziano]] casts [[Find the path]], and knows the way to our target.
With our airship, we embark on the first day of a 7 day journey to [[Mors Magicae]] and the [[Holy Avenger]].
