[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

We'll head to the dragon island today, in hopes of being able to destroy both the deceased dragon and the live one. We assume the live one might not agree with that however.

[[Verras]] gives us 2 scrolls of Decompose which should take care of the flesh of the dragons, and are then teleported straight into the cave - although the teleportation was a rather painful affair with a few mishaps.

As predicted, the red dragon is a vein and self-absorbed creature which revels amidst his modest collection of gold, platinum, art and pretty rocks.

He's willing to let us decompose the skeleton of his father in return for our artefacts, which we don't really agree to. He lets [[Arinya]] go outside to scry on Hadak.

While she's outside however, he attacks me. [[Tiziano]] and I are able to make our escape though, and we quickly pick up Arinya to find a safe place to recuperate.

Arinya sets up her Tiny Hut in a hopefully hidden location, and we begin to rest.

A while later however, an [[Efreeti]] that works for the dragon finds and attacks us, with a few companions. He got more than he bargained for though, and even though he tried to retreat half-way through the fight, some solid teamwork ensured his demise.

After all this commotion, we trek a bit further and at dusk set up the Tiny Hut again to commence the night.
