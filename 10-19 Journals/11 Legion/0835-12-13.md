[Location:: [[Northern Islands]]]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

Day fifteen.

[[Tiziano]] notices a pair of islands off in the distance.

Sure enough, after a while the rest of us are able to make out the shape of dual island, one of which emits some black smoke that indicates an active volcano.

The temperature here seems to be rather pleasant.

At the docks, there are a number of small humanoids working on a raft.

Before we get to shore, Tiziano gifts me the [[Belt of Hill Giant Strength]], and I thank him and start attuning to it.

As [[Gilmer Chatan]] takes the ship into a bay, we can make out that the little humanoids are [[Goblin]].
When they finally spot us approaching, they run inland.

We land with the sloop Pudding Of The Day. Gilmer Chatan suspects we'll have trouble with the goblins, as they're usually aggressive and very territorial.

Sadly, there is no information on where we can look for the artifact. [[Fiona]] takes to the skies to try and get a view of some noteworthy landmarks, but she can't make out much besides the jungle.

There is a small trail that leads inland, so with no better options we follow that.
After a while, Tiziano sees a pair of serpentine eyes looking at us. We engage the eyes in conversation, and are greeted by a [[Yuan-ti]], followed by three more.

They try to coax us to their camp, quite clearly with questionable motives.
We're able to diffuse the situation without escalation though, and we move on.

Once out of earshot, [[Arinya]] tells us that the Yuan-ti have supposedly diverged from humanity after they started worshiping some serpentine deity, after which the group left, rarely to be seen again.

Further along the path the woods start to clear, and signs of civilization start to appear, such as farms and huts.
Suddenly, [[Tiziano]] falls into a pit trap, getting injured slightly.
From then on, we pay more attention to traps, and find several other. We either avoid or dismantle them, and eventually arrive at a palisade.

A [[Goblin]] city is behind the palisade and it's gates, and after a relatively short discussion with the guards they agree to fetch their boss, [[Wreelk]].

From the looks of it, these Goblins are trying their best to turn into a descent civilisation instead of a feral tribe.
The guards escort us to a small tavern (although everything here is small).

We're told the tribe is called the [[Severed Face Tribe]], a name dictated by their past in which they scalped the faces of their fallen enemies and used them as decoration. A number of severed faces still line the tavern walls.

We get to talking and they tell us about their deities, [[Gork]] and [[Mork]]. We visit their temple (which, in all honesty, is little more than a hastily built shack) and a few other landmarks of their small civilization.

The priest of their temple told us that there are supposedly also humans on the adjacent island, which have no civilization and are mostly feral.

We talk about their relationship with the [[Yuan-ti]], which has been a long and bloody war. The Yuan-ti hunt them for food, and the tribe has diminished notable in strength since they came under siege.

The enemy is stronger, but the Goblins have far greater numbers.

Most of the Yuan-ti are on the northern side of the island.

They know something about the artifact we're looking for. Sadly, they don't know the location, but the priest can tell us that it's the Bowl of Winds.
