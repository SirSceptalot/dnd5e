Day 6/6

We arrive in [[Amaroth]] shortly after midday and debrief with [[Ecbert Dhaleus]], who promotes me to Spectre - Special Tactics and Recon.

This gives me an extra magical ability: once per day Commander's strike. An ally of my choosing gets an immediate extra attack after his own attack.

Besides this promotion, I can take ownership of my Adamantine +1 Full Plate Armor.

End of mission: [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]].

There is a new mission for us, again in the city of Collegento.
It seems the Temple of Asmodeus in Collegento has taken over the city.
Operatives from Ashein are already gathering forces, so not sending forces of our own would be political suicide.
Resources committed to this operation: Three spectres (myself with my 2 companions), Render, Reckoning, Bastion and a small detachment of Warforged soldiers.
Also joining us is the "female" warforged we captured a while back, who it seems was working for Verras.

New mission: [[Liberate Collegento]]

After being briefed on this situation, we acquire nourishment and bedding in the [[Demon and Toad]].
