[Location:: [[Iglberg]]]
[Mission:: [[Investigate the disappearances in Iglberg]]]

Final day of the journey to Investigate the disappearances in Iglberg.
We arrive late morning in the recognizable settlement, and go to [[Redcap Boots]] to greet the innkeeper Torben.

He's not present, but his wife [[Galiéna]] is. She informs us that Torben has been missing for 2 weeks.
In the middle of the night, he got up and proclaimed he needed to go do something, and hasn't been seen since.
They've tried tracking him off course, but the trail lead to the edge of the forest which is currently hounded (you'll get that in a second) by some type of dog-like creatures. (See? Told you you'd get it.)

She describes them as very large hounds that appear to walk but are actually floating. Besides that, they also seem to avoid sunlight. The sound ghostly, but are not translucent and definitely physically present.

These are most likely Yeth Hounds.

The people that disappeared are the innkeeper Torben, a hedge knight named Irfon Gough, Robert - a farmer and 5 children (4 female, one male) of  the charcoal manufacturers Adelhayt and Ulrich. The boy is 10, the girls 12, 9, 7 and 5 years old.

[[Arinya]] asks if we can have a look in their house to see if we can find anything, and returns with some twisted kind of dream catcher. A short examination makes here believe it's a type of remote activated scroll, which is in this case was likely used to charm the victims.
This is a known weapon of witches, so we have a good idea where to start looking: the Lady of the Woods.

We let the town wisdom [[Cerian Bedham]] know that everyone should search their homes for similar artifacts.

In order to see what we're up against, we go to the edge of the forest and do indeed see a number of [[Yeth Hound|Yeth Hounds]].
Besides them, there are also a few other creatures that we weren't expecting: 4 [[Redcap|Redcaps]].
They came, they attacked, they died.
Their belongings (blood soaked hats, iron boots, an assortment of mushrooms and a scythe) go into our portable hole, and we return to the wisdom to inform her of our findings. We also leave the equipment of the redcaps with her, to distribute to whatever villagers finds them useful.

At the end of the day we eat in [[Redcap Boots]], and sleep in [[Helmine's house]].
During the night, the [[Lady of the Woods]] comes by for some pleasantries. She tried to disguise herself as a regular old lady, but it was pretty obvious who she was.
