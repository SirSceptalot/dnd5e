[Location:: Between Esterna and the Restless Mire]
[Mission:: [[Settle the Restless Mire]]]

We arrive at the edge of the mire after dark and park the [[Dancing Hut]] nearby, planning on spending the night in the hut before exploring the mire at dawn.

[[Spyro]] and [[Fiona]] notice small beacons of light closer to the mire, when one starts flashing.

Upon closer inspection by [[Tiziano]], it looks like zombies are throwing themselves at the magical barrier in hopes of getting through in a fashion which is far too coordinated for a regular horde of zombies.

The fact that there's nothing drawing them towards the other side of the barrier is also a dead giveaway of something unusual driving them.

The most remarkable thing is how they suddenly change tactics to throwing one of their numbers over the barrier.
After a few attempts, this works. They're also able to repeat this, so it looks like we're going to have to deal with this.

After killing those outside, the rest retreat and we go back to the hut to keep watch.
