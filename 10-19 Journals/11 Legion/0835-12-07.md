[Location:: at sea]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

Day nine.

A ship is spotted in the distance, we make a small detour to get to it.
The ship is called [[The Earnest]], and is manned only by [[Jorge]], a human mage.

He's conducting magical experiments which could potentially be very dangerous - namely a teleportation circle on a moving platform, such as a boat.
It wasn't entirely his idea though, he thinks he's being punished because his last teleportation circle failed, resulting in the death of an ambassador.

He doesn't want help and wishes to continue his experiments, so we leave him to it.
