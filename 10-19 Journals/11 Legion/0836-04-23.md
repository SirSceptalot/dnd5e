**Morning Respite**

Following the arduous combat of the previous day, we elect to partake in a tranquil morning. The [[Lady of Vines]] receives additional intelligence regarding the war effort from us and, in return, extends a heartfelt farewell. A rare moment of mutual respect.

---

**Arrival in the Eternal City**

After consuming the midday meal, we utilize [[Transport via Plants]] to reach [[Salvatore]] in the [[Eternal City]]. The city reveals itself as an architectural marvel, a layered expanse of beauty, albeit tarnished by decay. Its Gothic splendor, both haunting and sublime, is diminished by neglect. Movement within the city is sparse, conspicuously quieter than what one might expect for a settlement of its magnitude.

We are escorted to the palace, a structure mirroring the city’s decline. Boarded windows and somber guards clad in heavy armor serve as a prelude to our meeting. One such guard, after removing their helmet, guides us through a labyrinth of hallways. At the throne stands [[Ludwig Grivor]], patriarch of the Grivor clan. His attire—ornate robes and fur embellishments—suggests he values appearances despite his surroundings.

---

**Discussions with Ludwig Grivor**

Ludwig introduces himself as a protector of the Eternal City, a "kind vampire" by his own assertion. However, he warns of unkind vampires and other creatures that roam the city. While self-defense is permissible, unnecessary violence is discouraged.

The conversation pivots to [[Hadak]], the pit fiend. Ludwig advises that our primary challenge will be to separate Hadak from his army. His own experience with pit fiends underscores the peril involved. Ludwig, at 1,153 years of age, offers both wisdom and wariness.

Ludwig also proposes hospitality, offering food, drinks, and even celestial and fey blood.

**Meta Notes:**
- Celestial blood restores 1d10 HP and provides *Greater Restoration.*
- Fey blood induces confusion for one hour and a euphoric state.
- Blood potion restores 1d4 HP per round for three rounds.

With a pointed glare, I dissuade Arinya from partaking in these offerings. [[Salvatore]] then leads us to the library.

---

**Library Exploration**

The library, boasting over 1,500 years of accumulated knowledge, is vast. Each member of our party undertakes their own research:

1. **Arinya** discovers information on [[Morrigan]], known as "The Death Maiden." Morrigan despises undead, often venturing from her domain in the Dusk Meadow to eliminate those who vex her.
2. **I** seek insights on [[Whisperer]]. Information is scant, identifying it as a demon-like entity tied to Vecna, the "Whispered One." A speculative manuscript questions its origins, suggesting it is not inherently demonic.
3. **Tiziano** finds records of [[Verras]], identified in an ancient elven birth registry. Remarkably, she is the last elf born on Aurocondor over 1,400 years ago.

**Collaborative Research:**
We delve into Vecna, uncovering his ascension from a necromantic wizard to a lich. Notable findings include his role in the betrayal and death of Mystra, the goddess of magic. Her demise, orchestrated by Asmodeus and others, had profound cosmic repercussions.

---

**The [[Black Library]]** 

Our inquiries lead us to chained tomes and a corridor of copper railings, where malevolent energy—manifesting as purple lightning—impedes our progress. After forcing through these obstacles, we find ourselves in the enigmatic Black Library, a room devoid of exits. Sand begins to pour from the ceiling, prompting us to locate a magical book containing a portal. We escape narrowly, emerging into a hallway.

Here, the desiccated corpse of a wizard lies with the following items:
- 25 gold pieces
- 10 onyx stones
- A jar of grave dirt
- A jar of brackish swamp water
- Faded notes describing the library’s elusive nature

The wizard’s notes lament how the library "feeds on the need for knowledge," mocking its seekers by leading them astray.

---

**Encounters and Discoveries**

While exploring further:
- We encounter a skeletal figure sweeping the floor. It pays us no heed and is left undisturbed.
- Arinya locates a scroll imbued with negative energy, usable once via spellcasting.

Subsequently, we face six [[warforged]]—Acolytes of [[Hadak]]—armored and armed for battle. Despite our attempts at diplomacy, combat ensues when Arinya prematurely attacks. A spellcaster among them dominates [[Tiziano]], forcing him to assault us. Though the situation is dire, we prevail. The last warforged surrenders.

**Interrogation Results:**
- Name: [[Bearer]]
- Rank: Vexillor (banner bearer)
- They served [[Hadak]], believing their actions would restore universal balance.

Bearer provides limited intelligence, mentioning [[Sheogorath]], a bizarre clown-like entity, who supposedly enforces the library’s rules. Tiziano and Arinya futilely search for a library card.

---

**Conclusion**

We secure various items, including maps and notes. Despite the chaotic day, we establish a temporary camp. Arinya performs a ritual to create a magical Tiny Hut, providing shelter for her and Tiziano. I remain vigilant, keeping watch over them and our prisoner.
