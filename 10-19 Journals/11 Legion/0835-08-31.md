[Location:: [[Ermedes]]]
The night was uneventful.

After breakfast, we venture to the [[Copperbank]] district in search of somewhat who could tell us more about the seal on the letter.

After a some twenty minutes walking to the Copperbank, we are able to find a seal carver after a bit of asking around.
[[Gaullir Veroulle]], a human male with a small business, readily admits the seal from the letter was his work.
It was a commission for a brown haired girl that paid four times the regular price in order to expedite the work and keep it off the records.

He describes her as late twenty, early thirties, brown hair, rather tall and a regular build. She had a strict and barking voice. He knows that she works in a tavern, but has no idea which one.
I try to create a sketch based on his description, which Gaullir finds recognizable, but not a perfect image.

She carried an image of the desired seal, which was a lotus flower which is indeed often used in Ashein, but is not particularly *of* Ashein. It's more a generic symbol of faith.

Our current plan is to go to the castle and request a descent sketch artist visit [[Gaullir Veroulle]] to create a sketch of the unknown woman, and distribute this sketch to all the guards.
Once all the guards have her image and all ways out of the city are sealed, we can place posters with a reward around the city.

At the castle we are invited for lunch as we explain our plan, where the military commander [[Iacovone Abenzio]] joins us.
He will see to the creation and dispersal of the sketch.

There is still plenty of daylight to burn, so we set off to the various taverns to see if we can find the mystery woman.
First stop: [[Dancing Beggar]], since it's closes to Gaullir Veroulle's shop. Sadly, no matches.
The [[Yellow Cup]] doesn't seem to be her stomping ground either. The owner was friendly enough to ask if he could do anything to accommodate Warforged in his tavern, which I do appreciate. I offer him a drink in my place.

On our way to [[Hydra's Tavern]] in the Mercenary Ring, [[Arinya]] is clearly in need of some privacy for whatever reason, but doesn't want to come out and say it for some reason.
I ask if [[Tiziano]] wishes to accompany me to Hydra's Tavern so Arinya can have her privacy, to which he agrees.

As it turns out, Hydra's Tavern and the [[Swift Finish]] are under the same roof, but with each a separate entrance.
We don't find the woman we're looking for there either.

When Arinya returns, we move on to the Swift Finish. Being a brothel, we can't just have a look everywhere, so Arinya poses as an interested party in search of a girl.
A number of options are paraded in front of her, none of which closely resemble the woman we seek.
She does ask about a girl she can hear but is not part of the procession in front of us, and is granted access to her room in exchange for some coins, while Tiziano and I wait in the central area.

After some time, she emerges and we go towards the city proper again.
Once outside the mercenary ring, a girl appears in our midst as by magic - which turned out to be a good guess on my part, as Arinya had cast Invisibility on her.

She is introduced by Arinya as [[Ephemera]], a 15 year old girl who was apparently kept in the Swift Finish as a slave, as were a number of the other girls.
After being kidnapped during her hike from school to home, she was beaten and raped in order to prepare her as a "good slave girl". During her life in the brothel, she was kept apart from the other girls.

She used to live in Collegento, which should be about three days travel from here.

As we walk onward to the castle so I can take her statement for us to use to disband the brothel, she can confirm that she does recognize the sketch of the woman we seek, which she has seen in the camp of the Black Marauders.

Once in the castle, I request a room with writing implements and take her statement, which we then have copied for [[Abenzio Iacovone]].
In this meeting, he tells us that distribution of the sketches to the guards is going slowly but steadily. The guards are being supplied with the likeness as the come in for their shift change, so it does take a bit of time.

He wasn't aware of the situation in the brothel, but doesn't seem to be happy about it either. He'll have a word with the mercenary group that runs the brothel to let them know that no new work will be coming their way as long as this situation stands.

In the meantime, the girl can stay in the [[Temple of Erathis in Ermedes]] or the [[Temple of Bahamut in Ermedes]].

Regarding the murder of † Ennio Ginocchio, he believes the killer to be of only limited magical capability or one using magical items to accomplish these feats.

We say our goodbye to the captain and begin towards the temples, but the girl says she would rather stay with us, as she's afraid that the slavers might come looking for her in the temples - which would be the obvious place for a wandering girl to spend the night.

After stopping in the [[Yellow Cup]] to take dinner to [[Mineral Minded]], Arinya casts Invisibility on me in order to make us less obvious when going back there to spend the night.

While entering, I happen to see that someone was indeed watching us. Once inside, my companions send their dragons after the person, which started to leave as soon as we entered.
They're able to follow that person to the mercenary camps.

During the night, we suffer a brutal attack from a contingent of the [[Black Marauders]], assisted by the teleporting woman we were after.
Her magical ability allowed her to outflank us and kill [[Bruto Manganiello]]. Angered by this, I set in pursuit of the retreating Black Marauders, resulting in [[Arinya]] and myself being defeated and nearly dying while [[Tiziano]] was in unsuccessful pursuit of the woman.

They also manage to set [[Mineral Minded]] ablaze, concluding the completely devastating loss on our - mostly my - part.

The only good thing to come of this night is the fact that the 2 pseudo dragons were able to follow the woman back to the [[Copperbank]] district.

I drag the single villain we were able to slay to the castle for a meeting with [[Abenzio Iacovone]] once more, and to admit my humiliating defeat. My companions are not to blame in the least for this, the shame is completely mine.
It's also worth noting that Ephemera bravely stood her ground alongside Arinya in this fight.

Abenzio will issue a warrant for the arrest of the leader of the Black Marauders, Rassler Creighton.
This is not enough for me, and I'm determined to [[Destroy the Black Marauders]].
The rest of the night is spent recuperating in the castle.
