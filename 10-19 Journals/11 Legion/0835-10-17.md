In the morning, Arinya tells of a dream where her patron [[Illion]] was asked to pass on a message from [[Viantius]]: "The green tide shall roll in on the dawn of the second day". None of us know what this means, although I have a hypothesis that it might be an attack from the orcs. When "the second day" is, is also up for debate. The day after tomorrow? Or was yesterday the first day?
Why can't angels speak clearly?

Arinya said she also asked Illion to inform the white dragons of Jericho's approach.

When everyone is ready, we're off to [[Eugene Grey]]. His method of getting us to the docks unseen is in a weighted upside down boat, which we use to walk across the sea floor.
Once close to the docks, we let the boat rest on the floor and go to the surface. 

Just as we're looking to exit the water, we see a giant message written in fire warning of Jericho's approach.
In part with help from this insane distraction, we're able to get ashore unseen.

This section seems pretty abandoned, it seems the Exaltators are mostly busy reinforcing the front.

When we arrive at [[Wulfa and Heodamerc's Emporium]], which is a single story building made to fit right into the street and environment. It's nicely decorated with green glowing orbs.

The door is open, but inside we see the body of an Exaltator, completely covered in blood and small lacerations.
Looking at the body, there are numerous small slivers of glass embedded in the wounds, so we deduce that there was some type of magical trap that went off - most likely mirror related.

Arinya tries to cover the mirror with a cloth by mage hand, but as she does so the cloth is drawn into the mirror, at which point the mirror attacks us.

The mirror swallows us all up, and we all find ourselves behind the mirror.
At this point, we're in a hallway that consists purely of mirrors, at the end of which is a room with three stone walls and one which is covered by one large mirror.

Another door can be seen in the reflection, but in reality it  isn't there.
The door from whence we came is no longer there either, so we now have nowhere to go.

A furtive glance around the room reveals that a number of items are out of place when comparing the reflection to reality.
After matching these up, the door in the reflection opens up, and some sort of witch or hag appears. We can see this woman in the reflection, but again not in real life.

Arinya greets her, but is attacked for her courtesy. She's not actually standing by Arinya, but Arinya bleeds just as if she was there. Tiziano has the insight to run at her reflection, and manages to run right into the reflective world to attack the hag.

After seeing this trick, we all go through and are able to fight back.

Two more magic mirrors, somewhat similar to the one that got us here, appear in the fight and slam us with their shattered surfaces, but we're able to defeat our foes once  more.

After the fight, we can identify the creature as a [[Mirror Hag]]. The door opens when the hag falls, and we're able to exit the room, reappearing the real world through the mirror that originally swallowed us.

After this ordeal, we take a number of greatly useful items from the shop, such as a number of potions and scrolls, a [[Sentinel Shield]], [[Boots of Elven kind]] and a [[Portable Hole]].

We go back to the boat after this. At the water, Tiziano turns into a giant squid and pulls one guard under water, drowning him and netting us another weapon, shield and set of armour for the Avelund Resistance.

We go back to shore and meet up with [[Eugene Grey]] again, where we're given the bad news that the [[Exaltators]] retaliated by executing a quick raid on the tavern, killing [[Lala]].

Spyro and Fiona go out to see if they can find the Orcs approaching, but they can't.

The "plan" we hash out right now is to wait for "the green tide" tomorrow, and barricade the gates so the Exaltators can't run from the city, IF they were to do so, IF they started losing the fight.

We do some preparations and take 2 alchemist fires, 3 molotov cocktails and 6 flasks of oil.
