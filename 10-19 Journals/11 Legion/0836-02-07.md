[Location:: [[Collegento]]]
[Mission:: [[Liberate Collegento]]]

Travel to Collegento, day 5/5

We make [[Collegento]] in the afternoon. The outside of the city looks intact and normal, save for the military encampment with [[Bastion]] towering above it.

We meat up with [[Reckoning]] and [[Render]].
We can make out people from the [[Band of the Hawk]] as well.
[[Gunther]] from the [[Judicators]] (whom I had my duel with earlier) is present as well.
Another member of the [[Judicators]], [[Cynric]] accompanies him.

From the [[Band of the Hawk]] we recognize the Hawk himself, [[Griffith]].

The parties already present propose 2 options:
1: straight through the north wall and launch a full assault on the Temple of Asmodeus in Collegento
2: Go in through Nivemer's Stand and try a surprise assault through there.

I propose a combination of the 2: One party to distract the forces and begin assaulting the wall while a second party extracts civilians through Nivemer's Stand, and join up both forces to assault the Temple of Asmodeus in Collegento once the civilians are safe.
Secondary goal of the extraction party is to eliminate targets of opportunity as they present themselves.

[[Gunther]] requests a private meeting with us in his tent, where he reveals that he has been given the order by the organization of the church of Asmodeus to completely eliminate every member of this temple to set an example and ensure the church itself doesn't get a bad name.
He doesn't feel good about this assignment, and we agree that if there are any prisoners afterwards we will claim the prisoners in the name of Skogsland so he has a legitimate reason to avoid killing them.

Later we hear from [[Sentinel]] that inside the city a portal is being constructed -possibly to hell but there's no real way of knowing.

We have time left before the assault begins so we scry on [[Varius]].

He's in the [[Temple of Asmodeus in Collegento]], nervously inspecting a ballista. There's an [[Erinyes]] stnding next to him, insuring him it will work out.

"The Forgemaster awaits", she says.

We inform the troops of this, and distribute a few healing potions before calling it a night.
