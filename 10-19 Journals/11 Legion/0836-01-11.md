[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]]]

While [[Tiziano]] fetches the [Dancing Hut] and brings it around to the other side of the city, [[Arinya]] and [[Legion|myself]] go to the warehouse in [[Lower Amaroth]] to pick up [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]] - or one of the decoys, we don't know.

We're greeted by [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] and two other individuals: [[Verras]], the Arch Mage of diplomacy, and [[Elostrum]], the Arch Mage of Culture.

They inform us that we're to travel through Velgend, where we'll be joined by an escort of Elves from the Morthïr faction. It's likely that people from Ashein will try to take the bell by force.

We're lead to the room where the bell is kept, and I open our portable hole to place it inside.
[[Verras]] moves it into the hole by magic, and we depart towards Skogsland - Velgend.
The weather is a beautiful winter wonderland.

We're passed by people on horseback after a few hours, but they don't seem very interested in us.
