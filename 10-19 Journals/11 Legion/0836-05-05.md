Tiziano casts [[Find the path]], and we carry on.
The wind, the cutting sand, the distance and the heat is taking it's toll on the group, and a few  get rather exhausted.

We do make it close enough to the mountains to see what they look like, and it's extremely odd. The entire mountain range looks downright unnatural. It looks like it's been smashed together and torn apart multiple times by giant forces, some sections look carved away and other look pushed together.

Plots horen we "travellers... adventurers... experts. Come to my cave. I want to talk." in een fluisterende stem.
we see purple glowing spheres starting to illuminate a path between two tall cliff edges. The path is too narrow to ride my horse. After a while the path goes at an angle into a cave in the purple cliff.

Four arms, from 2 shoulders
his torso dissolves to the bottom into a purple mist
all of his skin is also purple mist

his head is that of a stag, with antlers and all

He says that he wants us to kill the other little gods. Little gods can kill and eat other little gods and become bigger gods. He wants to be more.
We begrijpen dat tijdens het sterven van de God van de Magie, héél veel kleine shards of devinity verspreid werden, die apart niet veel waard zijn, maar sterker kunnen worden door andere shards te verorberen.

I ask [[Nivemera]]. She thinks he's a shard of divinity that wants to gather more shards of divinity.

Arinya vraagt hem wat hij wil doen eens hij sterker is
He says he wants to change the world, but he doesn't know how or in what way.

Ik vraag hem hoeveel andere little gods dat er nog nabij zijn
hij heeft weet van The Purple Sun. He doesn't like him, he's not nice to him. They tried to kill each other.

Ik vraag hoe dat The Purple Sun vecht. He eats the spells and spits them back out.

They call him (the one we're talking to right now, not the purple sun) "The [[Changer of Ways]]".

From talking to him it becomes clear that nivemera was almost right.
He's not a full shard of divinity, but a partial shard of divinity that wants to become a full shard.

Arinya agrees to help him and shakes on it.

Arinya asks if we can call him "Changer" for short, and he agrees.

He gives us a pointy stone that acts as a compass, pointing towards the purple sun.

After a while travelling, we see the purple sun in the distance
We see him hovering above the ground, with wind and dust swirling around him/it chaotically

It attacks us on sight
I run in on my horse Unit with great speed, as it summons up an animated fireball in my path that meets me before I reach the Purple Sun

He summons a spiritual weapon on my other side, flanking me with the animated fireball.

As tiziano casts [[Haste]] on me, I throw caution to the wind, ignoring both these foes in favor of rushing the [[Purple Sun]] once more. Both attack me but neither manage to pass my defences, and as I force myself into the blistering heat of the presence of the [[Purple Sun]], I deal three heavy blows with my [[Forceful Glaive]]. Although the heat output is high, what really hits me are the forcefull pressure waves emanating from it

Arinya reflexively summons a [[Wall of Ice]] between us and retreats.

Arinya attacks the summoned opponents as I give chase to the purple sun
Once again the shockwaves pound into me, as I lay into the thing with my [[Forceful Glaive]] once more.

It retalliates in a devastating manner though, as both it and another of it's summoned creatures tear into me from two sides, killing my summoned horse and making me black out. My built-in emergency healing potion heals me just enough to save me from certain death, but I'm down and out.
As it summons more enemies to occupy my allies and it's pressure waves pound into my lifeless self, tiziano gets close enough to fire off a healing spell, turning me into a combatant again. (I only learnt of how this happened afterwards obviously, seeing how I was unconcious.)

I use my [[Lay on Hands]] ability to heal myself further, just in time for me to barely withstand another round of it's full-on assault. I remain standing this time, but I won't be able to wither this much longer.

I strike at it again, sending the full damage of my smite ability into it. I give as good as I get

My allies are no slouches either, as they methodically dismantle the summoned creatures one by one.

Tizi protectively casts [[Polymorph]] on me to turn me into a [[Tyrannosaurus Rex]], and turns himself into an [[Air Elemental]]. The foe manages to distance itself from us, but[[Arinya]] finishes the [[Purple Sun]] off with her [[Eldritch Blast]], after which [[Tiziano]] and I take care of the last remaining summoned creatures. While finishing off these creatures, I can see how the purple sun turns into a miniature black hole, sucking in a bunch of matter with it. It leaves behind an artefact that [[Arinya]] picks up and identifies.

Het is een stukje van de God of Magic, het kan spells en magic items 1x per dag rechargen. Méér dan interessant! Verder groen stof zoals van de kristallen, maar er is niks bruikbaars bij. Het lijkt alsof hier een samenkomst is van magische lijnen waardoor Purple hier graag rondhing.

We spend the night here and lick our wounds.
