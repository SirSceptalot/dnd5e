We exit the room and see several tree lords, dryads and a bunch of other related creatures.

We have a meeting with the [[Lady of Vines]] and her captains, and manage to convince her to lend us her shard of divinity through sheer openness and honesty.

As we talk and I see her up close, she seems much older than I initially expected.
She says she's hesitant to just give away the verdian crown to anyone that asks it, but she realises the gravity of the situation.

"I would like some assurances, and would like to offer you help, and as it is probably known to all of you, to gain a thing, someone must also give a thing. I ask of you a token of your goodwill, and for you to complete a taks for us. Once you do our alliance will flower and bloom, and we may lend you the verdian crown. We may assist you in you battle to come, I may even send out the First Thorn of Kurnoth"

Kurnoth is the old, dead, god of the hunt, who was in a relationship with [[Melora]], goddess of nature. A Kurnoth Hunter what could be called a child of the two, part hunter, part nature.

"In our beloved forest, an nightmare has arisen. This thing has a tendency to twist forest folk and twisting our desires into a darkers aspect of ourselves. This is why I don't want to send any of our forest folk."

The "nightmare" ceature, from what we've heard of it, is not a devil. It's a more sort of creature connected to the magic of the land than just a creature from another plane of existence.
It could possibly be older than gods, and be created out of nothing as a side effect of the creation of the world.

We are teleported to the corrupted forest.
Upon arriving, there's a *dead* silence. There are a few small noises that just highlight the absolute, suppresive silence. [[Tiziano]] has it worst of all, since his connection to the forest and nature let him notice far more than the rest of us how silent everything is.
[[Tiziano]] also notices a sigh in the distance, a sigh of contentment for the arrival of something new.

[[Tiziano]] and I look for useful traces, and find none.
He casts a spell to locate ravens, in order to speak with them.

They warn us of an angry guardian by the name of [[Drycha Hamadreth]], who has zero tolerance for creatures that don't belong. To be fair, the ravens didn't tell us the name, but [[Nivemera]] recognized the legend by the description.

We have no idea how to find anything, so we start to walk. After a short time, a loud shrieking announces the arrival of [[Drycha Hamadreth]].

As it's charging us, we desperately try to convince it not to fight us, which doesn't work quite as well as we had hoped. However, after a short scuffle [[Arinya]] and I do manage to turn the tide and end things. Perhaps not entirely amicably, but well enough to leave each other alone.

After a short rest, we carry on. We start to notice that the beast is toying with us, appearing in our peripheral vision but vanishing before we can get a clear look at it.

[[Arinya]] guesses that she might be able to lure it out by taking a nap, since it was clearly sold to us as haunting people in their nightmares.
The plan seems to work. Shortly after she dozes off, she starts tossing and turning.
When she suddenly produces a bleeding gash, we try to wake her up but don't seem to have success. However, she does suddenly wake up with a gasp, as the nightmare creature suddenly appears behind us, looking more confused than we are.

[[Arinya]] later told us that she dreamed Amaroth in ruins, and her parent's cabin in ashes.
As she roamed across [[Skogsland]], all she could see was death and decay, and banners with a large letter H. Perhaps a reference to Hadak.

She said that she started insulting the nightmare beast as soon as she saw it.
It attacked her, which is when she was also wounded in real life. In her dream, she managed to cast Banishment, which is when the beast appeared in real life.

A furious battle ensued, which would show us exactly why the other forces of the forest didn't want to get involved. Besides the handful of thralls it had under it's control, it was a fury on the battlefield, teleporting to and fro, while casting a variety of spells such as disintegrate, attacking our minds, but also charging us with it's antlers, and slashing at us with it's too many limbs.

With some solid teamwork, we were able to defeat the beast.

[[Tiziano]] is able to extract a gem from the corpse that it used as focus point to cast spells, which I store in the [[Portable Hole]].

I cut out the heart of the beast as proof, and Tizi teleports us back to the [[Lady of Vines]].

After presenting proof and a short conversation, we're presented with the [[Verdian Crown]], which we may use until we've been able to defeat [[Hadak]].


In the skull, a gem is buried which tizi clumbsily extracts by bashing it as an ape.
I cut out the heart of the beast.
We then teleport back to the hidden glade.

After presenting the heart of the beast, we receive [[Shard of Divinity - Nature|The Verdian Crown]], to be returned after [[Hadak]] is defeated.

After taking a short rest, we try [[Scrying]] on [[Reckoning]] and the horned devil. We learn nothing of use with the former, and the latter only shows us that he's flying somewhere over [[Quintus Mons]].


The local loremaster revealed to us the enigmatic nature of the [[Bibliotheca Aeterna]]. It is not a physical place, but rather one that manifests when it is truly needed. Some locations serve as easier gateways to this mystical library than others. ^cfe59d

An example of its storied history involves the vampires of the Grivor bloodline. Their origin traces back to when the god of undead chose to invest more profoundly in mortals, making them one of the earliest vampiric lineages. After the god of undead perished, the Grivor vampires assumed control over other undead bloodlines and began to amass a vast repository of knowledge. Approximately 240 years before the Diversion, they lost much of this accumulated lore. However, due to the magical nature of their resurrection, much of this lost knowledge was magically restored. ^2e7f94

It is said that if one's need is dire enough while in a library, they may find themselves transported to the elusive Black Library. One potential requirement for this is becoming lost in a library. These vast repositories of knowledge are magically interconnected, and truly skilled magical librarians possess the ability to navigate between libraries through these hidden portals. ^c1a05e

After a brief discussion, we considered reaching [[Anaire Tyualzara]], the ancient linnorm, before Hadak could get to her.

[[Arinya]] performed a ritual to contact another plane. During the ritual, she sensed her psyche connecting with a dark entity, which allowed her to ask five questions.

1. Can [[Hadak]] obtain the shard of divinity from [[Anaire Tyualzara]] by the end of the day tomorrow? The entity responded, "Technically, yes."
2. Is it likely that the [[Queen of Scales]] would win against Hadak? The entity replied, "She doesn't want to."
3. Can you give us the name of the next shard [[Hadak]] is going for? The answer was, "[[Whisperer]]."
4. Can you give us the name of an ally on our side who knows the location of the [[[Anaire Tyualzara|Queen of Scales]]? The entity named "[[Salvatore]]."
5. In your perception, do you think we can get the shard before [[Hadak]]? The entity ominously replied, "You better run, little girl, and hurry; there is a small chance."

With each response, [[Arinya]] felt herself becoming more constricted by an entity with scales, and the pain grew until she was jolted out of her trance after the final reply.

We contemplated how to contact [[Salvatore]]. The only method we could think of was using our [[Sending Stones]] to contact [[Verras]] and asking her to relay a message to [[Salvatore]], requesting the location of [[Anaire Tyualzara]] and an update on his progress.

I sent the following message using the [[Sending Stones]]:

"Can you please ask Salvatore to give us the location of Anaire Tyualzara, plus an update on his progress? Alternatively, do you know its location?"

The reply came:
"Hello Legion, nice to hear from you. I'll pass the message along. How are things? It's been quiet. [[Verras]]."

Shortly after, I heard the following message in my head:
"Legion, kindly go to sleep tonight while holding hands. Kindly, [[Salvatore]]."

At the end of the day, we followed Salvatore's instructions. I lay down with [[Tiziano]] and [[Arinya]], holding hands.

We started dreaming of being on a red/purple-ish beach with a lighthouse in the distance and a storm forming above. In front of us stood [[Salvatore]], with [[Arinya]] and [[Tiziano]] by my side.

Salvatore greeted us with, "So, you wanted to talk." We explained our predicament with [[Anaire Tyualzara]], her shard, [[Hadak]], and the ritual [[Arinya]] had performed.

[[Salvatore]] knew the rough location of the [[Anaire Tyualzara|Queen of Scales]] but said she was extremely hard to find. He also updated us on his progress with the vampires, stating they were interested in an alliance. We agreed on a time and location for a meeting the following day, which we would reach via the [[Transport via Plants]] spell.

After that, we spent the rest of the night in peace.
