[Location:: aboard the [[Dauntless Explorer]]]
[Mission:: ]

[[Tiziano]]'s speech starts to make sense again today. I help him with work on his belt again.
[[Arinya]] does some more research on [[Ambrosia]], but is missing a few key facts to be able to do much useful research.
She takes it to [[Tiziano]] and [[Nivemera]], and together they do come to an understanding:
The reason why demon or angel blood works best, is because they have been given their power directly from a deity, making it more stable in such applications.

Hence, in order to make Ambrosia WITHOUT using the blood of angels or demons, either the blessing of a god is required (which won't happen), or the blood of a Great Old One, or one of it's children.

[[Arinya]] then turns to [[Illion]] to ask for more information, who pushes this sentence into her mind: *Every old one loses it's child and then yearns for a surrogate*.
This prompts the realisation that [[The Child]] which we killed in the other realm was likely the spawn of a Great Old One.

We might be able to use it's blood to craft Ambrosia, but going back to a strange dimension after killing a god's child there is on our schedule in the foreseeable future.

While they're doing that, I have a sudden brilliant insight myself while working on the schematics for my [[Defence upgrade]]: I could just attach a leather strap to my shield. I get to work straight away and finish my project quickly.

At a certain point later that day, there's some commotion above deck.
There appears to be a man in a sloop drifting about. Upon sending off to meet him, he seems completely out of it and only repeats "the black" over and over.

He seems dehydrated and in poor condition, but not wounded.
We take him aboard and go through his things to search for clues of his origin
We find only some non-magical trinkets and one Mind Rune, which can give advantage when trying to resist a magical insanity effect.

We take him into our chambers to ensure he doesn't hurt himself.

Addendum: we later learn the man's name is [[Geoffry]]
