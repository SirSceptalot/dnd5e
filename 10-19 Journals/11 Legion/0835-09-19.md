[Location:: [[Collegento]]]
We go to [[Nivemer's Stand]], where [[Fiona]] and [[Spyro]] do a flyover to see if they can find the priest or a signal of his. They don't, city is filled with fog, seriously hindering their scouting ability.

They do find an unexplained light source, a pulsing red glow.

When we go in, we find a large amount of hastily placed poles with religious amulets and the likes hung from them, likely to keep whatever unholy forces are inside from leaving.

When we come to the large red gem that's emitting the light pulses, we can see that it's damaged and slowly cracking further. We decide to go back out to talk to the priests to ask for advice, since we know little about it - except that it probably contains the soul of Nivemera.

The first temple we visit is the [[Temple of Sarenrae in Collegento]] where [[Waldrun]] advises us to only involve [[Horacious]] of the [[Temple of Pelor in Collegento]]. Since Nivemera was a cleric of Pelor, there could be some political upheaval if too many other priests got involved.

It doesn't take a long conversation with [[Horacious]] to realise why Waldrun thinks that way - the man is on a power trip and likely only brings a smile to someone's face when they see him leave.

Despite his big talk of not being afraid, he's quick to appoint someone else to join us to investigate further, but only after extensive prodding. I get the feeling he barely cares about the people of the city, and only wants to hold a position of power.

An Acolyte by the name of [[Leandros]] will join us to help investigate what's going on inside the palisade.

After returning to the glowing rock, he can't tell us much about it sadly.
[[Tiziano]] is able to pick up a few tracks, of which we follow the first set to a small building. We find no life inside, but [[Arinya]] does pick up a piece of paper describing a ritual to contact other dimensions, which she adds to her collection.

We go back outside to follow the other tracks, which lead to a larger building where we can find the abandoned campsite of the people we're looking for. It doesn't look like they slept here more than once though, so this isn't really giving us much hope in regards to their chances of survival.

We do tour some of the biggest streets around the district, but find no sign of them. Tiziano's keen eye spots several strange creatures around the city spying on us, but since they leave us alone, we decide to return the favour.
Besides that, he also spots a red crystal which we fail to identify, but still take with us. It looks somewhat similar to the red crystal that supposedly houses Nivemera's soul.

We decide to go to bed in the early evening in order to be fresh for the latter part of the night, since most activity is said to take place during the night.
