[Location:: [[Ermedes]]]
After everyone wakes up, the usual breakfast buffet awaits, after which we're guided to the assembly hall where the prince, [[Naarhunthes]] and the other high priests and the captain of the guard are seated. [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] is standing apart as an observer.

After a few formalities, we're offered free stay at the [[Yellow Cup]] anytime we visit the city, along with his gratitude and that of the city of [[Ermedes]], calling us friends of the city.

After we left the room, [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] takes me aside and tells me that until otherwise recalled, I'm to go out and act as a roaming independent law keeper, ensuring justice is served wherever I go.
I'm especially to keep a keen eye out for any potential threats to the peace between [[Ashein]] and [[Skogsland]].

I promise to try to uphold the ideals and moral values of [[Paragon]] wherever I go.

We warn the three girls of our immanent departure, collect the [[Seal of The Experts]] Arinya had [[Gaullir Veroulle]] carve for us, and take of in our horse drawn carriage, at roughly mid-morning.

The rest of the day is spent travelling, while [[Arinya]] crafts a number of the much needed potions of healing and [[Tiziano]] further attempts to teach me the Aquan language.

Aside from a few checkpoints where [[Ephemera]] invokes her freedom to travel granted by her citizenship of a Free City, the journey is uneventful. Passage might have been more challenging without her, as it's clear my kind are not appreciated here.

At night, we set up camp in a quiet area.
