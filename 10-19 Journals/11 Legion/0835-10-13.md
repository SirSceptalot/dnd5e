[Location:: [[Avelund]]]
[Mission:: [[Restore productivity of Avelund mines]]]

I bury the cat corpse and dub it snoopy.

We have a look if we can trace where Jericho went, but while we're following his tracks, a patrol  attacks us without any warning or provocation.
Not a bad fight. The large mutated bastards spawn some sort of monsters when they die.
There are other patrols approaching after the fight so we make sure we're gone, not wanting to get an army on our heads. We take a short rest in Emerson Hale's house. While we're hiding there we can hear that our fight caused a lot of commotion outside, as there are a bunch of soldiers running around.

We can't sit around and wait all day, so Arinya makes herself invisible and scouts ahead to [[Ophelia]]'s house.
A while later she returns with the news that we can try to make it as a group later that day, after things have calmed down.

We talk some more with [[Emerson Hale]], who reveals a number of other people that are part of the resistance.
Allison Raby is a blacksmith that can also do some fighting.
Eugene Grey, a smuggler that changed the direction of smuggling.
The other names he gives us are people we've already met or heard of.

With help of Arinya's invisibility, we make our way to [[Hershel McLachlan]].
On our way, Arinya - who only made me invisible, is stopped by a couple of guards. When they start to use their position to coerce Arinya to go with them, it becomes quite clear that their intentions are not pure, so I attack them.

After the fight, we can hear various screams and shouts in the vicinity. Wanting to avoid getting surrounded by these guys, we run and are able to escape across the rooftops.

When we eventually make our way to [[Hershel McLachlan]], he suggests we start by intercepting or preventing the shipments for Behektur that come from the [[Avelund mines]]. Since Jericho goes there every few days to pick up a small container to take to Behektur, it must be something of significant import.

He's currently on his way there, and Hershel strongly advises we avoid him.

We're starting to formulate something of a plan.
	1. Rest
	2. Sneak out of the city
	3. Make it to the mines without fighting Jericho
	4. Clear the mines

In the meantime, we go to [[Ophelia]] to see what info she can provide. We make it there without incident.
She says she's been in the inner city a few times by becoming invisible, and has noticed that there are often carrier pigeons that leave Behektur's place, to fly out across the ocean.
She'll see if she can find a way to stop the pigeons from reaching their destination. ^88a76c

We work on the plan a bit more with [[Ophelia]], and come to the following plan for the time being:

1. Rest
2. Sneak out of the city
3. Make it to the mines without fighting Jericho.
4. Wait for Jericho to head back to the city
5. Clear the mines.
6. Head back to the city.
7. Wait for Jericho to leave the city again, for the mines or otherwise.
8. Kill Jericho (still a bit fuzzy on this part of the plan, perhaps with a trap)
9. Liberate the outskirts.
10. Liberate the inner city.

The plan does get a bit fuzzy from point 8 onwards.
Before heading out towards the mines we decide to go introduce ourselves to [[Eugene Grey]].
We make it there without issue by way of invisibility. Once introduced, we continue to make our way out the city.
Once we're a good distance from the city and the path, we find a place to camp for the night in Arinya's tiny hut.

At the end of the night, we do in fact spot [[Jericho]] in the distance making his way back to the city.
