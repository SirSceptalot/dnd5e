[Location:: On the road to Esterna]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

Arrival in [[Esterna]] at the crack of dawn.
[[Cordelia]] points us to a location where we can park the [[Dancing Hut]].

As we enter the [[Cradle District]], we're lead to the [[Temple of Erathis in Esterna]], which [[Cordelia]] informs us is named "[[Temple of Erathis in Esterna|The Cathedral of The Builder]]", where the rest of the [[Judicators]] have arrived. 

It would seem all the head temples are here in this single district, as they're all significantly larger than any other building - even other temples - in the city.

We're lead into a smaller building at the side where a number of other [[Judicators]] are, [[Gunther]] among them, dressed in purple and gold robes as opposed to their usual armour.
They're having breakfast and invite us to join, while [[Cordelia]] excuses herself to her room.

[[Gunther]] already made a report for the council, who's asking to meet with us tonight.
We're free to roam about the city, but advises us to do our best not to appear threatening, given that I'm very clearly foreign military.
We're told we can find most of the weapon and armor shops in the [[Silver Reach]] district, and most of the magic shops in the district just outside the gates, the [[Explorer's Way]].

We visit the [[Temple of Sarenrae in Esterna]].
Although the weather outside is dreary, the inside looks as if glorious sunshine flows through the windows.

[[Arinya]] inquires about repairing the [[Mortar and Pestle]], and a servant quickly fetches a man named [[Galerin]], also known as the [[Galerin|Radiant Lord]].

He clarifies that the artifact is actually property of the temple, but does not intend to take it from us.
He'll tell us how we can repair the artifact if we do something for him first.

They're expecting trouble from the [[Restless Mire]], the swamp up north, but they can't spare the people to investigate it right now.

New mission: [[Settle the Restless Mire]]
Further info has been saved in the mission log.

After accepting this mission we do a bit of shopping. [[Arinya]] sells her [[Wand of the War Mage]] and purchases a [[Rod of the Pact Keeper]].

My companions want a drink, so we head over to a tavern in the [[Cradle District]] called the [[Emerald Pipe]], run by the halfling [[Edmond]].

There are some shenanigans with me playing the part of a simple construct while [[Tiziano]] "controls" me.

After all this we return to the [[Temple of Erathis in Esterna]] to prepare for the debriefing of the [[Judicators]].

We're lead to the palace, into a throne room where a council of 15 is seated.
They're introduced as the [[Council of Faith]].

In the center is a woman clad in radiant white robes and a veil, on a throne that towers above the chairs of the other council members.
There's an advisor-looking type standing next to her.

We recognize [[Galerin]], a priestess of Sarenrae, as one of the council members.
Other members are unknown to us, but here's a short description of the members and their displayed symbol
- Elf, male, Avandra
- Elf, Corellon
- Elf, Seganin
- Dragonborn, Bahamut
- Human, Female, armored, Erathis
- Gnome, Ioun
- Gnome, Asmodeus
- Goliath, male, Kord
- Halfling, Delora
- Dwarf, Moradin
- Dwarf, Pelor
- Unknown (veiled), Raven Queen


They ask for our account of what happened in Collegento.

The Goliath of Kord, the Dwarf of Moradin and the Human of Erathis all seem very interested in the fact that Hadak was able to dominate the Warforged.

The Elf of Corellon says this does not concern Ashein, and proposed to close the borders.

The Goliath of Kord thinks they should use this to invade Skogsland and take back lost territory.

When I try to refute this by claiming this isn't in accordance with the creed of Kord - honorable battle - the human priestess of Erathis answers that there have been plenty of provocations from our side to warrant a war.

After [[Galerin]] brings up the point that this particular situation is caused by devils, the Gnome of Asmodeus speaks up to say that their church does not support Hadak or his actions, and they would be happy to see this situation dealt with.

There is still some doubt in the council towards the church of Asmodeus after this however, and they say the church will be watched for a while.

There allow me to say my piece in favor of peace between our two nations, but I'm not sure it made much of a difference.

When we arrive back at the [[Explorer's Way]], my companions have dinner while I update Ecbert Dhaleus through the sending stones.
I inform him we met with the council, that roughly half of them were friendly towards Skogsland, but new animosities are not inconceivable. No talk of international devil hunt.
He replies that he understand and will keep an eye out for mobilizations.

Once we're in our room, [[Gunther]] comes knocking at our door.
He believes the council won't push for a full-out war straight away, but they likely will take back the "free cities" that came from Ashein - Collegento and Lefrastein.

[[Arinya]] scries on Render, and sees him on a ship doing some manual labor.
He hands over a package to another warforged, who then promptly jumps overboard.

There's no sign of Hadak or the Erinyes.

We spend the night in the [[Explorer's Way]].
