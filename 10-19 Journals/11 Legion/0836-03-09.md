[Location:: [[Ekholm]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

We move on foot towards the fortifications and attempt to lure a patrol away from their towers, which partially works.
During the fight [[Hadak]] shows up, which puts us at a great disadvantage, forcing us to retreat.

We take a short rest to recover in Arinya's Tiny Hut and inform Ecbert Dhaleus of the situation. He requests us to attempt to at least bring back a single dominated Warforged.

We half-way devise a plan to set ourselves between their camp and Ekholm to intercept roaming parties that might go to the city for supplies.
We first go through to Ekholm to find a new [[Javelin of Lightning]], as my last one got lost in the battle against Hadak.
