We finally manage to meet up with the [[StormMaw Tribe]] today. After some negotiating, their leader proposes three trials as a final way to prove our worth as business partner.

A battle of physical strength, wrestling match against the chief's wife [[Verna]]. I win this round.

A battle of constitutional strength, in the form of an eating contest which Tiziano loses.

A battle of wits and dexterity, a flighting match on carried shields which Arinya wins.

Two out of three is acceptable, and a trade relation is agreed upon. During the ensuing celebrations, we find out that the liquor Caleb Arianwen will be trading is produced by a [[Storm Giant]], at a rate of roughly 10-20 barrels per year.
[[Ilavu]] will go over tomorrow to negotiate a slight increase in production.

For the help with the negotiations, I talk Caleb into giving us a 5% cut on his earnings.

The rest of the day and night is spent celebrating.
