[Location:: at sea]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

Day four, still calm weather.
[[Gilmer Chatan|The captain]] says we're fortunate so far to have had descent winds without storms or overly rough seas.

While we're working downstairs - helping the crew out - we suddenly hear a bell ring on the deck.
The crew is fighting with several [[Kuo-Toa]]. We join the battle and make quick work of the fish-men.

They don't have much useful on them, except a few pearls worth an estimated 50 gold.
Also among their belongings is a small piece of the figurehead of a ship, and a few pieces of very fake diamond and other pretty stones.

A few of the crew were wounded during combat, so we use our abilities to help them heal.
