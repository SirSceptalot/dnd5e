- **Damage Output**: 8d10 force damage.
- **Range**: 600 feet (normal), 2400 feet (extended).
- **Ammunition**: None required; energy-based.
- **Recharge Requirement**: A brief delay between shots.

This innovation promises to elevate _Bastion's_ offensive capabilities to unprecedented levels.
