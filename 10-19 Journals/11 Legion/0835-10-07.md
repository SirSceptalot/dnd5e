[Location:: [[Velgend]]]
Finally some good progress with my Aquan lessons.

We arrive in [[Velgend]] by noon, at the northern section called [[Honey Heath]], where we pass by an alchemist store, a general trader and three taverns ( [[The Wanton Jester]], [[Topaz Wand]] and the [[Cracked Tankard]]).

There are a number of messages on the notice board that would indicate a recent influx of elves in the city, along with an increased (or perceived increased) rate of crime.

We ask about this when we stop in the [[Topaz Wand]] for drinks, and are told that there is an elven outpost not too far from there that used to trade with Avandar. Something must have happened there that forced them to leave.

There's a mixed crowd in the bar, so it seems it's not all bad.

Most of the refugees are in the southern shanty town they've dubbed the Forlorn Court.
Issues arise mostly at the outskirts or when there's a market, but other than that the city itself remains pretty safe.

As usual, a few ruin the reputation of the larger crowd.

We swing by [[Griffin's Emporium]] (the trader) a bit later.
Interestingly,  this place carries not only a vial of Hill Giant Strength for a "reasonable" 300 gold coins, but also a potion of Frost Giant Strength, for a heftier fee.
We're low on gold at the moment, so we leave them where they are.

We decide earn a bit of it though, by selling a number of the healing potions Arinya crafted while travelling. We're able to buy supplies to make an equal amount and turn a nice little profit.

We also find a bank that's willing to exchange the bank notes we got selling the Dread Knight Skull (375 gold).

Dinner and rooms wait for us back at [[Topaz Wand]], where we chat a bit with the innkeeper [[Elyn]].
After talking about her past a bit, she offers a scroll of Identify for Arinya to copy into her spell book.
