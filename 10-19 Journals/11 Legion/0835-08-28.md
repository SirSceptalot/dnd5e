[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
At dawn, [[Rizerin]] guides us to the possible location of the [[Morthïr]] ritual.
On our way there, I try to learn some more Aquan, which does start to sound like the actual language.
After a good walk we come across the ritual, and are attacked on sight by the people guarding the ritual.

After fighting them, we investigate the floating rock that was at the centre of the ritual, and find that there is room for different entities in there. [[Arinya]] is subjected to a severe blast of cold while touching the stone, so there might already be a hostile entity in there.

A short dialogue is established, and it comes to light that there are multiple entities in the stone.
It's decided that I'll prepare a spell to determine what the nature of these creatures is.

In the meantime, we collect the items worth selling from the slain.

Loot
Gold pouch (50gp), light mace, chain shirt
2x: studded leather, short sword, long bow, explorers kit, 10 gold (each)
2x: leather armour, short sword, long bow, 20 arrows.

During the night, a number of tiny lights infest a passing dear, making its eyes glow.
It looks at me for a few moments, and then goes on its way.
