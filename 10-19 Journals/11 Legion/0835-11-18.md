[Location:: [[Iglberg]]]
[Mission:: [[Investigate the disappearances in Iglberg]]]

In the morning, we go back to the forest where we're quickly attacked by several [[Yeth Hound|Yeth Hounds]].
[[Tiziano]] makes the trek to the cabin of the Lady of the Woods a lot quicker by transforming himself in a giant eagle and flying us to a nearby location - a small clearing in the forest where he can land.

The rest of the journey is done on foot - we quickly spot that there are several eyes on stalks pointed in the direction of the path to the cabin, so clearly the hag was prepared for company.

We take care to avoid running in their field of view and soon arrive at the cabin, where there are several adults and one child digging around the cabin.

Several [[Redcap|Redcaps]] guard the place.

The people digging appear to be digging out her hut, which has large chicken-like legs at the bottom.
This accounts for the three missing adults ([[Torben]], [[Irfon Gough]] and [[Robert]]) and the male boy - the whereabouts of the girls are unknown at this point.

Negotiations with the [[Lady of the Woods]] fail and a fight ensues. The "sisters" of the hag emerge from the pond next to the house. This is one bipedal creature with three heads. There appear to be children trapped in its abdomen.

We're able to kill all the opponents except the hag itself, who turns invisible and manages to escape despite our best efforts to track her.

After the fight we quickly try to rescue the children from the belly of the triple-headed creature, but soon realize that they're not just inside the beast but somehow merged into it.

We find the four girls inside the cabin.

After a little searching and trying, Tiziano manages to activate the magical hut, so we now have a [[Dancing Hut]]!

Once again, we take the equipment from the fallen redcaps to give to the villagers.

There's a letter inside the hut as well.

We finish excavating out the dancing hut and use it to go back to [[Iglberg]]. After reuniting [[Torben]] with [[Galiéna]] and letting the others go back home as well, we inform [[Cerian Bedham]] of the (now resolved) situation.

On a whim, I attach a pair of the Redcap boots to the front of the inn, and suggest they now have an actual name for their inn: [[Redcap Boots]].
