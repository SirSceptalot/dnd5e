# Prologue
**To the Finder of This Journal,**

If you are reading these words, it means my time has ended, and I have departed from this plane—though not, I hope, without purpose. I am Legion, a warforged Paladin created to serve a singular cause during a time of chaos: to uphold justice and prevent the atrocities of war. I was forged by Ecbert Dhaleus for Paragon, a military organization born from his personal dissatisfaction with the increasingly ugly and bloody nature of the war. Paragon was his "pet project," created to combat war crimes and restore a sense of honor to a conflict that had become a stain on the ideals it once stood for. This duty became the core of my existence, and even in the immediate aftermath of the Last War, its legacy will shape the choices I will make.

The pages of this journal will chronicle the journey of a being created not to live, but to serve. My story begins in the fires of war and will span the fragile peace that is now unfolding, a peace whose terms will reshape the lives of all who fought, including my own. As the truce between Skogsland and Ashein is forged, my role will evolve. I will be assigned to protect those who seek to heal the wounds of the world, such as Arinya Vallin, whose quest for the Mortar and Pestle will intertwine her fate with mine.

As a Paladin of the Crown, I swear an oath to protect civilization, uphold its laws, and champion the balance of justice above all else. To the people of Skogsland: your hope will be my armor, your well-being my sword. To the fields and forests, rivers and mountains: I vow to guard your integrity. And to the spirit of justice: I pledge my unyielding loyalty, above all thrones and councils.

I begin writing this journal because it becomes clear the truce will change my life irrevocably. In these pages, you will find my thoughts, my decisions, and my deeds—for better or worse. Let my victories inspire you, my failures caution you, and my choices remind you that purpose can endure even beyond its original design.

If my words reach you, then my time will have passed, and the mantle of action will be yours. Should you choose to continue the path I walk, let these pages guide you. And if you seek only to understand, know this: I am a construct of metal and will, but I strive to be more. My purpose is to protect, to serve, and to strive for justice.

May your path be brighter for having found mine.

— Legion

# Events 835-07-31

Our journey led us to the village of [[Iglberg]], where we encountered a peculiar scene: soldiers camped before an elf engaged in administrative tasks. The elf introduced himself as [[Emil]], an official in the village. Nearby, a Tortle named [[Tiziano Vecellio]] was reviewing a notice on the message board.

The notice detailed a bounty for the elimination of a [[Peryton]] suspected of killing local farmers. [[Arinya|Lady Vallin]] acquired the notice and questioned Emil regarding the attacks. [[Tiziano]] expressed skepticism about the creature’s guilt, advocating for evidence before action. We agreed to investigate and verify the Peryton's involvement. Emil provided names of witnesses to assist our inquiry:

- [[Waltraud]]: Widow of one of the victims and witness to the attack.
- [[Kajetan]]: Another widow who witnessed the creature’s actions.
- [[Mina]]: Wife of the first victim, though she did not see the attack.

A hedge knight had also reportedly taken interest in the bounty, though we did not encounter them directly.

## Investigation

Our first task was to examine the victims' remains in the graveyard. With notable skill, [[Tiziano]] conducted an autopsy. Each corpse had suffered catastrophic injuries, but only the heart had been consumed. The wounds appeared consistent with those inflicted by a large bird, though they could not be conclusively identified.

1. **Witness Accounts**
    
    - **[[Kajetan]]**: Described the creature descending upon her husband from the forest. She heard a screech and found his body upon returning with help. During the discussion, we learned of ongoing [[Gnoll]] raids in the area.
    - **[[Waltraud]]**: Initially hostile, greeted us with a crossbow. She described the beast as having no front limbs, contradicting sketches of the Peryton. She admitted she fled the scene and provided minimal additional information.
    - **[[Mina]]**: Discovered her husband's body after the attack but did not witness it. She revealed that the three victims were close friends, along with a fourth man named [[Volker]], who left the village a month ago.
2. **Volker's Connection**
    
    - [[Volker]] was married to [[Helmine]], the town wisdom. Upon visiting her home, we found her absent. A neighbor suggested she was likely in the forest gathering herbs.

While tracking Helmine, we were ambushed by [[Gnoll]]s. The skirmish resulted in their defeat. From the bodies, we recovered:

- 3 sets of hide armor
- 3 wooden shields
- 3 spears
- 3 longbows
- Assorted coins, totaling 22 gold.

Further investigation revealed the Peryton near the village. It appeared majestic, with a stag's body and deep blue wings. We refrained from engaging and returned to Helmine’s home.

## Helmine's Cabin

At Helmine’s cabin, a foul odor prompted further investigation. Inside, we found a ritual circle, rotting corpses, and a collection of notes. [[Arinya]] recovered a document detailing Helmine’s account:

- Helmine sought aid from a mysterious "[[Lady of the Woods]]" after learning of [[Volker]]’s infidelity.
- She performed a ritual intended to bind Volker to her but instead killed his paramour and mortally wounded him.
- The failed ritual transformed Helmine into a [[Peryton]], initiating the recent attacks.

We secured the area and decided to confront Helmine upon her return.

## Final Confrontation

At dawn, the Peryton returned. Despite attempts at diplomacy, it attacked. After a brief but intense battle, the creature, revealed to be Helmine, was neutralized. Her remains were delivered to Emil as proof of the completed mission.

## Aftermath

Emil rewarded us with 100 gold for the Peryton’s death and an additional 15 gold for eliminating the Gnolls. I negotiated additional compensation:

- Lifetime free use of Helmine’s house, managed by the owner of [[Redcap Boots]].
- A discount at the armory in [[Heidelburg]].

The new town wisdom, [[Cerian Bedham]], tended to [[Tiziano]]’s injuries and conversed with Arinya. Emil expressed interest in uncovering more about the "[[Lady of the Woods]]" and indicated he had additional work for us in [[Heidelburg]].

The day concluded with arrangements made for our next steps.

## Bestiary

- **[[Peryton]]:** A stag-bird hybrid with a brown body and deep blue wings. Known for targeting humans and consuming their hearts. Recent attacks in [[Iglberg]] were revealed to be caused by [[Helmine]] after a failed ritual transformed her into this creature.
- **[[Gnoll]]:** Savage humanoids resembling hyenas. Engaged in raids near [[Iglberg]] and ambushed our party in the forest. Equipped with hide armor, wooden shields, spears, and longbows.

## People

- **[[Tiziano|Tiziano Vecellio]]:** Tortle encountered in [[Iglberg]]. Skilled in forensic analysis and prefers peaceful resolutions. Assists the team with investigations and combat.
- **[[Emil]]:** Local administrator of [[Iglberg]]. Provided witness information and rewarded the team for eliminating threats.
- **[[Waltraud]]:** Widow of one of the victims. Described the [[Peryton]] as lacking front limbs. Hostile but cooperative once calmed.
- **[[Kajetan]]:** Widow of another victim. Witnessed the [[Peryton]] attack her husband.
- **[[Mina]]:** Widow of the first victim. Did not witness the attack but provided information about the victims’ connection to [[Volker]].
- **[[Volker]]:** Former resident of [[Iglberg]] and husband of [[Helmine]]. Left the village a month prior to the [[Peryton]] attacks. His infidelity and involvement in a failed ritual were central to the events.
- **[[Helmine]]:** Former town wisdom of [[Iglberg]]. Transformed into a [[Peryton]] after a failed ritual. Eliminated during the final confrontation.
- **[[Cerian Bedham]]:** The new town wisdom of [[Iglberg]]. Assisted with medical aid and conversed with [[Arinya]].

## Organisations

- **[[Redcap Boots]]:** Agreed to manage Helmine’s house. Will rent it out when not in use by the party and provide accommodations as needed.

## Locations

- **[[Iglberg]]:** A small village plagued by [[Peryton]] attacks and [[Gnoll]] raids. The center of recent events involving [[Helmine]].
- **Helmine’s Cabin:** Located near the forest. Scene of a failed ritual, resulting in the transformation of [[Helmine]] into a [[Peryton]]. Currently in the party’s possession.
- **[[Heidelburg]]:** Town offering discounts at its armory due to successful negotiations with [[Emil]].

- **Gnoll Equipment:** Hide armor, wooden shields, spears, and longbows retrieved from fallen [[Gnoll]]s. Sold for a modest profit.
- **Reward Gold:** 115 gold coins received from [[Emil]] for the elimination of the [[Peryton]] and [[Gnoll]]s.
