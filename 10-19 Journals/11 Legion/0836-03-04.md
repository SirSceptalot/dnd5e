[Location:: en-route to Heidelburg]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

Day 5/5
Further work on the [[Defence upgrade]].

We make it to [[Heidelburg]] before dusk.

There seems to be a lot of digging at the foundations of the walls around the city, with magical symbols and some sort of sphere at the bottom. We decide to leave it alone.

We go to [[Fizzy Flippy Frost's Fantastic Fascinations]] in order to see if they have anything worthwhile, and invite [[Fizzy Flippy Frost]] to have a few drinks with us in the [[The Singed Hare]] that evening.

We stop by the [[Sunshine Sanctuary for Wayward Dragons]] with [[Spyro]] so he can see how things are there with his old friends, and invite the owner ([[Lady Sybil Ramkin]]) to have drinks with us too.

In the evening, we naturally go to [[The Singed Hare]] for merrymaking, nourishment and rest.
