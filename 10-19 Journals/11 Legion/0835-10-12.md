[Location:: [[Avelund]]]
Updates for mission: [[Restore productivity of Avelund mines]]
We arrive in [[Avelund]] a few hours past sunrise. From afar we can see that the gates of the main city are open. It looks like we can enter the outer ring without issue.

There are several patrols doing perimeter duty though, a few regular looking soldiers accompanied by one exceptionally large specimen.

We walk through the outskirts and stop at a tavern, the [[Harlequin's Alehouse]]. We have a chat with the owner [[Lala]], a female halfling.
She tells the story how a man named [[Behektur]] came into town with a group called the [[Exaltators]], started talking about a new religion and managed to gather a large enough following that he was able to take over the inner city.
A number of people had the foresight to flee into the outskirts, but those that didn't are now trapped inside.

The outskirts aren't safe either, as patrols are constant, and they aren't for the safety of the people. [[Lala]] tells us he's doing his daily speech at the gates, for which we hurry to catch the end of it.
We hear his closing statements about how wonderful life in the inner city is, and leaves a few vials on a small platform. I grab one and we return to the tavern where Arinya performs a ritual to identify the stuff.

She can tell that it's very addictive and would have various random effects, but she can't quite determine what they would be. It has the colour and viscosity of blood, and has some transmutation, necromancy and divine energy or magic in it.

While talking to Lala about who managed to get out or not, she mentions the cleric [[Cleve Pabst]] who resists [[Behektur]].

He was tortured (broken on the wheel) for calling Behektur a false idol.
The poor man is being tended to by a local Wisdom called [[Bertie Bennet]]. We go to visit.

Being in a position to offer help, we do so. He needs surgery to remove bone splinters from the sites of the breaks, after which I use my own magic to heal the cuts.

Bertie recommends we go talk to Ophelia, who is also part of the [[Avelund Resistance]]. Further advice is to avoid going outside during the night.

Back at the tavern, [[Lala]] urges us inside since night is falling. She says the large (mutated) [[Exaltators]] roam the streets stirring trouble at night.

As we hold watch during the night, a sort of mutated hill giant walks around with about ten of the large Exaltators.
When the hill giant sees me, he creates a devastating soundwave, while the others around him seem to have a big laugh.
One of them also kills a stray cat for fun.

After they've passed, Arinya sees someone sneaking around and uses a spell to communicate with him. He says he's on his way to smash the vials he picked up earlier at the gate. He wants to do it specifically at the gate in order to send a message, although he doesn't want to get caught. His name is [[Emerson Hale]], a former guild master.

The mutated hill giant is named [[Jericho]]. If it's his actual name or just what he's called here is unknown.

Emerson also tells us that Hershel McLachlan is the leader of the resistance.
We may also want to visit Virgil Oshea, the mayor of the outskirts.
We get the address from Emerson so we can contact him if needed.

While we were talking, Fiona saw [[Jericho]] leaving the city.
