---

Production starts on my enchanted adamantine chainmail armour.

At dawn, we return to the mansion of † Horace Ringmann to explore the remainder of the crypt.
Upon our arrival, the large crowd announces the death of a guard, whose corpse looks as depleted as that of the initial victim, mr. Ringmann.

Nothing has changed in the room where we last did battle, save for the drying of blood.
Another trap like the first can be found in the downward spiraling corridor, although [[Tiziano|Tiziano's]] keen vision grants me a more peaceful discovery of the item this time around.

The room at the end holds a sarcophagus, and another ensemble of those shadow things, which we are somewhat more successful at fighting this time.

The grave holds a skeleton and a clearly mutated skull with room for eight eyes.
Adorning the skeleton are the following items:
	• A vial containing an unknown substance
	• A ring
	• A helm
	• A glowing axe

The single-edged axe seems of pristine craftsmanship, and glows in the dark.

Tiziano grabs the plaque that adorns the tomb, and after also gather the skull and other items from the grave, we make our way back up.

When we come back in the mansion proper, [[Arinya]] finds a safe and is able to open the lock. To my disappointment, she raids and stashes the contents just as I hear a guard knock on the door.

Deciding to keep the morale lesson for later, I open the door and try to distract the guard, giving her time to restore the painting covering the safe back to its original location.
While doing this, I can clearly hear Tiziano gathering up silverware from a nearby drawer. Thankfully the guard is focused enough on the horrible skull that I'm pushing in his face to not notice, but I don't like the direction this group is taking.

After these shenanigans, we report back to [[Emil]] and hand over the skull. Sadly, it seems Arinya did not take the contents of the safe to hand it over to Emil. Rather than expose her now, I elect to talk to her about it in the evening.

[[Frey Goldhammer]] is able to translate a number of the runes:

Here lies [[† Ogrim Stormbrow]], blessed by [[Lolth]] and slain by the servants of the [[Raven Queen]].
Though he lies dead, he may never rest and one day he will find his way back to Aurocondor to rejoin the war eternal.

While Arinya is off for a few personal errands, I once again visit the administration to see if there is a local law declaring the possessions of the deceased open property, but alas. Arinya will need to return what she has taken.

While returning to [[The Singed Hare]], a telepathic message reaches me from [[Paragon]].

*Legion,
Movement spotted from [[Ashein]].
Paragon on high alert.
Be vigilant and report strange activity.*

I confirm the order to the sender and increase my vigilance.

When reunited with the other in the Singed Hare, I manage to convince [[Arinya]] and [[Tiziano]] to return the stolen items, although it took quite some work.

They agree to return the items at once, so we cross the town square once more towards the administration, although we run into [[Emil]] just as he exits.

Not wanting to complicate our relationship, I decide to be brief and technically honest by declaring the items to be from † Horace Ringmann.

Back at the Singed Hare, we find a while later that [[Frey Goldhammer]] has accepted our invitation to buy him a few beverages in return for his translation work, and the rest of the evening is spent in his company.
