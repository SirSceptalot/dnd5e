[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

During the morning, we see the air ship approaching as the city prepares to defend itself.

The city tries to reach the ship with [[Ballista]] but falls short, while the ship has free range to punish it with it's cannons.

After the city endures this for a while, [[Hadak]] jumps out and falls to the ground like a meteor, followed by a veritable rain of soldiers.

As soon as we see this, [[Tiziano]] transforms into a [[Giant Eagle]], picks [[Arinya]] and [[Legion|I]] up, and flies us to the ship.

We try to approach without the [[Horned Devil]] noticing us but fail. We do make it on the ship though, and what follows is a glorious fight.

The new [[Warforged]] are primarily spellcasters, and quite the worthy opponents. Of greater trouble though, is the [[Horned Devil]] . Good teamwork between [[Tiziano]] and myself defeat him though: we manage to break one of this wings, after which I promptly throw him overboard. We see he manages to fall at a reduced rate, but he's out of the fight.

Another difficult moment comes when one of the new [[Warforged]] manages to [[Dominate]] [[Arinya]], who promptly casts [[Banishment]] on [[Tiziano]], leaving me the sole combatant on the side of good.

I manage to hold my own against the 3 [[Warforged]] and [[Arinya]] long enough to break the spell on [[Arinya]].
After that, [[Tiziano]] is returned, shifting the balance severely back to our favour, allowing us to take the ship.

There is no time for celebration however, as the battle on the ground is still ongoing.

We immediately rush to search the rest of the ship with weapons at the ready, but find no more enemies. The [[Warforged]] we encounter don't attack us on sight, so I waste no time in establishing my rank and having them assist us - which they do without fail.
Besides a number of soldiers, we also find [[Magister Gribolt]]. We can tend to him after the fight.

As the [[Warforged]] come to the top deck and see the corpse of Hadak's Warforged, they search him, find a sphere on his person and try to destroy it.
Upon demanding an explanation, they explain that this [[Orb of Souls]] is an item that [[Hadak]] and his troops use to control them, as it can contain a portion of their soul.

[[Tiziano]], upon my request, is able to break the [[Orb of Souls]] swiftly, gaining us more respect and loyalty from what is now effectively our crew.

After these distractions, we get back to the task at hand: the battle between [[Hadak]] and the [[Divine Empire]]. [[Hadak]] is clearly leaving a path of death and destruction, as even the army of [[Tsukahara Bokuden]] is not able to halt his progress.

Seeing this is not a battle [[Hadak]] will lose, [[Tiziano]] manages to hash out a plan.
I would love to say "we" did this, but it was mostly [[Tiziano]].

1. Stay near [[Tsukahara Bokuden]], at a safe altitude
2. [[Tiziano]] transforms both himself and [[Arinya]] into a [[Giant Eagle]].
3. [Tiziano]] carries me, and the three of us fly stealthily near where [[Tsukahara Bokuden]] will make his stand
4. We feed [[Arinya]] a potion of [[Invisibility]] to improve her chances in the next step
5. When [[Tsukahara Bokuden]] inevitably falls, [[Arinya]] is to swoop in at the last moment and steal his corpse - or the artefact he leaves behind - and fly away
6. If [[Arinya|Arinya's]] eagle form is killed by [[Hadak]], she will attempt to use her [[Cape of the Mountebank]] to cast [[Dimension Door]] to take her to a safe distance, then use her own [[Polymorph]] spell to transform back into a [[Giant Eagle]] and fly back to the ship.
7. If she fails somewhere along the line, [[Tiziano]] will attempt an extraction with his own [[Giant Eagle]] form

At this point, [[Hadak]] is still quite a distance away from [[Tsukahara Bokuden]], so we take the time to try and find targets of opportunity to shoot at with the cannons. Although we do manage to do some damage, in the grand scheme of this this might not have a large impact on the strength of [[Hadak]] and his army.

[[Hadak]] takes his time and methodically uses his limitless [[Fireball]] spells to destroy the resistance.

After a while [[Hadak]] reaches [[Tsukahara Bokuden]], with [[Reckoning]] near him.
Despite [[Tsukahara Bokuden]] clearly charging himself with magic, he doesn't last more than a handful of seconds before [[Hadak|Hadak's]] sheer might and ferociousness flatten him, calling our plan - and especially [[Arinya]] into action.

I'll remember him as a brave warrior who stood his ground in the face of overwhelm superior force.
I hope to one day die as brave as him.

[[Arinya]] speeds towards the battle as [[Hadak|Hadak's]] hammer falls upon [[Tsukahara Bokuden|Tsukahara Bokuden's]] dying body, and manages to snatch it up a mere instant before the hammer creates a crater in the ground where he lay.

In a fury, he manages to catch up with her and deal serious damage, but fly in and distract him she manages to make her escape with the corpse of [[Tsukahara Bokuden]].
[[Tiziano]] and myself are now on the receiving end of his wrath. After a short but brutal and fierce areal battle - about which tales will be told and songs will be written - [[Tiziano]] manages to take the lead and fly us to safety.

We somehow manage to make it back to the ship, all alive and having completed our objectives.

As midday approaches, we collapse on the deck, overwhelmed and overjoyed.

After a short rest, we examine the body of [[Tsukahara Bokuden]], and find the following:

- \+2 Full plate armour. Very intrinsic and well made
- \+2 [[Forceful Glaive]] (1d4 extra damage)
- [[Amulet of Proof against detection and location]]. Requires Attument.
- [[Ring of Mind Shielding]]
- 4 [[Javelin|Javelins]]

Last but not least: the [[Shard of Divinity - War]]

Deciding the surplus of items are of more value being used instead of sitting in our portable hole, we give the following to the crew to distribute among themselves:

- 4 javelins
- +2 full plate armour
- My old [[Greatsword]]

After this, we go below decks to have a conversation with [[Magister Gribolt]]. 
He's lost a lot of weight and has quite a few scars. As we approached, he was reflexively tucking away food in his pockets, although he relaxed and took it back out when he saw it was us.

We ask him to wear the [[Amulet of Proof against detection and location]] for as long as he's still with us, which he eagerly agrees to.

We ask about the [[Orb of Souls]]. He says that Hadak took over the Warforged by force of will at first, but that this was not sustainable. By creating the [[Orb of Souls]], he could hold on to 25 souls per orb without the need to focus on them.

[[Magister Gribolt]] says that Hadak's new type of [[Warforged]] - [[Puppetmaster]] - is created to help control them. They each hold at least one [[Orb of Souls]].

He says destroying the orbs won't be easy. You can do a high level [[Dispel Magic]] on the orb, a regular [[Dispel Magic]] on individual affected, or pass the affected or the orb through an [[Antimagic Field]]. The field won't destroy the orb, but it will break the link and require it to be re-established.
Another method is to attune to the [[Orb of Souls]] and release the souls then, but this isn't a viable method during combat obviously.

Gribolt says that most Warforged are hostile towards Hadak, but a few may align with his cause.
Obviously, his [[Puppetmaster|Puppetmasters]] are.

Render hated being ordered to fight on behalf of meatbags, which was why he aligned with Hadak.

We ask him about the ship, if he held back or delivered his best work. He said he did create the best he could. However, he says a lot of energy being used by the forge can now be diverted elsewhere. He can think of the following enhancements:
- Increase propulsion, to change the speed from 65 to 100 ft. ^40026b
- Enchant the weapons to increase damage and make every attack magical
- Power a shield which could take a certain amount of damage

We opt for increased propulsion, as this is most likely to help us achieve our goals faster and get to all the following shards before Hadak.

When talking about [[Verras]], [[Magister Gribolt|Gribolt's]] mood darkens.
She's far older than she looks, a full blooded Elf of at least 300 years old. Her past from before Skogsland is unknown. He finds her a formidable individual, and is certain she's more interested in her own advancement than that of Skogsland. ^2abe9c

After this conversation we have a look at the provisions: everything is still plentiful except perhaps food.

Lastly, I decide to update Verras about the recent events.

> One battle won. Obtained airship, crew, gribold, shard of [[Tsukahara Bokuden]] and Hadak's keystone. Any ideas from Weiss and Omnissiah on remotely releasing hacked warforged?

The answer that returns it the following:
> I will check with both of them, for now: well done, very good results booked. Keep up the good work.

We decide to name the ship [[Bastion (Ship)]] in honour of [[Bastion]].
