[Location:: en route to the Workshop]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

Travel resumes after breakfast.

When we approach one of the guard towers, we use a dose of [[Clank be gone]] on me, but to no avail - we're detected anyway.

[[Tiziano]] goes on reconnaissance and sees a giant ruined tower emanating a strange glow which travels up in a spiral. As the glow grows upwards, the tower seems restored to it's former glory momentarily.

After we reunite, we move on towards the Workshop.

Eventually we reach it, the door having been torn of it's hinges.
There are multiple gunners inside, another with a sword and a last with a flamethrower.

After defeating them we enter the [[Workshop]], where we find a long hallway with a magical portal. Through the portal is another hallway which leads to a round chamber with an ornate pillar, studded with a few crystals and several holes where the crystals should be.

We're observing this from a little distance, as we're suspecting something bad to happen or be inside here.

When we roll a barrel in towards the pillar, we hear a creature making a noise.
We convince it that we mean it no harm and lets us approach.
Tiziano can identify it as a [[Nothic]], a mage cursed by the gods for his greed or digging too deep for forbidden knowledge. They are deformed creatures who's brains have been damned to seek out all sorts of magical artifacts and trinkets for eternity, without ever knowing why or who they used to be.
His name is [[Bismoth]].

The pilar has a drawing of a dodecagram on it, with each point having a line to one of the 12 sockets that house (or can house) a crystal.

Three of the crystals are in place, the rest of the sockets are still empty.

Each hallway leads to a room through a magical portal, instead of old-fashioned doors. There's a sign above each portal.

The first one we go through is called the Armory, where we find nothing of interest.
Next we enter the Enhancement Chamber, where three man-sized chests/coffins lay, which have been broken through from the inside.

Soon three odd looking warforged walk woodenly towards us. They don't respond to commands or queries, and attack us.
They are obviously defective in a sense, but also very strong physically.
Most notable thing about them however, is that fire actually heals them.

After defeating them, [[Tiziano]] and I examine their remains and the few notes scattered about the place. These look like some sort of prototypes, made from a special material which is likely the cause of both their healing factor and their insanity.

There's a pilar which is vibrating with energy in the center of the room.

It looks like everything of value has already been stripped from the room.
One of these warforged has a crystal like the ones in the center pillar with the dodecagram where his power core is supposed to be.
We take this with us.

The next room is labeled "Soul Extraction Room".

There's also a large central pilar in this room, seemingly sending out waves of energy.
There a large humanoid next to the pillar, connected to it by tubes, blindfolded and with a large sphere on his shoulders. He seems unresponsive to outside stimuli.

There's another of these crystals in the floor where the tubes from this creature meet the floor.

[[Tiziano]] believes this setup is meant to give the spark of life to the freshly built Warforged.

There's a lot of dust on the creature and the sphere, so it looks like he's been there for a long time.

The label on the next portal reads "Dunamus".
Inside is a gray mist that rolls over the floor. There's a rack with three vials and some paperwork.

There's a tripod in the center of the chamber where another of the crystals is placed, and a dodecahedron floating above it.

The mist in one corner is constantly taking shape and fading again, which is odd.
As we all enter, the fog shapes fully into a creature which attacks us.

After the fight, [[Tiziano]] tells us they're probably [[Time Elemental|Time Elementals]], possibly a single creature manifesting itself in different points in time.
Likely immune to poison and psychic damage.

We find a number of papers describing little spheres of energy in nearby swamps. If someone is able to capture this energy, they might possibly be used to create potions that give an effect to can bend time and access other timelines.

Perhaps this is something to investigate later.

As we remove the crystal from the tripod, the mist in the room fades.

The next room has an apparatus looking like an Iron Maiden, except this doesn't look like it's used for torture.
Several tubes lead into the device, coming from containers arranged around it.

Another crystal is in the center of this room.

We decide to sleep here before doing anything else.

During the night, I notice [[Bismoth]] passing by our Tiny Hut, and going through the next portal. A little while later, he returns with another crystal. [[Fiona]] follows him at my request, and she sees him place the crystal in the pillar at the room dubbed Mansion.

She sees him return to the Soul Extraction Chamber, pulling out the crystal there and going back to the Mansion with it again.

We complete our long rest.
