[Location:: at sea]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

Day six.

A shipwreck is found.
From the looks of it, it was heavily damaged by hitting rocks and fixed on sea, but the emergency repairs seem to have failed causing the ship to sink.

After being prodded by [[Arinya]], [[Gilmer Chatan]] says they probably ran aground on the island where we're headed, or from a few rocks not far from here.
According to him, we don't run the risk of running aground anytime soon, so we shouldn't be worried.

[[Arinya]] sends [[Fiona]] out to investigate, but she can't make out much without going underwater, which Arinya tells her not to do.

Arinya then casts water breathing on herself, [[Tiziano]], Fiona and [[Spyro]] so we can go look for ourselves.
We search through the wreck, although Tiziano seems to be mostly having fun underwater - it has been a long time since he's been in a body of water.

Most of what I find is junk, although there is one nice looking sealed leather pouch.
Arinya has a bit more luck, and comes back with 2 bottles of liquor, a peculiar looking jug and a pouch with 44 gold pieces.

Back on the ship, we find that the leather pouch I found contains 2 potions of superior healing - great find.
The jug is identified by one of Arinya's spells as an [[Alchemy Jug]]. She can chose one of several liquids to, upon which the jug produces said liquid to a certain capacity.

We continue the journey, nothing else happens for the rest of the day.
