[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Dispel elemental emitting portals]]]

After breakfast we go to the tower district to meet up with Aldor Vandren who has a task to accomplish.

[[Aldor Vandren's Tower]] tower is a relatively simple and new tower, it seems this wizard has only settled here recently.
The most striking feature is the fact that there are two adjacent doors, only one of which is structurally in the building.

The other door looks more like a portal than a door. We knock on that one, and [[Aldor Vandren]] answers.

We're lead inside, upon which the situation is explained. Due to an accident with his magical tinkering, he has opened a portal that leads to a place filled with fire elementals and lava men called Magmin, who are now spilling out the portal and wreaking havoc in his tower.

New mission:: [[Dispel elemental emitting portals]].

Long story short, we go defeat them and dispel the gate.
They each leave behind some sort of heart or essence, which we take with us in a protective chest - these items remain very hot, so we don't want to set fire to the rest of the items in the portable hole.

Back at the administration building, we inquire about the job in the Free City of Collegento.
After a short conversation with Administrator [[Varus]], it's determined that we might have a conflict of interest and will not receive this task.

The next possible assignment is managed by administrator [[Dreali]], who informs us of missing farmers and children in Iglberg.
There was already a group of freelancers sent there, but they went missing.
We'll head off tomorrow to Investigate the disappearances in Iglberg.
New mission: [[Investigate the disappearances in Iglberg]].

In the meantime, we head back to the [[Demon and Toad]] for nourishment, and to have a better look at the damaged [[Mortar and Pestle]].
When [[Arinya]] casts the spell "Identify" on the item, she can confirm that the damage is indeed more than cosmetic, and might hamper its full potential.

After the meal, we go the library in hopes of finding a bit more information on the object.
We do indeed, but most importantly we found that the original resting place was in the temple of Sarenrae in the Nation of Ashein. This is the first place to go if we want to find how to restore it.

While at the library, Tiziano searches for information on a mythical book that could improve his intellect, but only finds some conjecture on possible locations: Aurocondor or the court of the queen of winter and darkness, somewhere in the Fey Wild.

Tiziano and I spend a few more hours working on the Belt of Hill Giant Strength that he's been crafting for me.
Finally some relaxation playing darts, sleep for my meaty companions.
