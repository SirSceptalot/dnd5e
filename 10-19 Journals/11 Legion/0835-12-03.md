Day five. A bit less wind, but that also means a calmer ocean so we're still able to make good headway.

At night, we're attacked by a barge of [[Orc|Orcs]]. It's a more interesting fight than yesterday, but not the worst we've had by far. We find 82 gold coins and a golden bracelet on them, but we also take their full plate armour and their axes. We tug along their barge, because it seems like a waste to just let it drift away.

I also did my duty during combat by capturing one of their souls, and sending it to hell after a short conversation with [[Nivemera]].

The rest of the day is calm.
