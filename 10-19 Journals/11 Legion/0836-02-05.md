[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Liberate Collegento]]]

Travel to Collegento, day 3/5

A rider on horseback catches up with the [[Dancing Hut]], who turns out to be the "female" warforged. It introduces itself as [[Sentinel]].

Since [[Sentinel]] will be traveling with us, we donate the horse to a nearby farm.
[[Sentinel]] is wearing a new skin suit, which is explained by saying that we're going to a place where the values of Ashein hold much sway, so it's useful to be able to communicate as "human" without being judged as Warforged.
The suit is a gift from [[Vanguard]], an organization similar to Paragon.
The main difference is that, while Paragon dedicates itself to protecting the moral values of Skogsland, Vanguard is a more results-oriented organization that will do whatever it takes to achieve results.
They are not above sacrificing the few or making "tough decisions" to protect Skogsland.
