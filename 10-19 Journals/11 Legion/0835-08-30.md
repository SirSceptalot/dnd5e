[Location:: [[Ermedes]]]
After watching my companions eat breakfast in the magical mansion, I watch them go shopping but not purchase anything.
Shortly thereafter, we are teleported to the city of [[Ermedes]], where we emerge in what seems to be a barn. Exiting that particular building, we find ourselves in the beautiful garden of the small palace

[[Ecbert Dhaleus]] walks us to the nearby palace - although villa might be a more apt description - where he promptly ignores the guards and walks in, the rest of us in tow. We walk into a study, where we are greeted by two individuals.
Prince [[Prassede Altomari]] is joined by a dragonborn dressed in a simple blue robe, a priest of Bahamut named [[Naarhunthes]].

The Archmage explains that we are here to solve the murder, and takes his leave.
After his departure, the prince explains that the murder victim is † Ennio Ginocchio, head of the merchant guild.
He was found dead in his warehouse, stabbed in the back. A letter and symbol were found on his corpse. There is no clue as to the murderer.

The symbol was that of Kord. The letter has a symbol on it as well, but they were not certain of its origin. They suspect one of Ashein, but this is a mere guess.

The letter threatens a man named Bruto Manganiello, which prince [[Prassede Altomari]] says is the head of the [[Blacksmith guild of Ermedes]].

After receiving a letter giving us full access to the city and cooperation of all its inhabitants, we begin our investigation by going south-east to [[Bruto Manganiello]].
We pass the city square where three temples are situated, the Temple of Erathis, Temple of Bahamut and the Temple of Ioun.

After a bit of asking around we find the forge, [[Mineral Minded]], in an impressive three story building.

Bruto himself hasn't seen anything out of the ordinary since the murder. He gives us the location of the victim's widow in the [[Copperbank]] district. The body is kept in the [[Temple of Bahamut in Ermedes]], which is our next stop.

The outside of the extravagantly decorated temple is calm, with only a few people milling about this early in the day.
[[Naarhunthes]] is nowhere to be seen, but another cleric leads us to the body of [[† Ennio Ginocchio]], down in the cold basement.

He has an obvious appearance of a man that was well off.  Rather large with a face that has obviously smiled frequently.

Examining the body, [[Arinya]] notices that his eyes are black with coagulated blood, as if the was subjected to a blinding effect such as the spell Blindness.
There are bruises around his legs, so he might have been tied up or otherwise clamped shortly before his death.

The stab wound came in from his back and exited through his chest. The wound at his back was scorched and burnt, but not boiled. The front of the wound shows no signs of heat, so the weapon was most likely a flaming sword, not a heated metal weapon.

These types of weapons are used by a large array of groups, so there is little to go on here.

Our next stop is his widow, [[Luziana Ginocchio]], which we meet at their beautiful home.
Once there, she openly declares that their marriage had little love lately, as they stayed together more as friends or siblings than as lovers. She's dressed in very ostentatious and pompous hoop dress.

Her husband had many enemies due to his high standing, but other than that nothing noteworthy.
These types of enemies are usually not the type to commit these kinds of murders.

They frequently donated to the [[Temple of Erathis in Ermedes]], so it's safe to say they had more interest in keeping them alive.

He was killed during a routine inspection of the warehouse.
Many people could easily know his schedule, but it was easy to devise if you didn't by observing him for a number of days.

[[Luziana Ginocchio]] says the priestess of Ioun, Liberata Noviello, would be one of her main suspects if she were to look into the murder, besides the owner of the apothecary, Elana Speratza.

She can also give us a piece of information that wives don't usually have: her late husbands favoured brothel: [[The King's Court]].

According to her, Ennio frequently went to the Temple of Bahamut for advice and guidance.

He was a very beloved man, as he spent a lot of money around the city.

Besides her husband (Head of the Merchant Guild), and [[Bruto Manganiello]] (head of the [[Blacksmith guild of Ermedes]]), another prime target would be [[Fleiselvung]]: the head of the Scribe Guild.

Miss Ginocchio believes the end game could be complete destabilization of the city. One group in particular, the [[Black Marauders]], seem to have ambitions to take over the city so would benefit greatly from these deaths.

Ennio opposed trade agreements with [[Ashein]], so someone from there could still be the culprit.

Luziana won't stay in Ermedes now that her husband is dead, but will move to Amaroth where she has family.
Most of the money will go to her, although some of it will flow to the guild.

After speaking with Luziana, my companions are in need of nourishment so we pass by the [[Dancing Beggar]].

Once refueled, the warehouse where Ennio was murdered is our next stop. The warehouse belongs to the guild, not to Ennio, and is shared by a number of guild members, who pay mercenaries to guard it.

Tiziano's keen insight and skills lead us to a number of conclusions.

Firstly, the easiest fact to deduce was that Ennio was murdered in the middle of the warehouse.
Second, it seems the attack came from above. Whoever dealt the killing blow did so by leaping down from the roof support onto the man's back, hitting him so hard the sword went straight through and cracked the floorboard beneath.

Following the tracks in the roof support beams, they seem to originate at a random point among the beams, with no trace of having climbed up there. Along with the information we already had that there was magic at play, it would be safe to assume the murderer teleported into this vantage point.

Lastly, it seems like the tracks went from the site of the murder in the general direction of the mercenary camp, although this is not certain. There are any number of directions the path could have gone after it became too muddled to follow.

Following Tiziano's instinct, we head to the [[Mercenary Ring]], where we can make out four distinct groups, three of which we recognize or can identify.

• [[Band of the Red Hand]]
• [[Black Marauders]]
• [[Jarnbrun]]
• A group with mostly young and lively looking mercenaries.
They seem well organized.
Their banner is that of a white sword with wings on a blue background

There is a strange goblin who sells potions in the camp of Jarnbrun that Tiziano briefly interacts with him, but doesn't purchase anything.
This is also the camp that provided the guards for the warehouse on the night of the murder.
We have a brief discussion with them, but learn nothing new.

[[Rakash]], the strange gnome, engages us a second time, and Arinya exchanges potions: our "poison" for a "mind read".

We then return to Bruto's house to spend the night and keep guard.
