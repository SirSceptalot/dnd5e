[Location:: [[Hillford]]]
We were attacked by a [[Hill Giant]] during the night. It did not survive.

We come upon a small settlement named [[Hillford]] where we pay to have our carriage and horses taken care off, as the rest of the journey will be too difficult to navigate with them.

Before nightfall, a small cave at a narrow path spawns a [[Troll]]. It did not survive.
It did leave behind 2 beautiful stones, a Hill Giant Strength potion, and a vial of Keoghtom's Ointment.

We sleep in the cave where the troll used to live.
