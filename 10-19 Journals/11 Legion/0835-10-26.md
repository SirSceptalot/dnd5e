[Location:: [[Avelund]]]
[Mission:: ]

We scry on Jericho once more. This time, he seems to be on an island chopping down trees with which he's building a raft.

Apart from that, we hear that the situation with the Kobolds at the mines is still a little difficult, but holding for the time being. Production has restarted, and the mine is most likely the most secure in the world since the Kobolts keep adding new traps to keep others out. They dutifully inform the human miners of all new traps.

There is a large celebration in the evening to signify the start of the city's new life.
