Shortly before dawn, the man regains his composure, and we learn his name: [[Geoffry]].
The last memory he has is of [[0836-04-04]], the day after he and the rest of the [[Sacrosanct Monastery|Monestary of Saint Solar Macharius]] were attacked by Hadak.
They stole a [[Crystal of Passage]], an item required to gain entrance to Aurocondor.
While they were still recovering from the initial attack, they were also attacked by some sort of monsters that performed a ritual that turned a number of their defending paladins into abominations themselves.

The abominations occupy most of the coast line.
Geoffry can tell us a few things about fighting these abominations:
- protection from Evil and Good works against them
- they have mages
- They're slow and relatively easy to hit, but resilient
- they show coordinated fighting
- magic does work against them
other then that, they don't seem to have any specific vulnerabilities.
At least some of them seem to have minor regenerative capabilities, but not like a troll. Killing them ends it quite well.

After the conversation, I take some time to write a scroll of Protection from Evil and Good for Arinya.
