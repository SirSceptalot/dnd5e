[Location:: [[Heidelburg]]]
Day 2 of armour production.
Arinya will use the time we wait to craft healing potions for ourselves, and asks if I can take some time to go see if the local library has any more information regarding the Mortar and Pestle.

At the [[Heidelburg Conservatory]], a lady halfling who introduces herself as [[Fausta]] greets me.
She finds a few documents stating that the set has been blessed by Sarenrae. It is supposedly located somewhere beyond Avandar, the last bastion of the elves, in the mountain range called Endike, at the rivers called Gods Fingers.

The city is something of a place of pilgrimage, on account of it being hard to reach due to the rivers and mountains.

After returning from my quest, fraught with peril, I return victorious to relate the hard-won intelligence to lady Arinya.

In order to make myself useful while we wait, I offer my services to administration at the going rate, which is 1 gold, 5 silver a day. Since I can easily run double shifts, this will earn me 3 gold a day.

Reserving 6 hours a day for sentry duty at the inn leaves me a few hours to spare, which I use to tend to the wounded at the town hospital, in consultation with the city Wisdom, the half-elf [[Elysande]].

Sometime during the day, [[Arinya]] had a visit from [[Emil]], who rewarded her with 425 gold coins as reward for returning the property from within the safe of † Horace Ringmann.
Good things come to those who wait.
