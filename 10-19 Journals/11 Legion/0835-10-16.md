[Location:: [[Avelund]]]
[Mission:: [[Restore productivity of Avelund mines]]]

We try to find out what we can from [[Emerson Hale]].
About the dragons, he can tell us that there is rumoured to be an entire island somewhere that has been subjugated by a red dragon.

The inner city also has something of a threat from a floating raft-type island manned/inhabited by Orcs. They occasionally try to raid the city.

We don't get much more useful information from him at the moment.

We have a peek outside, where there seem to be fewer guards than usual. It doesn't look like any fires broke out during the night.

Tiziano asks around a bit outside and can get directions to Almira Maiden's house.
On the way there, we're almost intercepted by a patrol, but Tiziano manages to distract them with a conversation while Arinya and I retreat back with [[Emerson Hale]] into his house.

As it turns out, [[Tiziano]] was able to turn the conversation into a volunteered interrogation.
He uncovers that there is a shortage of Ambrosia, and a number or people have been trying to create it with alternate ingredients, with limited success.
Their mage Krisius has been capturing demons to try and use their blood instead of the celestial or draconic blood to craft Ambrosia,  but this doesn't work out very well.
Results are unreliable, and it turns some people insane.

When Tiziano is reunited with us in Emerson Hale's house, we make another attempt to reach [[Almira Maiden]], with Emerson tied and gagged as our prisoner.

We make it with no issue. She says she's been expecting us, but not this soon. She takes us into her hidden basement to talk in peace.
She reveals that she's the actual leader of the resistance, where Hershel McLachlan was her front and cover.
We tell her what the most recent developments are, and together we try to get some more useful information from the traitor [[Emerson Hale]].

Again, he knows precious little. He guesses they could try to force a captured young white dragon into obedience with enough Ambrosia.
Other than that, he also confirms that the magic shop Wulfa and Heodamerc's Emporium has not yet been raided.

Almira guesses that Jericho should be gone for at least a week.

After all is said and done, I finally take the last step in Emerson Hale's life and execute him by decapitation, in order to spare him from the execution by torture that would likely befall him should he stand trial.

After this unpleasant business is over, I ask Almira why the magic shop is so important. Her answer is that there are few items there that could really help us out.

We plan to go underwater to the docks tomorrow before dawn, raid the magic shop and see what damage we can cause there. If Behektur or Krisius present themselves as a target of opportunity, we might take them. If not, we might try to take out their armoury or other supplies.

First things first though, we still have a number of hours to do something useful in the current day, so we'll see about taking out another patrol like we did yesterday.

Sadly, it didn't go nearly as smooth as last time.
We were able to take them out, but not before they were able to call and get reinforcements.
The second group was starting to surround and wear us down, so we had to bail out before we were able to kill them all.
As a result, we didn't get their weapons and shields.

I was able to steal one of their horns that they used to call for reinforcements though, so we do at least have that.
Also, there are at least two of their elites less, along with a couple of regulars (although their numbers are likely large enough to be called limitless.

While we're retreating, we stay within visual range of several patrols, taunting them somewhat.
I'm not sure if our actions caused this, but at a certain point we heard a different signal blown on one of their horns.
Following this, all of the guards retreated to the inner city, closing the city gates behind them.

Seeing as we now have free reign in the outskirts, we stop by a few people to give and receive updates.
First, [[Ophelia]] to ask if she's found something to stop the carrier pigeons from reaching their destination.
She says she's been working on some automated hunting device, but it isn't finished yet.

We stop by [[Bertie Bennet]]'s place next, to see if she can keep up with the casualties that undoubtedly made their way there after Jericho's attack. The worst is over, and she can handle it at the moment.

Next stop is [[Almira Maiden]], where we debrief and give her the horn to do with as she sees fit.
We also tell her about the retreat signal that sounded earlier, which she hadn't heard.
She immediately goes out to send a few messages around, to make the most of the opportunity to move freely.

While she's gone, [[Tiziano]] sends [[Spyro]] out to have a look at the inner city, where he counts at least 20 [[Exaltators|Exaltator]] Elites.
There are also a large number of oil barrels being moved around which might be either for offence or defence.

In the evening, Arinya makes another potion of healing, after which I start guard duty for the night.
