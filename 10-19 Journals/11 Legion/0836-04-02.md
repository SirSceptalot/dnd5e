[Location:: aboard the [[Dauntless Explorer]]]
[Mission:: ]

Still stormy seas.

An extraordinarily strange moment occurs during this day: we cross a ship that seems to be us from the - or a possible - future.

The other us claim to have returned from Aurocondor, victorious.
A number of details about them have changed from our current state.
My counterpart seems larger and stronger, but my [[Dreadblade]] has also been replaced with a beautiful golden sword.

[[Arinya]] has one eye which looks feline, and one of her hands seems to have withered away.

The other [[Tiziano]] has some sort of eye in his shield.
Afraid of messing with the future, we sail on.
