[Location:: [[Avandar]]]
During the night an envoy of Rahode Yushrish visits us to ask what our decision is regarding the task of preventing the ritual of the Morthïr.
We answer that we will [[Stop the Morthïr ritual]].

After breakfast, we fetch 250 gold coins worth of supplies for [[Arinya]] to craft healing potions.
While in the shop, we ask about the potions we found. The owner is able to identify them as:
	- Greater healing
	- Superior healing
	- Filter of love
The last one is illegal, so I dispose of it.
We keep the other two.

In [[Mado en Gylex' Arsenal]] we are able to purchase the "Wounding" property for my battle axe.

Other than that, we sell the other weapons we have accumulated from various battles, and I resupply my stock of javelins.

We also purchase a [[Wand of the War Mage]] for [[Arinya]], and spend the night in the [[Gypsy's lodge]] once more.
