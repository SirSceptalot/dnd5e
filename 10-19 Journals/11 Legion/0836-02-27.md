[Location:: [[Workshop]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

[[Fiona]] and I tell of what Bismoth did that night.
We go back to the Iron Maiden device, which I start to remember. It's my earlier memory actually, as this is where I was "born" into my body.

Each of the chests here are filled with a material: stone, metal, some sort of fiber on a coil... All materials that make up a warforged.

There are also a number of levers attached to the device.

We enter the next chamber, the "Prototype Room".

There are a number of Warforged body parts scattered about. There's a spread-eagle person-shaped container.

We find schematics of Warforged here, which I take with me. They might come in handy for researching my [[Leg upgrade]].

Besides those there are also three vials of a substance, which according to Arinya's Identify ritual contain a "Mote of Possibility".
These are some sort of things that can mess with time.
When one inbibes one of these, you go back in time slightly once in the next 8 hours, just enough to redo your last action.

Lastly, we find three warforged mutagens.
1. Fey mutagen: gives access to the Fey Step ability
2. Blue Dragonborn mutagen: Can shoot a lightening bolt for 15ft. once per day
3. Aquatic elf mutagen: swim speed equal to land speed

Only one can be activated on a Warforged.

We don't attempt activation just yet, but see what's in the next room first - the Fiber Extraction Chamber.

It looks like an inside garden there, grass floor and a large tree with roots growing all over the chamber.
Upon second glance however, we notice the tree has flesh and hair growing on it as well. As we notice this, the tree turns around and looks drooling at us.

After retreating through the previous portal and peeking back into the fiber extraction chamber, [[Arinya]] is attacked by the tree, after which we defend ourselves.
We find another one of the crystals in machine housed in the same room.

The next room is labeled "Forge", and that's exactly what it looks like. The furnace has an odd blue flame that emits no heat. It holds another crystal.

We peek into the next room (Smelter), where a fire elemental is having a fight with a number of the mini-canon wielding people we fought outside.

Fairly soon the fire elemental is defeated, after which we eliminate the remaining combatants.

During a desperate moment in the fight, I suddenly hear "the Omnisiah hears you", black out for an instant and awaken with my sword through the chest of one of my enemies.

We get some black powder and gold from the corpses, along with a rolled up document.

The following room is dubbed "Nourishment Chamber".
A large room is filled with tables and chairs, and has an automaton cook - which is promptly controlled by the Omnissiah.
It advises us to check the inside of the armor suits in the next room (aptly named the Armory).
We do, and see the corpse of [[Bismoth]] there. As we approach, one of the suits of armor animates and attacks us.
 
 There's a crystal in it, giving us a total of 7 crystals with the remaining 5 already being present in the pillar.
 
 On our way back, we stop by the Prototype Room to infuse my body with the Fey Mutagen, giving me the Fey Step ability.
 
We take a long rest after this very painful procedure.
