[Location:: [[Avandar]]]
When the others awake, I tell the experience I had with the dear and the diminutive creatures invading it.
[[Arinya]] believes it to be a manifestation of the spirit the Morthïr were trying to catch.

As planned, I cast a spell to determine what inhabits the stone, which turns out to be a number of Fey and Elementals.
Other than that, the platform also registers as a consecrated area.

We agree to release the creatures, and I break the rock in order to release the entities. It explodes violently, wounding me lightly in the process.

The resulting cloud of wisps and other ethereal entities frolic around me playfully briefly, before floating off into the forest.

The rest of the day is spent traveling back to [[Avandar]], with Arinya on the small cart the stone used to be on, making potions. Tiziano keeps an eye out for perils along the way, while I draw the cart.

Back at the gates, [[Rizerin]] has a word with the guards, and we're let in.
Going back to the [[Alfarin]] district, we're transported down with a series of lifts, since the cart is too large for the spider transport.

[[Rahode Yushrish]] welcomes pays us the promised 100 platinum coins and an additional 274 gold coins for the weapons and armour we collected after the fight with the cretins from the Morthïr.

Just as we're leaving, I'm contacted mentally by [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] who inquires to my availability.
I answer :" Now. Location: Avandar."
The order "do not resist the following" comes.

[[Ecbert Dhaleus]] teleports in, although with less grace than expected.
He inquires to our location, and swiftly casts Fly on each of us to get us out of the pit quickly.

Once outside, he casts a spell to create a mansion, and lets us enter.
The mansion has all required commodities, and he instructs us to relax, clean up and meet him in the sitting room an hour later.

Once there, he debriefs me and gives me a new assignment, giving my friends the option to join the mission. [[Solve the murder in Ermedes]]
There is a situation in the free city Ermedes, where someone of political import has been murdered.

These cities are supposed to be a neutral buffer zone between [[Skogsland]] and Ashein, but it seems the murderer would rather have the city of Ermedes - where the murder took place - destabilize politically.

We are to solve this murder and apprehend the murderer.

So far, there is no suspect and very little information.
There is a murder victim, and a city that is starting to become polarized politically.

[[Ecbert Dhaleus]] will transport us to the cities' fort, where we will meet prince Prassede Altomari.

Before ending the day, the Archmage is gracious enough to grant my request to upgrade my power core to the one taken from the fallen Clockwork Myrmidon.
