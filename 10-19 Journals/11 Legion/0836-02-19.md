[Location:: [[Restless Mire]]]
[Mission:: [[Settle the Restless Mire]]]

At a certain point, we encounter a large field littered with bodies. After one of them suddenly animates and grabs [[Arinya]], we decide to travel on by flight, courtesy of Air [[Tiziano]].

Underneath, we see ghouls and various other undead.

After a few hours of flight, we reach some ruins. We see no hostile creatures, but there are strange markings and patterns on the ground.

As we approach though, various undead, a necromancer and a [[Shambling Lord]] appear out of nowhere.

The [[Shambling Lord]] was controlled by the necromancer, but both managed to flee.
it's obvious our actions liberated the [[Shambling Lord]] from the necromancer though, so he might be out for revenge for being held captive.

We find various bottles of a dark red substance (which [[Arinya]] can identify as "regular" [[Ambrosia]], some herbs to craft potions with, roughly 750 gold pieces, and a few journal pages.
From these, it's obvious that the necromancer is actually [[Krisius]].

We send a short message to Ecbert Dhalius to ask if they're scrying on Render and Reckoning regularly.
There's a reply to say they are, and they're on a ship.
Several other warforged made landfall and are headed inland.
