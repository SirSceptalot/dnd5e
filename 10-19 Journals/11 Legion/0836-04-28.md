Day 5/8

The fifth day of our journey was marked by progress across several fronts, a testament to the diligence and skill of our crew.

---

## Completion of the Sanctification Ritual

[[Arinya]] successfully completed the casting of [[Hallow]] this morning. Having devoted herself to an uninterrupted 24-hour effort, she retired to her quarters for a well-earned rest. Her commitment to this monumental task is commendable. Upon waking, she plans to prepare another [[Potion of Healing]], further enhancing our preparedness.

---

## Ongoing Projects and Tactical Preparations

### **Armor Enhancement**

[[Weiss]] and I continued our work on the upgrades to my [[Spectre Battle Master Armor]]. This undertaking has demanded precision and focus, but the envisioned improvements— the integration of automatic lightning retaliation—will prove benficial in close combat scenarios.

### **Ship Modifications**

The [[Warforged]] crew began construction of the ship's planter, designed to sustain the oak tree summoned from the [[Feather Token]]. This work proceeds efficiently, and I am confident the completed structure will meet the specifications required for [[Tiziano's]] [[Transport via Plants]] ability.

### **Artillery Progress**

[[Magister Gribolt]] remains engrossed in the development of [[Gribolt's Great Glowing Gun]]. He assures me that this weapon will be operational by the conclusion of our voyage, a timeline that aligns with our strategic needs.

---

## Unanticipated Assistance

Midday, a scroll appeared in my hand, accompanied by a note inscribed in elegant Elven script. The message read: _"Use this, and push it in."_ Given the distinctive penmanship, I suspect [[Verras]] to be the author. With [[Arinya]] still recuperating, I consulted [[Magister Gribolt]] to interpret its contents.

His findings were remarkable: the scroll contains the level 9 spell, [[Gate]]. This formidable magic aligns with [[Verras's]] prior commitment to aid us in confronting the [[Agaros]]. Its acquisition marks a pivotal augmentation to our arsenal, one that shall be employed with both care and precision.

---

## Rest and Reflection

While others toiled, [[Tiziano]] elected to spend the day in quiet reflection, exploring the nearby woods. Such moments of repose are as essential as the work we undertake, providing clarity and fortitude for the challenges ahead.

---

## Closing Observations

The events of this day underscore the unity and resilience of our assembly. From the labor of our [[Warforged]] to the arrival of unexpected aid, each element strengthens our position. As the journey advances, I remain vigilant, ever preparing for the trials yet to come.
