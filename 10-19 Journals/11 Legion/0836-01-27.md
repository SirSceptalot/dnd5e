[Location:: [[Avandar]]]
[Mission:: [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Galguth]]]

After breakfast we coordinate with the officials and go back to the cave where we fought yesterday. We don't encounter Galguth this time, but we do notice that the gate has been toppled.
[[Tiziano]] examines the marks around it and assumes that Galguth tried to drag it off, but didn't manage to complete doing so.

Back in town, we make some room in the portable hole by selling the weapons and armors we took the day before. All in all, it nets us a nice 1845 gold.

In the afternoon, a hunt is organized for Galguth.
Participants besides us are [[Taliesin]], [[Thamior]], [[Vanuath]] and [[Enna]].

We go back to the cave in hopes of finding Galguth there, but instead we find a boobytrap that spawns a number of creatures.
We're victorious, but eventually return to the city without finding Galguth.

We spend the night with [[Taliesin]].
