[Location:: [[Northern Islands]]]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

We're still in the Yuan-ti building in the room where the Hydra was, and go across the hallway now.
After politely knocking, a cobra with the face of a human opens and lets us in.

He's a [[Naga]] called [[Koshalas]], not a big fan of the Yuan-ti.
From the looks of his room, he's quite the magic user.

He's here to guard a golden egg (present in the room) from the demon/deity Sseth, who is worshiped by the Yuan-ti.
More specifically, he's not actually guarding it from Sseth himself - he'd be powerless to stop him - but from the Yuan-ti who would use it for something nefarious such as summoning Sseth.

The egg contains a fragment of Sseth.

[[Koshalas]] makes it quite clear that he despises the Yuan-ti.
After some begging, Arinya receives a one-time use ointment for true seeing, and a scroll of Power Word: Chicken.
In return, she teaches him the Minor Illusion spell.

We ask what could be in the well we saw in another room, and he thinks it's a demi-god, a modified Yuan-ti called an Anathema. They're powerful, but not immortal.

We say our goodbyes around midday and head for the pyramid, which [[Koshalas]] identified as a hatchery.
(During this travel we'll go on to send Fiona back to Koshalas with a note to inform him of the location of the prisoner we found earlier).

On our way to the pyramid, Paragon messages me that they found Behektur in Ekholm. We're to meet up with a team there to destroy him.
New mission: [[Defeat Behektur]]

The pyramid is next to a Yuan-ti village. We avoid this on our way to the pyramid, which is an open style building instead of the usual solid stone structures.
Inside, bodies are being dumped in a well in the centre of the room.

After clearing the available guards, we see that this building was being used for blood sacrifice, and pick up a number of weapons, armour and valuables.

A short rest in the tiny hut later, we begin clearing the other floors. Notable lot there is a wand of spell storing for [[Arinya]].
There's an underground floor which is drenched with blood, and inhabited by [[Assassin Vines]] which guard the Yuan-ti brood.
As a matter of honour, we leave the eggs intact, although a number were trampled in the fight.

We spend the night in the tiny hut on top of the pyramid.
