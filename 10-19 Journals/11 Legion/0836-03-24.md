[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

We scry on Tisiphone, and have success this time. From the looks of it, she's hiding in an abandoned pigeon till.

She notices the scrying eye, says "Welcome back" and stares at the eye until it vanishes.

Going outside, there are a number of pigeon till around, but having [[Fiona]] look inside reveals only pigeons.

Not having a further trace on how to locate her, we go to [[Ealalmod's Vault]] to sell the skeleton bits we got off the dragons:
- 1 horn, for 5 gold
- 5 teeth, for 25 gold each
- Knuckle bones, 5 gold each

185 gold all totaled.

We head back to [[Weiss]] to talk about the possibility of equipping me with wings, but the weight seems to be an issue.
It might work in a very limited fashion if we replace my armor with a more lightweight Mithril version, but I don't want to forgo the capabilities my current armor has.

We head back to the tattoo shop we visited earlier, and agree to get both Arinya and Tiziano a tattoo to enhance their constitution.
This will cost us 2x 2500 gold.

Most of today is spent with setting Aryina's tattoo.

Approaching our [[Dancing Hut]], we see smoke from afar. As we hurry towards it, it's clear the thing has been set ablaze and is now running around, igniting other nearby sources of flammable materials.

[[Arinya]] casts Icy Storm to douse the flames and stop the chicken hut, which promptly collapses under the combined damage.

Arinya believes repairing it might be similar magic to that of Golem production, so we set out to find a wizard that can produce Golems.

He advises to start of with finding a carpenter that's able to repair the physical damage first.

We decide to look into this tomorrow and go the [[Demon and Toad]] for some rest.
