[Location:: [[Collegento]]]
After the usual ritual of nourishment, we're off to the library to see if we can find tactical information to prepare us for battle with Giants.

We find there are at least 6 subtypes in the region:
Hill Giant: Simple minded, selfish and aggressive

Fire Giant: Militaristic, evil and aggressive. Immune to fire damage.

Stone Giant: Stealthy in their own environment, very good at throwing stones. They live reclusive lives.

Frost Giant: Immune to cold damage. Likely not very hospitable.

Cloud Giant: Most relatable to humans as far as character goes, some are good, some are bad.

Storm Giant: Mostly friendly or at least reasonable types, guardians of sea and water creatures.


At noon, we eat lunch in a tavern called [[Crossed Wands]], a more up-scale place.

After that we make our way to the northern market where all the various temples and cathedrals are located. The temple of Sarenrae is our target, where [[Camedyr Baughan]] offers another bodyguard job.

He says there are reports of strange lights that are coming from Nivemer's Stand, along with increased activity, worrying people that something was going to happen. Camedyr volunteered to investigate, but he wants a group of bodyguards to keep him safe.

At our request, Camedyr also gives us a little more info about Nivemera.

Since we'll be gone at least a fortnight, he does say that he'll go in sooner if he finds another party that can take him.
I advise him to take a beacon of some sort, so we can look for him should he be forced to go in before our return.

We end the night with dinner in [[Crossed Wands]] and sleep in the [[Jester and Mug]].
