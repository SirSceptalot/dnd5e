[Location: [[Heidelburg]]]
At dawn, we leave [[Iglberg]] and head for [[Heidelburg]], arriving there without issues shortly after midday.

The notice board, prominently present near the main gate, immediately draws our attention.
Being the subtle man he is, [[Tiziano]] grabs all the notes from the boards and returns to our cart to read them.

Following the signposts to the centre for administration, we meet once more with [[Emil]] and are given the promised note for a discount at the armoury [[Forging Ahead]].

[[Tiziano]] produces one of the notices he took from the board regarding an asylum for [[Pseudodragon|Pseudodragons]] and asks if he can get a discount there as well. Surprisingly, he adds that business to the document too.

Tiziano is intrigued by the [[Heidelburg's redneck bandits|job offering regarding a farm that was taken over by bandits]], and we're given some rudimentary intelligence.

Our next stop is [[Forging Ahead]], where Arinya acquires a studded leather armour with her own money.
The owner, [[Frey Goldhammer]], makes no issue of the discount promised to us by the voucher from Emil. Perhaps they've a deal with the city for reduced taxes in cases such as this?

After outfitting Arinya in her new armour, we make our way to [[Fizzy Flippy Frost's Fantastic Fascinations]], where we're greeted by [[Fizzy Flippy Frost]], an enthusiastic female gnome with electric blue hair.

There are a number of interesting magical items on display there, most of them solidly out of our price range at the moment.

The [[Pseudodragon]] sanctuary, [[Sunshine Sanctuary for Wayward Dragons]], is our next stop.
Located in a large mansion, this looks to be the work of a person or family that took it upon themselves to save these cat-sized dragons.

There is a sulfuric odour inside, where we find a number of the creatures playing around, although they all have their stinger wrapped or corked.

Tiziano has a short conversation with a lady that tends to the magical beasts and is soon playing with one of the creatures, as is Arinya.

It doesn't take long for both to be convinced and pay a small sum to adopt one each. They both seem honest in their intentions to take care of them.
The one Arinya got is named [[Fiona]], the other one is [[Spyro|Sir Walter the Third]], but Tiziano said he might rename him.

After seeing how expensive adventuring life is, Arinya decides that she would like to press [[Emil]] for a bigger reward for [[Heidelburg's redneck bandits|clearing the bandits from the farm]], so back to the administration office we go.

The reward is subsequently negotiated from 500 to 750 gold coins, provided we proceed to fulfill at least one extra job of our choosing for the city.

We're told there were no survivors, but a milkman might have seen the bandits, so we go to visit him next.
[[Randal]], the milkman, is only able to provide very rudimentary information on their armaments, which at least include crossbows.

From there we go to the vicinity of the farm itself to scout before attacking - we heard rumours that there might be many bandits. After Tiziano puts one of his most useful skills to work, morphing into an animal of his choosing, he scouts the place and finds there are a few lookouts in front and back. Their sides are not defended, but they are not spread out enough for this to make a big difference.

There is a patrol of five men that may make a good first target to diminish their numbers 

My companions want to get more participants before commencing the siege, which we try to find at an inn ([[The Singed Hare]]) where a brawl tournament is scheduled to take place that evening.

The inn is run by the elf [[Eliorod]].

The girl and the turtle spend what we have left of the evening devising a plan to get some of the fighters to join us in battle, after which they get a room and I perform sentry duty.
