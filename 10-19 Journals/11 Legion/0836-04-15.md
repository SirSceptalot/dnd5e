En route to the [[Eternal Forest]].
[[Tiziano]] uses the [[Orb of Scrying]] to find the [[Lady of Vines]].
He can see a "person" not of flesh and blood, but bark, wood and sap.
She's sat atop a giant tree that's grown in the shape of a throne.
Various creatures are milling about near her, making offerings and the likes.
Other creatures come spawning from the trees in the vicinity, in what looks like teleportation. ^96e356

After this, he uses the [[Find the path]] spell to guide us in the right direction. Tizi helps the crew of [[Bastion (Ship)]] fine-tune the direction of the ship.

He estimates it to be about 6 days travel with [[Bastion (Ship)]] at the current pace.

Today is also the first day of the modification to the ship's propulsion system by [[Magister Gribolt]].

While we travel, [[Tiziano]] and [[Arinya]] work on the [[Belt of Fire Giant Strength]] to replace the [[Belt of Hill Giant Strength]] that I currently use.

I create a [[Potion of Protection from Evil and Good]], that I keep on me.

During the afternoon, [[Verras]] sends me a message with the information that [[Weiss]] can't find a way to release the warforged from a distance.

My response:
> I would appreciate if they can keep trying. Perhaps they might find a way to use us as a proxy?

I hear nothing back, but suddenly a very confused looking [[Weiss]] appears near me.

After some initial confusion she tells us that the war isn't going extremely well, as many of the archmages are reluctant to deploy the [[Warforged]].

We give her the rundown of what we've done and accomplished so far.
In regard to the [[Orb of Souls||Orbs of Souls]], [[Weiss]] thinks she might be able to overrule them in a radius of 60 feet around her.

She'll also try to work on a way for us to release the warforged without them being in her radius of influence.
