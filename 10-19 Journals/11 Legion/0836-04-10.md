[Location:: aboard the [[Dauntless Explorer]]]
[Mission:: ]

The day is again spent helping Tizi with his crafting.

We make it to the island as it's painted in a beautiful red sunset.
There's a single lantern on the beach, but other than that we see no illumination on the island.

[[Tiziano]] objects when I propose taking the sloop to shore since he thinks there might be some of those hostile creatures in the water. Instead, he proposes to fly us in his giant eagle shape.
He can't carry the lot of us, though, so he'll carry [[Arinya]] and [[Geoffry]] while I ride in the [[Portable Hole]].

When I emerge from the hole, [[Arinya]] is arguing with [[Tiziano]] about the death of [[Geoffry]].
It seems Tizi pulled an **INCREDIBLY** stupid stunt which ended with [[Geoffry]] landing in the water, where the creatures there killed him instantly.

Also present is another [[Tortle]] that introduces himself as [[Salvatore]]. He claims to be sent to guide us to the monastery, by a being that cares about cosmic balance.

He takes us through the path in the cliffs to a ruined gate that has clearly seen battle recently.
Clearly a defensive chokepoint, where we're attacked by the abominations Geoffry was talking about.

After fighting them, we have some time to inspect them closer, and can see some of these are actually [[Sahaugin]], others are transformed humans.
These are creatures that can live both on land or in water and are naturally quite aggressive.

We take 4 javelins and one breastplate from their corpses.
After a short rest, we continue along the path (which is actually quite lovely, there's some type of marble in the rock walls).

A little further we make it to more open grounds with lovely pastures. It looks like there should be cattle grazing, but the place seems eerily void of large life.

Further, we happen upon a tiny village that looks eerily quiet.
We enter the center of the settlement ready for combat. We need not wait long.

A single figure rushes us and right into a world of pain. He's swiftly joined by a horde of his brethren though, but they all meet the same end.

[[Salvatore]] took quite a beating, but everyone lives.

There's nothing to be found on the bodies.
In one of the houses, [[Tiziano]] finds a hatch that leads to a room with an altar, upon which flesh lies rotting. [[Arinya]] thinks the symbols in the room are from a lost deity, but she can't put her finger on which one.

She goes to touch the altar in order to identify it and sees the symbol of Yig, father of serpents and worms and that which crawls.
She goes blank for a second. When she opens her eyes, one of them is yellow and serpentine.

She blanks out again, and says she contacted Illion. He had an intense struggle with the unknown deity for control, but lost, causing the deity to take over Arinya's eye.
He has no idea what consequences this will have.

It's an elder god though, so it might be quite bad.
Illion advises us to seek further information in Aurocondor.

During her conversation with Illion, he also said he'd try to keep an eye on Salvatore.
Aurocondor isn't particularly his domain, so his power might be quite limited there.

He advises us take care around the city Eternicum, as vampirism runs rampant there. Blood is the main currency for the residents. Tiziano should talk to the trees outside the city.

Illion was in aurocondor when the gods fell, even took part in the shattering of a few.
Depending on who you mention his name to, you may be greeted with a warm welcome or snarls.

The following is a list of items and their location, which Illion believes Hadak will be after.

| Item                       | Suspected location |
| -------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Shard of Neru              | Midlands           |
| Shard of Morrigan          | Dusk meadow, Aurocondor |
| Shard of the Lady of Vines | Eternal forest, Aurocondor |
| Shard of the Whisperer     | Bibliotheca Aeterna, Aurocondor |
| enir tiozara(?)            | quintus mons(?), Aurocondor |
| TSUKAHARA bokuden       | Location unknown |

Asking the locals is a good way to start searching for the items.
There's also a "search path" spell that might be of use.

Illion suggests not fighting him directly, but going around him.
He's going to be blunt - go in, destroy everything and take what he wants.

We could try to either take it from under his nose or take whatever he's going to go after next while he's focused on one thing.

Ideally, we would just take every item before he gets to it, but given his head start, this sounds optimistic.

After [[Arinya]] is done recounting this tale, she sets up the tiny hut.

While everyone is quiet, [[Salvatore]] suddenly says "Hello Legion, now you can listen too."
No idea what that was about. It seems like a conversation was underway, but I missed it somehow.

Either way, he says he knows Master Sliver - [[Tiziano]]'s master - and that he is called a deserter and other unpleasant names. He apparently had a duty, but instead he ran off with a special bottle of whiskey.
It turns out Tizi's brothers are also on Aurocondor.

Salvatore also divulges the name of his employer: Yog-Sothoth.
It is the parent of "The Child" which we killed a while back. It knows we did this, but doesn't seem too bothered by it. Old gods and their incomprehensible ways and all that.

We decide to rest for the night after this conversation.
