[Location:: [[Collegento]]]
[Mission:: [[Liberate Collegento]]]

Early the next morning, we head out to [[Nivemer's Stand]] to begin our missions.
We're able to avoid detection for a while from hostiles, including a [[Bearded Devil]] patrol.

A little while later there's little chance to avoid confrontation and we fight an [[Abishai]], a [[Merregon]] and 2 other creatures.

More battle and chaos ensues. At a certain point, the horn of our other crew sounds and we make our way to the [[Temple of Asmodeus in Collegento]] to join the assault.

During the fight an magical infused ballista arrow strikes [[Bastion]], turning him into a devil that the [[Erathis]] proudly introduces as [[Hadak]].

It soon becomes clear this creature is by far the most powerful entity on the battlefield, and the tide turns overwhelmingly against us.
Almost immediately, [[Hadak]] is able to control the minds of most of the [[Warforged]] present, including [[Render]], [[Reckoning]] and for a short moment, myself.

A number of us manage - or to be honest, are allowed to - to retreat, while [[Hadak]] tells us to send the message to [[Amaroth]] that he's coming for the rest of the [[Warforged]] population.

The mood is grim back at the camp. Nobody knows anything about this new devil, although everyone agrees that his power was overwhelming.

The [[Judicators]] invite us to join them to Esterna to discuss matters, and hopefully continue cooperation between our 2 nations.

I relay the information to Paragon via sending stones and am given freedom to travel with the [[Judicators]], while Amaroth will prepare for the situation as best they can.

During these discussions and licking of our wounds, a messenger comes bearing the news that it seems the city has been abandoned by the devils.
[[Arinya]] scries on [[Render]], and can confirm that he's on a ship.

They're talking about Magister Gribolt. Note added later: in hindsight, we should have taken this into account.

Suddenly, [[Hadak]] looks straight at the scrying point and says "Ah, we have a spy. Who are you?", confiming this devil has truesight.

After all this, [[Arinya]] informs us that she has set up a meeting tonight with Illion. It turns out, the reason why I was only briefly dominated by Hadak was due to his intervention.

As we meet him, he invites us to have a drink in his realm.
As we go through the portal he creates, it turns out his realm contains a classy bar.
[[Illion]] awaits us there, looking comfortable in a very classy suit.

After and among pleasantries, we discuss ways to prevent Hadak from dominating other Warforged.
[[Illion]] was able to free me because my soul was already under contract with Asmodeus, so we discuss the minimum contract required for this, and the option of extending this to all Warforged.

In return he asks for:
2 empty warforged shells to house the souls of his servants
1 building, large enough for a smith shop and bar
Maintenance personnel (maybe warforged) for the building
A chance of being a weapons dealer for Paragon.

After the negotiations, we talk a bit about Hadak his possible plans, and finally some small talk.

Before the small talk, the most useful bit of information [[Illion]] can give us is that the Erinyes that supports Hadak is called [[Tisiphone]].

Start of the mission [[Defeat Hadak]].
