[Location:: [[Collegento]]]
[[Isabel]] leaves us during the course of the day when we're near her hometown of [[Lawold]], as we travel the last stretch to [[Collegento]]. I try learning some more Aquan, but have made little progress by the time we arrive at the city from the south at dusk.

The southern shanty towns has a palisade enclosing it completely, with even the roads blocked off. We go around it to enter the city as indicated by Ephemera.
The walled off shantytown is called [[Nivemer's Stand]]. In the early days of the war, a powerful cleric - [[Nivemera]] - took her own life and the shantytown in order to create a magical effect to ward off the advancing army from Skogsland.

Her plan worked, but the area now seems to be permanently haunted. The palisade was built both to keep people out and other things in.

The city itself seems very lively, and with quite a diverse gathering.
A band of [[Goliath|Goliaths]] are the most striking group here, hauling big barrels towards the city.

Ephemera recommends a few inns: [[Witch and Mace]], or the [[Jester and Mug]].
The latter is still descent but somewhat more affordable, our local guide says, so we go there.

We pay for four meals ([[Arinya]], [[Tiziano]], [[Ephemera]] and [[Valencia]]) and a room as the rain starts to fall.
