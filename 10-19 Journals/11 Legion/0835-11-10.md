[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Restore productivity of Avelund mines]]]
[Mission:: [[Safe container for Nivemera]]]
[Mission:: [[Rodents of unusual size found in warehouse]]]

I check in with [[Paragon]] for a debriefing, while Arinya and Tiziano are having breakfast. There is much more activity than expected, with even construction going on to implement extra offices and the likes. Reckoning tells me that the Titan project is being suddenly approved out of the blue.

The Titan project was something being discussed towards the end of the war, but was canceled when the truce was arranged. The goal was to create far larger [[Warforged]] with a more straightforward approach to combat.

The approval of the project at this point is extremely strange, but not for me to question.
It looks like they're still planning the construction for the additional assembly lines right now, so it's unknown when the first units will be created.

After returning to the Demon and Toad to pick up my partners, we head over to [[Balthazar Gelt]] to see if he was able to complete [[Nivemera]]'s new home in a pendant for Tiziano to carry - he has.
Received item: [[Nivemera's Locket]].

Back in town we sell a few things we picked up along the way that aren't of much use to us anymore: the books [[Hater of Life]] and the journal of a mining crew.

Before sunset we go back to [[Paragon]] to ask if there are any new orders for me - there aren't.
They'll let me know if they'll find Behektur so we can go hunt him down. 

We have a look at the notice board to see if there are any interesting jobs in town.

There are 6 jobs that can earn us some coin. While the gold itself is of little interest, I must admit that it permits us to upgrade our equipment and be better at protecting the innocent.

1. 100 Gold - Test subjects wanted for magic items in Heidelburg.
2. 150 Gold - Rodents of unusual size found in warehouse.
3. 750 Gold - In search of mage to dispel chaos portal that keep spitting out elementals
4. 1000 Gold - Investigators needed for disappearances in Iglberg.
5. 1500 Gold - Mercenaries needed to investigate a possible conspiracy in Collegento.
6. 3000 Gold - Mercenaries wanted to protect an expedition to islands in the north.

We start off with the second job, since it sounds rather quick and easy.
New mission: [[Rodents of unusual size found in warehouse]].

The client's name is [[Angus Voorhees]], a man whose warehouse in [[Lower Amaroth]] is plagued by giant rats. An employee called Henry is missing.

After dispatching the [[Giant Rat|giant rats]] - that attacked us on sight as soon as we entered - there still remained a very aggressive [[Wererat]] in the room, trapped in [[Tiziano]]'s web spell.
Reasoning with it was not an option sadly, so it had to go.

Behind it was a tunnel, down which a number of cages were situated that were mostly empty. One held a man prison that matches the description of [[Henry]], the missing employee of [[Angus Voorhees]].

From the looks of it he has been bitten and will turn permanently on the next full moon.
It has just been full moon last night however, so unless he has been bitten very recently this man may be out of luck.

We're paid the promised 150 gold coins and take [[Henry]] to the [[University in Amaroth]], where they will try to help him as best they can.

We'll sleep in the [[Demon and Toad]] tonight, and visit Aldor Vandren tomorrow for the next Quest: Dispel elemental emitting portals.

We end the night on a light note, playing some darts.
