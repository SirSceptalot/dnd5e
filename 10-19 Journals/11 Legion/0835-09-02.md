[Location:: [[Ermedes]]]
After breakfast in the castle, we make for the [[Band of the Hawk]] to ask if they would be willing to lend their [[Hammer Squadron]] to help [[Destroy the Black Marauders]]. We're taken to the leader of the group, [[Griffith]].

The rumours about this group disliking the [[Black Marauders]] seems true, as he pledges his [[Hammer Squadron]] free of charge.

Next, we go to [[Jarnbrun]]. They also seem to have a strong dislike for the Black Marauders, but are not willing to risk their necks without payment.
They have 10 berserkers and 5 archers to spare for this battle.

Their leader, [[Ardan Aegersson]], is on good terms with the leader of the [[Band of the Red Hand]], [[Matrim Cauthon]], and would like to discuss it together with him to facilitate negotiations, so we head over to their camp together.

Matrim Cauthon is willing to expend one siege weapon - a Scorpion and 10 pikemen for this battle.

Since the camps are close together, the leaders suggest commencing the attack as soon as possible, before the Black Marauders can spot troop movements.

We have a brief discussion regarding strategy, we initiate combat which soon resulted in a resounding victory on our part.
The Black Marauders were not without their merit though, and at least they had the honour to fight to the last man.
All parties behaved themselves admirably, but none went above and beyond like the Hammer Squadron did.

They held back a much larger force at a chokepoint, and were instrumental in our victory.

After the battle, [[Tiziano]] and [[Legion|I]] help tend the wounded while [[Arinya]] oversees the correct distribution of the loot, our share of which amounts to 1800 gold coins, the carriage with two horses, and three potions: Fire Breath, Growth and Greater Healing.

After my companions take a short rest, we advance to the Swift Finish to deal with any visiting Black Marauders and with the owner.

When the owner is confronted by Arinya while Tiziano and I guard the exits, he calls upon some hellish pact, after which an [[Erinyes]] and a few bearded devils appear and try to save the man.
They fail, although the Erinyes manages to escape.

The slave girls are grateful for their freedom, and two of them, [[Valencia]] and [[Isabel]] accept our offer to take them along to [[Ephemera's village]].
The girls share some of their gold with Arinya Vallin, who gives it to me for safe keeping.

I drag the corpse of the former brothel owner with me back to the battle field, where we load it into our carriage along with the corpse of [[Rassler Creighton]].

We then head to [[Hydra's Tavern]] along with the other surviving warriors, where an evening of merry making takes place.
Under loud cheers I tell the glorious tale of how the [[Hammer Squadron]] stood their ground, shields aligned, against an overwhelming force.

When the evening ends, I need to load an inebriated Tiziano and Arinya into the cart, joined by the two girls. Tiziano explains a few of the strange customs some people have in regards to ineffective procreation.

We spend the night in the castle.
