[Location:: aboard the [[Dauntless Explorer]]]
[Mission:: ]

During the day, the [[Orc]] ship gets close enough that a battle ensues.
I deliver on my backlog for the [[12 souls for Asmodeus]], one for March and one for April.

We board their ship after defeating them all, and encounter 2 goblins. Of of them attacks me while the other seems unimpressed and rather peaceful in a "I'm not risking my neck for anyone" kind of way.
I put the little guy in a bag to calm down, and tell them that they can keep working until we reach land, at which point they may disembark or apply to become part of the regular crew.

We find a descent sum of coins, a Vial of Lightening, a letter addressed "to my sweetheart" that has an explosive glyph of warding on it, a number of mundane items, and finally: a magical deck of cards.
