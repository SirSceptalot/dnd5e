Going back to the battlefield from the night before, [[Tiziano]] is able to follow the tracks of the retreating zombies through the swamp, although it's a very hard trek.

We come to a clearing at some point where we're mesmerized and subsequently attacked by a [[Noon Wraith]] and some [[Willow Wisp|Willow Wisps]].

We take a short rest after this and carry on.

The Tiny Hut is once again summoned by [[Arinya]] for our nightly rest.

During the late evening, a beautiful young woman comes staring at the dome.
He skin is pale as milk and her eyes black as night. She's obviously not human.

We have a friendly discussion with her, in which she divulges that the odd behavior of the zombies coincided with the disappearance of a certain undead, a "zombie lord" or something.

She's annoyed by their behavior as well, and gladly tells us where the ruins are where  he was last seen.

After the woman leaves, [[Arinya]] divulges that she recognizes this creature from books, and it's a [[Bruxa]]
