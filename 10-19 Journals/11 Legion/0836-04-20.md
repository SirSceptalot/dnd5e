---

We're still on [[Bastion]], our air ship, and can see the [[Eternal Forest]] on the horizon.

I try to help [[Weiss]] again with her magical project of releasing [[Warforged]] from their mind control.
[[Tiziano]] tries to scry on [[Reckoning]], which fails.

After this he tries to scry on [[Hadak's Horned Devil]]. He hangs nailed to the wall, full of wounds which weren't delivered by us. He's clearly been severely punished for letting the ship fall into our hands. ^d53ee2

[[Tiziano]] can hear [[Hadak]] talking logistics with what he assumes is a local warlord.
He can hear [[Hadak]] say "*For once, we're the ones that need to hurry*".
It looks like there will be another march of a vast army. From the looks of it, whatever culling we did to his army will have been nullified by the - likely forceful - recruitments he's done here.

[[Tiziano]] estimates that he'll need at least a week to make it to his next target, [[Anaire Tyualzara]].

We discuss the option of sending a warning so they can be prepared for Hadak's coming. After some planning, we decide to send [[Edgar]], [[Arinya|Arinya's]] familiar with a letter and the instructions to issue a verbal warning to the same effect.

The remainder of the day for [[Tiziano]] and [[Arinya]] is spent working on the [[Belt of Fire Giant Strength]].

Suddenly, we're attacked by a bunch of [[Gossamid Archer|Gossamid Archers]].

## Round 1
Spells targeting their mental faculties (wisdom) seem to be quite effective, as Arinya makes them drop their bows. They're quite resistant to bludgeoning damage though.

The other Warforged operate the ballista or throw javelins.

## Round 2

I cast bless on Arinya, Tizi and myself, and move towards the fool that made his way on the ship. Arinya casts Scroching Ray and hits with 5 out of 6 rays on a single target, killing it in the process. The fire seems to have a very significant effect on them.

[[Tiziano]] continues his battle in his air elemental shape.

Our Warforged soldiers reload and fire the ballista again.

## Round 3

The [[Gossamid Archer]]s dive for their dropped bows. I try to use the diplomatic approach with the one that made it to the deck of our ship, but he's having none of it.

Arinya attempts to turn the battle with her Toll the Dead spell, but has no luck. Tiziano is well on his way to defeat his foe.

## Round 4

The archer on the deck, that I tried to talk to, shoots two arrows at me. I manage to evade one, and let met shield deflect the second.

I try to close the distance to him, but he flies out of reach. [[Arinya]] takes advantage of this to hit him with [[Eldritch Blast]].

Our Warforged allies try to aim the ballista, but they don't have a clear shot.

## Round 5

My opponent shoots at my feet while still remaining airborn out of my reach, and chuckles as I jump to evade the arrow.

[[Arinya]] tries to end the battle now with diplomacy, but our enemies are having none of it.

[[Tiziano]] is still wrestling his opponent for control over the bow.

## Round 6
The other archers that dove after their bows have returned and rejoin the battle. One of them is immediately struck by a ballista arrows, as the warforged have been poised to take a shot.
Another manages to shoot at, and hit, Arinya. The one fighting [[Tiziano]] also manages to land a few good punches, but [[Tiziano]], still in Air Elemental shapeshift, ignores the damage in order to fly away with his opponent's bow.

Meanwhile, I'm still unable to reach my flying opponent so I use my bulk, shield and armour to protect [[Arinya]] from incoming arrows. [[Arinya]], on her turn, casts [[Fireball]] to great effect, managing to kill two of our opponents.

Two are left breathing.

## Round 7

Despite my armoured body being in front of [[Arinya]], the other manages to flank her and hit her with an arrow. I use my Lay On Hands to heal her, and sheathe my axe, as I'm still unable to reach our flying opponents anyway.

Arinya takes revenge by killing the archer that hit her with her [[Eldritch Blast]].
One opponent left.


## Round 8
[[Tiziano]] manages to reach me, and tosses the bow he took from his opponent.
I successfully taunt our one remaining enemy with it, let him take it from my hand but grab him by the throat before he can fly away with it. He tries to free himself, but is hopelessly underpowered.

I drag him kicking and screaming into the brig, and unceremoniously toss him in.
He has a little critter with him that's keeping an eye on us.

After recovering and having everyone calm down from the adrenaline rush, we start a friendly interrogation.
He says they attacked us because we were trespassing, nobody is allowed to fly over, or enter, the [[Eternal Forest]] without their permission. We're able to gain some trust and convince him that we have no interest in hurting the forest. We give him his bow back, I give some food to his little critter, and we set him free without any further hostilities.

After all this, we start thinking of how we can gain the favor of the Tree Lords.
Without much of a plan, we descend to the forest below, where [[Tiziano]] casts [[Speak with Plants]], to talk to a willow tree. He asks what we can do to help help. The willow says his roots are a bit twisted, so Tizi summons a small group of badgers to start digging. I help digging, and rearrange the roots once they've been exposed, and cover them up again after that.

We don't really have any more ideas after that, so we go back to the air ship and resolve to figure something out tomorrow.
