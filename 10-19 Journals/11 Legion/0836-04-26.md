## A Cautious Communion

[[Arinya]] initiated contact with [[Illion]] through dreams, locating him amidst the familiar surroundings of his bar. Illion’s insights were both sobering and pragmatic:

1. **The Agaros Threat**: He described the impending battle with the [[Agaros]] as a test of unparalleled severity—one that would strain every resource at our disposal.
    
2. **On the Temptation of Magic**: Within [[Mors Magicae]], the profusion of magical energy invites overindulgence. Illion advised restraint, cautioning that while extraordinary feats could be achieved, the physical toll would be severe. In practical terms, it appears that exceeding one’s usual spellcasting limits is possible, but at the expense of health.
    
3. **Characteristics of the Agaros**: These entities are not merely adversaries of brute strength and speed but are also intelligent, capable of negotiation. However, this particular Agaros bears an understandable resentment, having once been impaled by the [[Holy Avenger]]. They possess a unique ability to dispel magical effects upon themselves, though Illion noted this power is sparingly used due to its cost.
    
4. **Interdimensional Escape**: A final troubling revelation was the creature’s capacity for interdimensional travel, complicating any plans to constrain it.
    

Illion’s warning was tinged with a broader concern—the [[New War]] continues to exact a heavy toll on [[Skogsland]], a reality that frames every decision we make.

## **Crafting and Tactical Preparations**

The day continued with measured productivity. While [[Tiziano]] and I collaborated on refining the [[Belt of Fire Giant Strength]], [[Arinya]] dedicated her focus to producing another potion of healing—a testament to our shared diligence. A break in our labors led to an enlightening conversation with [[Magister Gribolt]].

### **On Airship Armament**

Magister Gribolt outlined an ambitious proposal to enhance the offensive capabilities of our airship. The plan hinges on acquiring large magical crystals from [[Mors Magicae]] to construct artillery cannons. His specifications were as follows:

- Effective range: 600 feet, with projectiles extending up to 2,400 feet.
    
- Damage potential: 8d10, utilizing either lightning or force energy, dependent upon available resources and crafting time.
    

This innovation promises considerable tactical advantage, though its feasibility remains contingent on resource acquisition.

## **Reconnaissance through Divination**

Once again, [[Arinya]] employed her [[Scrying]] abilities, targeting [[Hadak’s Horned Devil]]. Her arcane sensor revealed the creature within a cavern, engaged in an inaudible conversation with [[Hadak]] and [[Anaire Tyualzara]]. While the visual details were clear, the distance rendered their dialogue indecipherable—a vexing limitation.

Later, [[Arinya]] resolved to cast [[Contact Other Plane]] to seek counsel from the enigmatic [[Yig]]. Recognizing the risks inherent in such magic, [[Tiziano]] and I stood ready to provide immediate assistance.

### **Questions and Answers from Yig**

Our inquiries were precise, reflecting the gravity of our situation:

1. Is there any way to remove the Holy Avenger from the body of the Agaros while keeping it incapacitated?
    
2. If defeated in battle, how can the Agaros be permanently neutralized?
    
3. Is indefinite imprisonment of the Agaros possible?
    
4. If we cast Banishment while holding the Holy Avenger, will the weapon remain in our possession?
    
5. Can the Agaros be slain while the Holy Avenger restrains it?
    

The responses were terse but illuminating:

1. No.
    
2. With extreme violence.
    
3. Yes.
    
4. Yes.
    
5. Yes.
    

In summation:

- Removal of the [[Holy Avenger]] without releasing the Agaros is unattainable.
    
- To truly defeat the Agaros, we must employ unrestrained force.
    
- Imprisonment remains a viable option.
    
- Banishment tactics can secure the weapon.
    
- Killing the Agaros is feasible under the Holy Avenger’s restraint.
