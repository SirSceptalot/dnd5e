[Location:: [[Avelund mines]]]
[Mission:: [[Restore productivity of Avelund mines]]]

Shortly after seeing [[Jericho]] pass by in the direction of the city, we get going towards the mines again.

Once there, we're happy to see no guards at the entrance, although we do find three [[Bearded Devil|Bearded Devils]] inside.
This isn't the first time fighting them, and we don't have any big issues in dispatching with them.

The next room holds a far more unpleasant surprise though, as three black tar-like substances animate and attack us.
When I slice one with my sword, it cuts cleanly in half but leaves us with two smaller opponents instead of the one large unit that I cut.

I nearly die twice during this fight, my companions are much more effective at fighting these from a distance that I am up close. The fact that they are severely corrosive doesn't help either, as my Paragon issued war hammer dissolves after a few strikes at the creature. Luckily, my magical armour and shield seem immune to this effect.

After scouting around a bit more, we can see that the mines are riddled with [[Kobold|Kobolds]], little humanoid-draconic creatures.

They won't be much of help to the mining operation either, but they're far from the cause.

A short rest later, we carry on and stumble upon a few drunken [[Exaltators]]. They were easily take care of, on account of their drunken state.
Arinya insist I take along three bottles of whiskey from that room.

We leave the weapons and shields here, since we can't exactly carry them with us.
The armours are useless anyway.

We do find a bit of gold and 6 vials of blood. Arinya can determine half are celestial, the other half are draconic blood.
After this room, we follow a set of stairs down to a double door. Beyond it is a [[Barbed Devil]] and 2 [[Merregon]].
After fighting them, we see a mirror in the room beyond them, which was used to trap a [[Deva]] in there. Their source of divine blood is immediately apparent.
Not sure where they get the draconic blood from though.

We don't immediately have a way to free the Deva from his prison, so we go back through the mines in search of clues.

Sadly, no clues present themselves, although we do find the following potions:
Foxes' cunning
Owl Wisdom
Invisibility
Alter Self
Fly
Another vial sadly got destroyed in the process of opening it's container.

Besides these, we also find an almost completed scroll of Summon Celestial Spirit and the recipe for [[Ambrosia]].

Finding no clues, we go back to the mirror for a brainstorming session.
The angel can't produce noise through the mirror, but with a large amount of gesticulating we manage to find out that the mirror could be unlocked with a code word, which turned out to be "apple" spoken in Infernal.

The angel is [[Viantius]], Deva of Erathis.

The mirror itself is still intact.

Since we already have the ingredients and the recipe, Arinya decides to brew a batch of [[Ambrosia]].

After this, we take the night rest in Arinya's tiny hut which is set up in the cave.
