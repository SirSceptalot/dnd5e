[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: ]

Despite the other shops we've already done, we still need a new [[Javelin of Lightning]], and finally find one here in [[Amaroth]] for 1500 gold.

Near noon, a message reaches us in which we're informed that Paragon has managed to restore the captured Warforged to a loyal state.
Our presence is requested in Verras' tower to join his debriefing.

In short: Hadak is eastbound, to a new continent. Before that, he wants to go to the dragon island where the ruling dragon was supposedly killed by a younger red dragon.

Hadak wants to take the body of the old dragon and turn it into some hybrid undead/mechanical creature to aid him.

[[Verras]] chimes in that this "new continent" is likely Aurocondor, the place where all life started. It was lost in it's own dimension that retracted under the ocean, but has recently risen out of the water again - although nobody knows why.

In front of it is an island with guardians to prevent creatures from entering or escaping from Aurocondor.

The debriefing also teaches us that Render spent a lot of time with Hadak, while Reckoning was sent out on missions more often, mostly to search for artifacts and other useful materials. He usually goes out with a few Warforged and a few Gunners.

We ponder if it might be a good idea to start rumors of an artifact at a location where we can set a trap for Reckoning.

The guardian island has a building and organization called the Sacrosanct Monastery, from which the defense of Aurocondor is led.

Verrass lectures us about the creation of devils. See attached: [[info on creation of devils]].

Before leaving, Verras hands [[Tiziano]] his [[Cloak of Displacement]] which he requested earlier.

We go to the library after this, where I try to find some information on the Sacrosanct Monastery. I've noted this information on the relevant page in this journal.

Tiziano comes back with a wealth of information on Erinyes, which might give us an edge next time we fight Tisiphone.

[[Arinya]] took this time to search for Elterion. She combed the entire library in a very efficient manner, but found only little information.

Most of what she found was a thesis by a student called Eribus. She asked the librarian if it was possible to contact this person, which she said she would try.

there is some tomfoolery during the evening with plant growth by Tiziano, after which we have another barbecue and spend the night in the [[Dancing Hut]].
