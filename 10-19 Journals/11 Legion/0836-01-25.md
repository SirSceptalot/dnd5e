[Location:: [[Avandar]]]
[Mission:: [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Galguth]]]

We arrive in [[Avandar]] late afternoon, where we park our [[Dancing Hut]] at the gates.
We soon hear of trouble in the [[Tellery]] district, where a huge frog creature is attacking and kidnapping people. This sounds sucpicously like a creature we once inadvertently released.

We enter the city and make our way to the [[Vanyarin]] district, where Faynaigen should be awaiting us.

We're lead to a magnificent study room in the [[Grand Library of Avandar]] where an elf named [[Glorandal]] tells us we may relinquish the [[Bell of Saint Solar Macharius]] to his care.

I refuse, since we have specific orders to give it directly to Faynaigen.

After some grumbling and a short wait, [[Faynaigen]] herself does appear, and we move the bell to her custody.

We leave her with her new toy, and go ask the guards about the kidnappings.
They lead us to a female officer called [[Elanil]].

Due to political reasons they can't officially hire outsiders in any capacity, but we're allowed to roam where we please from now on, and we might just accidentally find guard there that would obviously fight any evil they see.

It's been about 4 days now since the first attacks.

We decide to go to the waterfront at the crack of dawn tomorrow, where we should normally soldiers already patrolling.

We spend our night in the [[Cracked Goblet]], where we're greeted by the innkeeper [[Seldana]].
