[Location:: [[Avandar]]]
During the night, a few Gnolls manage to sneak up to the camp and attack Arinya behind my back.
In hindsight, this was generally considered a bad move as the offending [[Gnoll]] had e remaining lifespan of only a few painful seconds.

After resuming rest and traveling the rest of the day afterwards, we manage to make it to [[Avandar]] before nightfall.

When we are once again greeted by a guard, [[Rizerin]] sends them off by saying we are guests of house [[Yushrish]].

We enter the [[Vanyarin]] district but immediately make our way to the [[Alfarin]] district, taking one of the Goliath Spiders down to a meeting between Rizerin and his handler.

The tunnels are quite dark, indicating what we all know: [[Drow]] have excellent dark vision and don't need the amount of light other species do.

We are led to an expensive looking chamber where a female dark [[Elf]] lies in an ornate lounge chair.

Rizerin addresses the woman in a very submissive tone, in a language we don't understand.
After a brief conversation, she turns to us and addresses Arinya, apparently used to a matriarchal society.

She introduces herself as [[Rahode Yushrish]], thanks us, pays us the agreed upon 2000 gold coins and offers us more employment.
The Morthïr are looking to bind a spirit of nature, a creature of primordial chaos, to a stone, in order to force it to do their bidding or use it as a weapon.
They have little knowledge on the creature itself, not even the appearance is known.

We decide to spend the night in the [[Gypsy's lodge]], and have a short conversation with the owner [[Anold]] about the Morthïr, who he describes as a royal nuisance. The name of their leader is Vollon, and they reside in the Sindarin distict.
