[Location:: [[Velgend]]]
[Mission:: [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]]]

Departure day.
Upon arriving in the [[Dancing Hut]], we find a recently deceased individual inside.
There's an arrow through his heart, and many wounds of animal origin, many post-mortem.
[[Arinya]] recognizes this person as [[Nathyr]].

The animal wounds are fake though, it baffles me why someone would go to the trouble of faking an animal attack if they're going to just leave an arrow impaled in the victim's heart.

[[Tiziano]] finds tracks leading to and away from our Dancing Hut, and we follow them.
It leads many miles and many hours away to a camp populated by several elves, at least a few of them are likely [[Eladrin]].
[[Fiona]] and [[Spyro|Spyro]] do reconnaissance but are spotted. They fire a warning shot, but don't shoot to kill.

We go over and have a conversation with them.
Their leader is a woman called [[Valante]], and she explains how they were the party of [[Morthïr]] that were originally supposed to escort us on this mission. They freely admit to killing our previous escort [[Nathyr]], as it was seemingly an insult to be refused on such a high profile mission.
We see no reason to add interference in foreign politics to our current tasks, and decide to just let them escort us to our destination.
Other members of their party are [[Tharivol]], [[Taliesin]] (who we met previously in the bar), [[Mialee]] and [[Halinath]].

We end the night sleeping in their camp - in [[Arinya|Arinya's]] tiny hut off course.
