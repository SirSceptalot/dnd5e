[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

[[Arinya]] is off to the market in order to sell our cart and horses, along with various other supplies and loot that we have little use for.
In the meantime, [[Tiziano]] and I return to the Archmage of Magic to try and sell the potion of longevity we have, believing we could possibly make quite a pretty penny from this.

This time, a [[Kenku]] (Ravenfolk) assists us in getting us in touch with [[Rebis Rumerach]].
After sitting in the waiting room for a while, we're let into his office and agree that they will orchestrate the sale of the potion of longevity. We set a reserve of 8000 gold. There is a fixed administration fee that will be deducted, along with a percentage of the winnings.

After this [[Tiziano]] and I head back to the market place to see if [[Arinya]] has made any headway with her sales, but stop by an armoury on the way.
We commission two small sets of armour, one for Spyro and one for Fiona.
After agreeing on a 200 gold coin advance, along with another 330 gold upon delivery, we make the rest of the way to Arinya.

Together we still go get a few scrolls, and finally go back to the dancing hut and leave for Jarnhov.

There's still some daylight left, and since there isn't much to do while the Dancing Hut is navigating by itself, Arinya makes a few more scrolls.
I decide to return the favour and create 2 scrolls of Protection from Good and Evil for her.
