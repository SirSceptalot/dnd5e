[Location:: [[Ekholm]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

We arrive in [[Ekholm]] somewhere around midday.
After a short talk with the guard, they can confirm Hadak passed by and inflicted significant damage.
There was indeed some sort of cart with them, headed in the same direction, confirming our suspicions.

[[Arinya]] scries on Tisiphone again, and sees her pestering Magister Gribolt into working faster. She hears her say that they'll leave the continent as soon as the work is complete.

After this we fly towards the Spire as we continue our search.

We arrive there after dark, but [[Tiziano]] can still make out a combination of huts, ruins and tents being populated by both warforged and gunners.

We fly a while back again so we can rest at a safe distance from their fortifications and rest for the night.
