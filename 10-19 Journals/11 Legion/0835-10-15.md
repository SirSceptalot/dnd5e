[Location:: [[Avelund]]]
[Mission:: [[Restore productivity of Avelund mines]]]
[[Viantius]] flies us back to the city in the morning, saving us hours of travel and potential trouble.
He drops us off back at the [[Harlequin's Alehouse]].

After a brief conversation with [[Lala]], we carry on to Emerson Hale's home, where we discuss with [[Emerson Hale]] how far they are with the trap for Jericho, which is an extra-large deer trap. It should be finished by tomorrow. Mounting it at the trap site should take no more than an hour.

Emerson also says there are a number of weapons being produced by the various blacksmiths across the outskirts.

In turn, we inform him of our success at the mines, and all that was going on there.

Done with Emerson for now, we go invisible once more and head for [[Hershel McLachlan|Hershel's]] place, where Cleve Pabst also happens to be.
We ask if they've done some thinking about how to liberate the outskirts. He pretty much says the same thing as Emerson did, that they're producing weapons for people to use to fight with. It has slowed down some since they've had to divert resources to build the giant trap for Jericho.

Together, we also hash out a plan for us to ambush another patrol of [[Exaltators]]. This reduces the number of enemies and gives our side a few more quality weapons to fight with.

After doing this, we go to the agreed upon location ahead of guard shift change, wait for the help that'll collect the bodies and guard when it's done, and wait for the guards.

Our plan is executed flawless, and less than a minute passes from beginning of the attack to dragging all corpses and weapons into a nearby barn. The [[Avelund Resistance]] has a few more weapons and shields, and a few less enemies.

We go back to [[Hershel McLachlan]] after this to declare our small victory.
Since we have some time left now, I offer my services as unskilled yet tireless labour to the blacksmith across the street. Arinya takes the time to craft healing potions as usual.

While this work is going on though, Jericho approaches with two [[Exaltators|Exaltator]] Elite soldiers and a cart.
I manage to sneak back to the others without being seen, but once he reaches Hershel's place, we can hear them say "this is the place". They were clearly informed about our whereabouts.

[[Jericho]] smashes straight through the walls of the house with his hammer.
This is the first time I can see him in action, and although he's clearly a foe to be reckoned with, it's easy to see that he's quite a lumbering beast.
This isn't really a surprise, but if Arinya or Tiziano cast any offensive spells on him, they would do well to make use of his weak dexterity.

[[Hershel McLachlan]] begins distracting Jericho and his goons so we can make a safe retreat. Just before he does so, he tells us to find Almira Maiden.

While Tiziano changes into an animal to make a quick escape, Arinya teleports herself and me away with her magic cloak just as we see Hershel get crushed by Jericho's hammer.

Right after we rematerialize, Arinya sends Fiona back to Jericho to have a closer look at the cart, hoping she wouldn't be seen in the mayhem. She wasn't, and the cart seems to contain shackles that would fit a small dragon.
This would match the letter we found in the mines indicating that Behektur would send Jericho to the mountains to kidnap a young white dragon. Clearly, he wants a new source of dragons blood.

Arinya keeps Fiona out in order to track Jericho's path of destruction. He seems to selectively hit every blacksmith in the area.

Tiziano is quickly able to track us down thanks to his beast shape.

Once reunited, we discuss what happened and quickly come to the conclusion that Emerson Hale must indeed be a traitor.

With Jericho hitting other parts of the city, we go back to the [[Harlequin's Alehouse|tavern]] to regroup. Just as we arrive there (invisible), Spyro spots [[Emerson Hale]] sneaking out of town with a pack on his back.
He passes right by the tavern, so we're able to ambush him easily and drag him back into his place (which is nearby).

He admits to his treason. He says he made the switch to the winning team since he saw no good in trying to be loyal to a city and government that had abandoned him.

We keep him tied up during the night, while I do my best to weigh his fate.
Arinya finds 102 gold coins on him.
