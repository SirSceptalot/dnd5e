[Location:: [[Heidelburg]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

We visit [[Emil]] to inquire regarding Magister Gribolt and are given the keys to his tower, which is called [[Tùr Draoidheachd]]
He was taken 8 days ago. Again, the description leads us to suspect the culprit is Tisiphone.

On the way there, we stop by another shop to purchase the material component for the Revivify spell, along with a few ingredients for healing potions.

The tower itself seems fine, still invisible from far away but visible as soon as one approaches to within a certain distance.

We enter the tower and try to find clues to his kidnapping. The inside is, like the previous time, quite impressive with a seemingly endless plane contained within the tower.
[[Arinya]] finds a rune that disables the illusion, which makes searching much more convenient.

With the illusion gone, we do see all sorts of signs of a struggle. There's also an Erinyes feather here, so we can be fairly certain Tisiphone is to blame.
Once more, [[Arinya]] scries on her and sees her walking with an ocean or large lake in the distance. She can also hear swampland sounds and a cart riding.

We assume they're somewhere in Ekholm.

Exploring the rest of the tower, we find a ransacked laboratory. In the top floor is some sort of a small vessel, which is very likely a prototype of a ship that floats on air.

We message Ecbert Dhaleus to inform him of our findings, and set out in pursuit.

During the rest of the day, I take the time in the [[Dancing Hut]] to work on my [[Defence upgrade]].
