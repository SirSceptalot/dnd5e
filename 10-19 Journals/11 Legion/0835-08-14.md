[Location:: [[Little Ivy Wood]]]
First day of our journey to [[Avandar]] in order to [[Find the Morter and Pestle]].
[[Tiziano]] and [[Legion|I]] decide to spend some of the time we have to exchange some knowledge of our languages, Aquan and Elven, while [[Arinya]] will spend her days crafting more healing potions.

Before the day ends, we find ourselves at a small farming community named [[Little Ivy Wood]], where we stop at the [[Coal Faced Robin]], the local tavern.

We ask the owner [[Horfu Rodidotir]] about the herb bundles with amulets above all the doors, which she explains are to keep the White Woman out, some sort of ghost who lives in the [[Gribenwald]].

A number of the lumberjacks tell stories of going into the woods, but facing ever increasing resistance. This seems to be more creepy than harmful though.

By their account, the forest will first start to grow quiet. Those that don't find this eerie enough to return, the shrubs and branches of the forest will try to physically pull you back.
If even that does not deter you, they say the final warning is the appearance of "the white woman", which has been enough to scare away even the most determined travelers.

Since we need to pass through the forest to reach our destination, it would appear we will find out soon just how far this ghost is willing to take her scare tactics.

The rest of the evening and night pass rather uneventful.
