[Location:: [[Endike|Endike Mountain Range]]]
Sunday, another day of travel which may see us walking in the snow instead of greenery.

During the day, we're ambushed by a couple of [[Yeti]]'s.


One bear skull, a few dear skulls and a wolf skull plus a nice piece of piece of obsidian are the items we find nearby as prize for surviving the attack.

The path starts to show signs of civilization, such as paved sections and markings on the walls in a language none of us can read - although it might be old elvish.
There are a number of statues of ledges above us of humanoid creatures with wings.

Further down the path we finally come to a wall with a large ornate double door.
Testing the water, we feel that it does indeed have a healing property.

The doors are unlocked, and a dark corridor runs down, with the waters running up the stairs in defiance of gravity.

A few of the murals inside depict Pelor, the god of harvest and sun, who hates all undead

Mounts in the hallways hold torches that seem to have been recently extinguished.

Inside, it doesn't take us long to run into a number of slightly translucent undead creatures.
The fight nearly kills me, but [[Arinya]] and [[Tiziano]] manage to eke out the victory regardless.

We decide to regroup and rest outside and try again with fresh courage in the morning.
During the night, I spot a figure silently opening the door but retreating as soon as they spot me.
Other than that, nothing worth noting occurs this night.
