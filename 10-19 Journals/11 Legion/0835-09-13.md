In the morning I ask [[Ilavu]] if he would mind us joining him to see the giant, which he is fine with.

The trek there is not easy. It turns out my soles are not at all suited for travel in snow, as I slip and nearly fall to my death. Only quick actions by both [[Arinya]] and [[Tiziano]] saved me from plummeting to my demise.

On or way there, a rock depicts a ritual written in a language unknown to me. Arinya takes the opportunity to practice a skill she received from her patron recently, namely to cast a ritual of her own to understand any language. Fascinating.
While she does this, I ask Ilavu what the runes say, which he says is the story of the Storm Giant [[Arezsas]], how he came to this world and made it his home, and how he began brewing strong drinks to find peace with the creatures living near him.
It ultimately didn't prove very useful, but it did become a new hobby.

There is an incantation on the stone that Ilavu uses to summon Arezsas. A projection of him appears, and after polite introductions he and Ilavu start speaking a foreign language to work out the new deal. The words Aurocondor and whiskey are the only 2 I can make out.

Arinya can apparently understand it all, as the ritual she cast earlier gives her the ability to understand any language for a certain duration.

She decides to casually join the conversation and asks what Aurocondor is all about. The Storm Giant says it's the first land of the Gods, but returns to the conversation with Ilavu after that.

We go back to the tribe after the conversation ends.
