We come above deck of the ship to see Salvatore shouting commands to the navigator under supervision of the captain.
There are a number of arguments because Salvatore's directions directly contradict the maps the navigator has, but his information seems to be correct and they're able to safely guide us closer to our destination.

For my part, I continue writing the scroll of Protection From Evil and Good before noon, which I then give to Arinya. In the afternoon I help Tiziano with the crafting of the magic item he's working on, a Belt of Fire Giant Strength.

During the course of the day, fog completely envelops our ship. At a certain point, a shock and a tremor passes through the ship, indicating that the Guiding Stone succeeded in breaking us through the magical barrier surrounding Aurocondor.

In a conversation with Salvatore I learn that Hadak is likely to search for [[Tsukahara Bokuden]] in the [[Thrice-blessed empire]] first.

[[Nivemera]] in turn thinks it might be best to avoid confrontation with Hadak, and try to get the other items first.

On the other hand, I think it might be a good idea to try and destroy Hadak's ship while he's away on a mission, stranding him in Aurocondor. Alternatively, we could try to steal his Guiding Stone. This would have the same effect, but he will probably have it with him.

[[Arinya]] does a scrying spel targeted on Reckoning and sees the following:
He's in a cabin on a ship, together with another Warforged. They're having a conversation about the new Warforged that Hadak has created and brought with them on their ship. They find the warforged very odd and dislike their very existence. They also dislike that Hadak is bringing them along and using them to bolster his army.

Any interesting tidbit that Arinya overheard thanks to the scrying spell was the following: the other Warforged says that they'll see what the new units can do in two days, when the arrive to their destination. He wonders why they're going there now in the first place. Reckoning answers with "If we go there now, we'll have a bigger army when we face the warlord."

Arinya also says she could tell that they're flying above forest in their flying ship, but since she doesn't know the area she can't identify any landmarks which would help us identify their location.

Since we've made it through the magical barrier surrounding Aurocondor, there's no need to have the guiding stone in plain view so we hide it away in the Portable Hole.

As our ship (the Dauntless explorer), gets closer to the Eternal City we finally have a good view of it. It's a vast and impressive city with highly complex architecture and bridges between tall buildings.

There are many levels to the city, and also many towers.

After a while we make it to the docks where the crew parks the boat. The docks and the area around it seem abandoned.

From one of the buildings a man emerges and offers us a "free sample" of a dark liquid.
[[Tiziano]] and [[Arinya]] accept one from the man to be rid of him quickly, while [[Salvatore]] and I refuse.

After having moved on, we ask [[Salvatore]], who seems to know the way very well around here, what the dark liquid is. He says it's a basic blood potion. Some (vampire type creatures) drink it to satiate their blood lust, but for others it might vie a small health boost. He does warn of it's addictive nature.

We move on until we reach a small square with a statue in the center, where we meet up with one of Salvatore's contacts called [[Traakschyk]], who is ratfolk.

[[Traakschyk]] leads us into a house, into a cellar, through a hole in the cellar wall that leads into the sewers. As we traverse the sewers, we come to a point where the ratfolk guy stops us and tells us that we must start being very stealthy. There is an alpha garkain nearby, and we are advised to try and avoid a fight if at all possible.

After a tense few minutes, we manage to pull it off and sneak past it's lair until we are far enough away that our movement won't wake it up anymore.

As we move further along the sewers, we start to see buildings. The further we go, the more buildings we see, and even buildings built upon older buildings. It looks like the old city started to sink, but they just kept building on top of them.

After a while, we enter a region that [[Salvatore]] calls the [[Skavenhold]].

We're on our way to the [[Council of Gray Seers]], the group that leads the ratfolk/skaven. We're tolk to be careful, as there are plenty of backstabbing rats in here.

There is no regard for safety here, open machinery is spinning with people walking millimeters from moving parts.

We enter a specific building and need to do our best to squeeze into all sorts of narrow passages.
There's a lot of confusing talking, but in the end we're lead through a portal that leads us to the astral plane, which supposedly is a shortcut to other such portals - although it can be mighty dangerous there.

We land on a platform that starts to move to another portal. After few hours (I think), we're attacked by a [[Beholder]], but manage to drive it off. Tough fight, interesting creature.

Another while later, the platform stops and another "gnawhole" spews us back out into the world.

We can see the [[Divine Empire]] in the distance, and move on until we reach it.

After talking with a guard and giving my javelin of lightning as collateral, we're lead inside and into a very well maintained mansion - although the rest of the city is also extremely well maintained.

We're guided to a nice hall, where [[Tsukahara Bokuden]] is sat, flanked by bodyguards.

He looks younger than I would have guessed, and has an dark complexion with an Asian face.

The discussion with him goes better than I would have hoped, and he sees the danger Hadak poses.

He challenges me to a duel for sport, and we have a magnificent fight where I manage to take the upper hand - although we were both holding back slightly.

During dinner, he tells us he can have roughly 1500 men ready by the time Hadak arrives.
The plan is to have Hadak's forces batter themselves onto the city's defences, while we go round and attack from the rear.

[[Arinya]] scries on reckoning with a spell that's empowered, and can see at least 5 of Hadak's own warforged.

Before the day ends, [[Arinya]] has another conversation with [[Illion]].
