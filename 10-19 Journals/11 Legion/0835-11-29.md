[Location:: [[Jarnhov]]]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

We reach Jarnhov shortly after dawn. The advantage of being able to travel full days and nights cannot be underestimated.

Outside the gates of this small harbour town is where we park the [[Dancing Hut]], before searching for the captain.

After a bit of asking around, we arrive at [[The Broken Blade]] where we find [[Gilmer Chatan]].
He informs us that he's on a paid mission to find a bowl shaped magical artefact.

We can leave soon, he just needs to do a few more preparations and tell his crew we're ready to leave.

True to his word, we leave around noon. There are no issues during the rest of the day.
