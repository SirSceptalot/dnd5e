[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: ]

The festival is today.

We head back to the library to inquire about Eribus, where a message awaits telling us he'll be in the dining hall of the university around lunch time.

While I'm there, I look for books that might help me in my quest for a [[Defence upgrade]], and find a book by a certain Russel Hibbler, "On statics and dynamics".
I feel this has been a tremendous help.

[[Arinya]] has another look at finding some info on Pit Fiends, since Hadak is one. I've noted the information she found in my Bestiary.

It's time to go to our meeting then, and we find [[Eribus]] in the dining hall as promised.

We have a conversation regarding Elterion with him, and learn a tiny bit more - although most was in his paper.
When asked about a possible alliance between Hadak and Elterion, he says there is almost no chance at all that Elterion would collude with a Pit Fiend. They're more likely to be enemies.

He's currently researching Qliphoth, whatever they are.

After this, we head to Paragon to see if they have a few people that can do some scrying for us.

They have 2 people that can perform the ritual twice each. [[Arinya]] can user her orb to do a fifth session.

Soon after our request, a wizard named Persival arrives, who scries on Render.
He's standing on a boat which is in a dry dock. Gribolt is seen there as well, completing an incantation on a crystal embedded in the ship.

Persival also scries on Tisiphony, but this fails.

The second scrying will meet us later.

While waiting, we shop around the city to find ways to increase our combat effectiveness, and come across a business that performs magical tattoos.

We mull this over but don't make a decision yet, as any person can only have one of these on his body, lest they conflict with one another.

Earlier today, [[Tiziano]] was invited by Eribus to join him in his apartment to join him in a Qliphoth-related ritual, so he goes there while we wait for the other wizard to come and scry for us.

When he arrives (he's called Migosh), we have him attempt to scry on Reckoning.
He's in a forest in the rain, with other warforged around him.
He's talking with them about being bored and hoping for a new assignment.

Besides that, Migosh also tries to scry on Tisiphony, but this fails.

[[Arinya]] tries to scry on the Erinyes with her orb of scrying, which succeeds this time.
It works, and she sees Tisiphone walking throgh a small village. People are greeting her and inviting her for a drink during the festival. She clearly notices she's being watched by us, but seems unbothered by it.

With a robe hiding her armor and wings, she looks decidedly human.

Either way, we've decided on what makes most sense for a magical tattoo at the moment: a tattoo of fire resistance for me.

We'll need the raw materials as discussed earlier in the tattoo parlor, so we go to the Forum to order Fire Opal dust, worth roughly 5100 gold.

It should be ready for pickup tomorrow morning.
