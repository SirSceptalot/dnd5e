[Location:: [[Restless Mire]]]
[Mission:: [[Settle the Restless Mire]]]

We travel towards the ruins indicated by the Bruxa yesterday.
The terrain is certainly changing, from the typical swampy grounds to a more hilly terrain.

As we rise one large hill, there starts to be an accumulation of old weapons scattered around. There's also a notable complete absence of wildlife.

As we approach the summit, we start hearing the clatter of weapons and war in our heads, driving us to inflict violence on each other.
Fortunately, we all manage to shrug off the effects and carry on.

We trek the rest of the day towards the ruins.
