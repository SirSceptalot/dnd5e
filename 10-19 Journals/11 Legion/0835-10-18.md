[Location:: [[Avelund]]]
[Mission:: [[Restore productivity of Avelund mines]]]

At dawn, people suddenly start shouting outside, in a not quite panicked voice.
A strange golden translucent sphere surrounds the inner city, and we can see [[Viantius]] flying inside.

Team Spyro/Fiona get out and scout at once, and quickly confirm that the [[Orc|Orcs]] are indeed wreaking havoc in the inner city. They do seem to have more than a formidable force and little direction or discipline though.
Before long, a forward force comes barging through the city gates and our fortifications.

We're able to beat them, but a couple defenders from the resistance die.

Immediately after this small victory, a runner comes up to report that Behektur has broken through and is making a run for it at gate 1.

By the time we make it there, they tell us that Behektur has roughly half an hour head start.
He has two Exaltator Elite (Human) and Krisius with him.

We can follow them to a small space where they're resting, but we're too do a frontal assault and they teleport out.

Once back at the city, we can see the forcefield is back in place and [[Viantius]] has everything under control.
No more Exaltators or Orcs are outside the forcefield, and it looks like the [[Mawcrusher]] is having fun destroying everything inside.

Tiziano and I spend the rest of the day helping the residents of Avelund clean up the aftermath of the fights at the gates.
Arinya uses her time to craft another healing potion.

Near the evening, when it looks like the orcs are rounding up the rest of the [[Exaltators]], a party is starting to break out in the outer city.

[[Cleve Pabst]] tries to scry on Behektur, but fails.

After this, Arinya and Tiziano join the party while I go out and have a talk with [[Viantius]] from atop the wall he's sitting on.
We sit there gazing at the destruction.
