[Location:: [[Endike Artifact Cave]]]
Come first light, we venture back in and meet no resistance where the fight was last night.
We soon find an improvised barricade walling off a section close by, where light streams from behind it.

A polite greeting and some banter later, the elf [[Rizerin]] introduces us to his companion [[Bhatashk]].
Their party used to be three, but the last member lies dead in the room.

They are professional explorers, sent there to retrieve an item. Since our goal of surviving here seems to align with theirs, we decide to band together for the time being.

The artifact they seek is guarded by construct called a [[Clockwork Myrmidon]].

After defeating it, Arinya sees that a glow still emanates from within the construct.
We extract it, but nobody is quite sure what to do with it. We decide to bring it along in the hopes of being able to enhance my body with it somehow.

In another location in this underground maze, we find the book [[Hater of Life]] and a journal left behind by a miner looking for the same artifacts.

There are a number of other artifacts noted in the book, so it warrants another look.
The writer of the journal lamented the fact that they could not go further due to the Clockwork Myrmidon.

After systematically working our way through the underground corridors, we soon find the prize we were looking for: the [[Mortar and Pestle]].

A vile black liquid drips from the ceiling into the mortar below, which then overflows with the pure healing water that this river is known for.

As Arinya approaches, the [[Witch Amulet]] she carries for the [[Lady of the Woods]] begins to glow and crack, along with a number of markings and signs around the source of the black liquid.

The dome from which the liquid drips cracks, and out comes a toad-like creature that introduces itself as a [[Galguth]], a type of demon called a [[Greruor]].

This is exactly why I didn't want Arinya to strike a deal with that shady "lady".

Sadly, there is no point in fighting this creature now on its terms. All this would do is get us all killed, and the demon would still roam free. I have no choice but to let it pass, hoping I will be able to seek it out and send it back to hell from whence it came another day. We must [[Defeat Galguth]].

After watching it leave, we let Arinya claim her prize, and hope the good she can do with this artifact can offset the evil the demon will do before we can end it.

Once reunited with the two drow who waited for us in the central corridor, we advance to their target, where we are promptly attacked by more of those semi-translucent entities.

[[Bhatashk]], who fought so valiantly against the Clockwork Myrmidon, falls to their ghastly touch.

After the battle, [[Rizerin]] goes to the statue in the centre of the room, but fails to see a trap guarding the artifact, and also nearly dies. I'm able to revive him by laying on hands, after which he is willing to divulge the prize he would get for retrieving this artifact: 5000 gold coins.

They used to be a party of three, in which case his share would be roughly 1667 coins.
We agree to let him keep 2000, and take 3000 for ourselves. He has more than he would get originally, and we still get the lions share after saving his life multiple times.

The statue in the room itself is a very old depiction of the Raven Queen, but other than that there is not much of interest to be found in the room.

There is one room left in this underground lair, where we are attacked by an animated metal chain.
There is a small chapel in the centre of this room, with symbols of the betrayer god Tharizdun.

We have already released on demon today, so I suggest to the party to leave the vengeful betrayer god locked up.
The corridor to his room is collapsed under the combined efforts of Arinya and myself, so nobody accidentally wanders in and releases the god.

After all this, Rizerin insists on taking the heads of his fallen companions with him to prove their demise.

We take their weapons to sell.

Arinya decides to take the rest of the day to create a few more new healing potions with her new Mortar and Pestle, while Tiziano and I wander the corridors to see if we missed anything.

Tiziano does actually manage to find a secret passage, but we decide to wait until a new day to explore it.
