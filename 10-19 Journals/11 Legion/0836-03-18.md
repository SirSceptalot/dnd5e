[Location:: en-route to Amaroth]
[Mission:: [[Defence upgrade]]]

Another breakthrough!
I'll get there yet.

Speaking of getting there, we landed at the gates of [[Amaroth]] as the sun was setting.
The city seems to be preparing for festivities, the summer solstice. There are stalls and performers setting up everywhere.

Our [[Dancing Hut]] is still intact and were we left it.
We head for Ecbert's tower, where he welcomes us in formal attire.
It seems he's needed in the west, as Ashein has annexed Ermedes - a clear violation of their treaty. It's been decided the city must be liberated again - by force if needed, and since Ecbert is the arcmage of war, this task falls upon him.

[[Verras]], the Archmage of Diplomacy, will take over the case with Hadak in the mean time.

She surprises us by giving us each 1000 gold coins as compensation for work already accomplished, and promises and extra reward once the task has been completed.
Not sure which task she's referring to at this time, but I'm not one to questions such things.

The discussions between the council and Weiss are going well. One of here largest demands, a piece of land for the Warforged to call home after the war, is being agreed to.

My reward for seeing the "issue" with Hadak through would be to make me an ambassador for this new nation.

Arinya's reward would be to make her and her family officially nobility.

[[Tiziano]] may choose his own reward, and surprises everyone by saying he would like for [[Nivemera]] to be given a new body.

After all this, a few guards are fetched and we extract the prisoner from our [[Portable Hole]].

[[Arinya]] asks if we may park the [[Dancing Hut]] inside the city, to which [[Verras]] agrees. She walks with us to the hut, then takes a ride in it to find a suitable location.
She then has some paperwork drawn up to give us official permission to park there whenever we need.

We close the day by cleaning the hut, purchasing some niceties with which to decorate it, and having a relaxing BBQ.
