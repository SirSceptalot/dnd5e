[Location:: [[Jarnhov]]]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

Travel to [[Jarnhov]], which we reach right before dark.
There's a few notes posted at our chicken hut. Some complain of an illicit building, but another by a mage that says we should really inform people if we're to leave such an artifact on their doorstep.
The mage seems to have moved it a few hundred meters away from the city walls.

We receive our payment of 3000 gold coins from [[Gilmer Chatan]] here, officially ending our mission.

We quickly visit a few shops before they close and then leave for Amaroth in our [[Dancing Hut]].
