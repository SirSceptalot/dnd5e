[Location:: [[Heidelburg]]]
Post breakfast, we join a patrol of 4 guards to [[Horace Ringmann's mansion|Mr. Ringmann's home]] where the guards let themselves in, only to find the poor [[† Horace Ringmann|owner's]] remains.

While heading upstairs after the guards, [[Tiziano]] sees something skitter down the stairs and sets off in pursuit, alerting and guiding me in the process.

At the back of the cellar, where an impressive collection of wine barrels is to be found, the wall has been caved in, leading away to a tunnel.

The tunnel enters another constructed space where a number of dwarven symbols are carved in the walls.
This space leads into a junction, a long downward spiraling tunnel to the left with more dwarven symbols carved into the  walls, and straight ahead a small room with several alcoves.

The corridor that spirals down has at least one trap, which I found the hard way but was able to evade.

Moving into the main room, 2 undead hands - and only the hands - attack but are swiftly dispatched.

What follows next is the most harrowing experience I've ever had. We were attacked by 4 ghost-like shadows, the likes of which I've never seen. My weapon does little to them, until I call upon the magic granted to me by Paragon. This does seem to hurt them fiercely.

Nevertheless, I go down not once, but twice in this battle. I owe my life to [[Tiziano]], and am now in his debt.

After the fight, the only thing of value in the room are four rings of pure gold. The craftsmanship on these makes them worth far more than their weight in gold, literally.

We go back upstairs, barricade the entrance with some furniture, warn the guards to stay clear and walk outside.

Taking a short rest at [[The Singed Hare]] precedes another visit to [[Frey Goldhammer]] at his shop [[Forging Ahead]], where we inquire about the Dwarven runes we encountered in the hallways .

Judging by our sketches, he can identify the writings above the alcoves are names:
- Yadgrun shadowminer
- Hackic Brisselbraeker
- Nusgrolsia Runshield
- Ragruïk Flintmaker

The antechamber has the words "here rest the unresting dead".

These may be warriors in a long forgotten war.

After thanking Frey, we go to [[Fizzy Flippy Frost's Fantastic Fascinations]] to purchase 2 healing potions, one for [[Arinya]] and one for [[Legion|myself]]. [[Tiziano]] is able to provide himself with emergency healing in the form of the magical healing berries he can create.

Next up, we go to administration to inform [[Emil]] of our discovery, who decides to summon the archmage of culture, [[Elostrum]].
He'd like us to return in order to [[Clear Horace Ringman's Mansion]] of any remaining danger from the house so they city and its inhabitants are safe.
We negotiate a good price: an enchanted adamantine chainmail armour. This would take 7 days to produce.
In return, we must clear the rest of the underground corridors and turn in my current armour.

As we have no more plans for the remainder of the day, we find ourselves going back to [[Fizzy Flippy Frost's Fantastic Fascinations]] to have another look at her wares, more specifically the armours.
When it's once again clear that most items are out of our price range, I inquire about the [[Witch Amulet]] that Arinya agreed to carry along for the lady of the woods.
[[Fizzy Flippy Frost]] can tell that it's an enchanted item designed to react to the presence of something else, but she cannot deduce any more details.

Our next stop is [[Magister Gribolt]], where we respectfully decline his offer for employment in the form of a hunt for a Spectator, as a previous conversation between Tiziano and myself has made it clear that neither of us has much interest in an unprovoked attack on a creature for nothing more than a mage's fancy.

We eventually retreat to [[The Singed Hare]], where my companions have an elaborate pre-meal and meal.
