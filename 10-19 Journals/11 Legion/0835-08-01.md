[Location:: [[Iglberg]]]
Trading in all the spoils of the fights from the day before and using our reputation as local heroes, a cart and two horses are acquired for the low price of 90 gold coins.

The remainder of the profit from the previous day is divided between [[Tiziano]] (16gp), [[Arinya]] (16gp) and [[Legion|myself]] (15gp).

For today, we plan to venture to the "[[Lady of the Woods]]" in order to learn more about her.
There is no specific plan about what to do with her, this is purely a mission of reconnaissance.

Arinya remarks that [[Helmine]] † thought rather highly of her, although this is likely because she thought to profit heavily from whatever arrangement they had made.
Fortunately, Helmine's notes included a general description to the hut where she can be found.

After stabling our horses at [[Redcap Boots]] for the day, we venture into the woods, where Tiziano finds a variety of treats, leading us to a path towards our destination.

A destructive experiment proved the candy to be nothing of the sort however, as they disintegrate into a lump of insects, dirt and other less palatable ingredients.

The Lady of the Woods is obviously extremely suspect and most likely a foul creature. However, I have no proof of neither any malicious intent or any deliberately harmful actions on her part, so after an unpleasant conversation in which she refuses to reveal herself and attempting to bribe Arinya into transporting an amulet for her, we make for the [[Iglberg|village]] again.

On our way back to the village we are once again attacked, resulting in another four dead [[Gnoll]].
Due to a rather exuberant celebration of our victory by Tiziano, we are forced to retreat from a far larger group of gnolls that were alerted to our presence.

The sun is setting as we arrive back at the village. My companions have no great plans for the rest of the evening, so I spend my evening by going to the new wisdom [[Cerian Bedham]] to help her tend to the wounded.

Like the night before, I stand guard outside the inn when I eventually return.
