[Location:: [[Northern Islands]]]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

We fly back to the ship, carried by eagle [[Tiziano]].
During the flight, we encounter a large delegation of [[Yuan-ti]] enroute to the [[Goblin]] village.

We land, and [[Arinya]] is able to intimidate them into leaving the Goblins alone, at least for now.

Back in the boat, Captain [[Gilmer Chatan]] agrees to take us to our next mission ([[Defeat Behektur]]).

Another long voyage begins.
