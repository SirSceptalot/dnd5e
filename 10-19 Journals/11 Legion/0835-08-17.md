[Location:: [[Gribenwald]]]
Fourth day of travel, the terrain is getting worse still.
At a certain point, we come across a chasm that we fail to get the cart past in one piece, and we are forced to leave it behind. We do take the tarp with us for shelter.

[[Arinya]] and [[Tiziano]] continue on horseback. Since the horses would tire out before I ever do, I continue on foot.

Today is also the day where we start to see actual rivers instead of swamp, although the ground is still soggy. This is the first landmark that we were looking for, meaning our navigation attempt has been a success.
Arinya can feel some positive magic in the water, but it's too diluted to discern much.

Further upriver still, the various streams begin to coagulate in a larger river of a few metres wide, where we eventually make camp.

We don't always make a fire, but it's a good thing we did on this occasion, as it turned out to be the only way to defeat the Swamp Troll that attacked us during the night.

We discovered the hard way that these creatures possess staggering regeneration as long as they are not subject to fire damage. It didn't look quite as sturdy as a regular troll, but the regeneration alone made it almost indestructible.

It even started to grow back its head after I detached it from the rest.
After the fight, [[Tiziano]] told us that he heard these beasts don't really have a society of their own, but mostly live alone and work as mercenaries for other races.
