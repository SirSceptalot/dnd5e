[[0835-09-21 (alternate timeline)]] | **Thursday, 0835-09-20** |  [[0835-09-21]]

[Location:: [[Collegento]]]
When we emerge from the portal, it soon becomes apparent that almost no time passed on this side.

After a bit of orientation, we focus on the three soul gem shards we found in the Far Realms.
Since we've already tried communicating with the large soul gem, we decide to try again with these three shards.
Only Tiziano is successful, but he finds out that the soul contained in them is part of [[Nivemera]].

The summary of the conversation is that she traded her soul to an entity know as The Gatekeeper so she could stop Skogsland advancing army. This caused her own god Pelor to become angered, and to capture her soul in a stone where it is tormented.
She appreciates that we want to help her.

We go back to the [[Temple of Sarenrae in Collegento]] to deliver the news of [[Camedyr Baughan|Camedir's]] demise, as well as his remains.
[[Waldrun]] can't give us much information about how to save the soul of [[Nivemera]], but he recommends we go to the [[Temple of Asmodeus in Collegento]] to see [[Varius]].

Start of a new mission: [[Save Nivemera's soul]].

He agrees to exchange favours. He would send someone with us to free the soul of Nivemera, if I agree to send one evil soul straight to Asmodeus each month for a year. This can be accomplished with a small trinket they'd give me.
Start of mission: [[12 souls for Asmodeus]].

The usual path is for the Raven Queen to pass judgment upon the souls, who then sends them to hell or where they belong.
Asmodeus would enjoy if this process can be sped up a bit.
I negotiate for the soul to be kept in the trinket for an hour to give me time to make a final deliberation.

While the item is being created, we make our way to [[Borsodi's Auction House]] to put the Dread Knight Skull up for auction. It will be up for auction on [[0835-09-22]].

After this, we return to the [[Temple of Asmodeus in Collegento]] where [[Runa]] is ready to join us to retrieve Nivemera's soul from her prison.

We go to the docks to acquire a boat, in order to approach [[Nivemer's Stand]] from the waterside so we don't get seen.
We want to do this unseen since the people from the Temple of Pelor in Collegento would "not appreciate" our actions to save [[Nivemera]].

At Nivemer's Stand we go to the location of the soul stone and set up the tiny hut around it.

According to Runa, the easiest way to release the soul from the stone is to crack the stone, which we'll do by stabbing it with three metal rods.
Preparation consists of drawing symbols on the ground with chalk, preparing a small silver cage to catch the soul when it leaves, and performing a small ritual.
We each take part in the ritual in form of a small offering or word of encouragement to Nivemera.

When Nivemera's soul emerges, so do three salamander-like creatures, who immediately attack us.
These were apparently torturing her soul.
The soul manages to retreat to the silver cage, while we deal with the other creatures.

While scouring the environment, we see another of the [[Illithid]] called [[Orestes]]. He quickly decides to relocate back through the portal.

The night is spent in Arinya's tiny hut in one of the buildings.
