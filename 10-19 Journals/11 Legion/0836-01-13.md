[Location:: between Amaroth and Velgend]
[Mission:: [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]]]

We're getting near Velgend when a group of [[Judicators|Judicator]] warriors approaches - Paladins.
The glorious fight that follows is one which we just barely manage to win, both sides keeping the promise that we'll spare each other's lives - although there was a bit of a misunderstanding on Arinya's part. We part amicably and travel onward.

Sometime later that day we receive a message that we've been assigned a new contact because we had a bit of a quarrel with the Morthïr the last time we met them.
The new contact is called Nathyr, who will met us in Velgend on the 20th of this month.
