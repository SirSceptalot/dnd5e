[Location:: [[Avandar]]]
There are 2 new guards to guide us through the city.

When [[Arinya]] asks about the healing river, we are advised to visit their [[Temple of Avandra in Avandar|Temple of Avandra]].
A priestess there tells us that the river we are looking for is the one by the docks, which is known for its abundance of life.

We leave the city and travel due north east, along the river.
On the road I try to get acquainted some more with the Aquan language, but without much success.

Other than that, the day and night pass without anything noteworthy.
