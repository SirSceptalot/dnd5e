[Location:: [[Gribenwald]]]
Fifth day of travel in total, second day along the river.
The going is quite a bit better than the previous day. Better terrain, and no hassle with the cart.

[[Arinya]] spends some time working with [[Fiona]] as well.

At dusk, we come across a good camping spot where we spend the night.
Sadly, an [[Owlbear]] takes it upon himself to attempt to raid our camp. He will not listen to reason, and a fight ensues that nearly kills Arinya, and very much kills the [[Owlbear]].
