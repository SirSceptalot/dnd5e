[Location: [[Northern Islands]]]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

After breakfast, courtesy of the Goblins, we head north to the ruins.
Along the way, we're attacked by a number of [[Yuan-ti]], and notice they put venom on their weapons.

We take the following from their corpses:
Small bronze pot with smelly substance, possibly fermented roots.
Satchel with small mushrooms. Likely poisonous to most life, regular food for them.
Small piece of green/gold metal. Likely a piece of armour, although none of them wear anything similar.
3 sticks of incense, used to invoke nightmares during sleep.

4 sets of leather armour, bows, arrows and scimitars.

After a while the terrain becomes more mountainous and difficult to navigate, so [[Tiziano]] considers turning himself into a giant eagle and carry us. Since I would add considerable weight though, it's decided that I'll go into the portable hole, eliminating my weight from the equation.

During the trip Tiziano has spotted the aforementioned ruins but also a smaller building carved into a mountainside.
We landed at the latter. We enter the construction, and soon find ourselves dodging arrows after stepping on a pressure plate.

As to be expected, we're attacked by Yuan-ti at a certain point.

There's more traps, which we fortunately find in time.

At a certain point we make it into a larger room, where a small statue of a winged serpent is placed. The wings are feathered, not a membrane as one would expect.

There's also a carving at the base which reads "Here lies the anathema, our leader and destroyer."
This was written in an unknown language, but Arinya was able to read it by performing a magical ritual.

There's a well in the center of the room, with a iris door at the bottom instead of water.
We decide not to go in yet and have a look at the other door in the room.

There was a riddle written on the door: "Speak my name and I break".
The answer was "Silence", found by none other than yours truly.

Inside is a crystal sphere and 3 Yuan-ti. After dispatching of the hostile creatures, [[Arinya]] is able to determine that the sphere is an Orb of Scrying.
There's also a few golden bracelets, some gold coins and a small figurine with green emeralds for eyes.

There's another door from behind which we can hear moaning, and after a short rest and preparing for more combat we open it. We find cells and some sort of disproportioned Yuan-ti, with a human sweating and moaning unconscious in another cell. He seems to be transforming into some type of Yuan-ti, although not a regular one.

Tiziano manages to at least stop the process, although we don't know if the person will heal or not.

A bit further we find 5 doses of Yuan-ti venom, and a batch of the stuff used to transform people into the strange creatures.

In another hallway we find 2 doors, one of which is trapped by 2 lightning bolt traps. I'm able to set off one of the 2 traps by throwing the severed head of one of our enemies at it, but the other part seems undisturbed.

We decide to go to door number 2 first, and are faced with 2 Yuan-ti Brood Guards and a [[Hydra]].
Tough fight, but we manage to come out victorious again.

The bounty is all right, some gold and another figurine with emerald eyes.
Oh, there's also the artifact we've been looking for, the [[Bowl of Winds]].
We spend the night here, in Arinya's tiny hut.
