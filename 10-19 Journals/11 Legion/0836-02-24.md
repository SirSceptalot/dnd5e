[Location:: Between the Restless Mire and Esterna]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

No progress with my [[Leg upgrade]] plans today.

We make [[Esterna]] by end of day, and go the [[Temple of Sarenrae in Esterna]] where they recognize us and fetch [[Galerin]].

He casts Zone of Truth, and we have a debriefing.
He holds up his end of the bargain by leading us to the shard of divinity hidden deep within the temple.

We are lead through increasingly more lavishly decorated corridors to a room with no windows yet bright daylight inside.

The daylight comes from a golden arch at an altar. The room casts the illusion of an endless sunny plane in throes of a magnificent sunrise, even though it's currently evening.
The floor is layed out with a beautiful pattern, and a path flanked by large pillars that seem to reach the heavens above lead to three golden rings concentrically rotating a large eye within a floating fire - a [[Shard of Sarenrae]].

It greets us with "Welcome to this sanctuary".

We declare we're here in hopes of repairing the [[Mortar and Pestle]].

It will consider our request, but it wishes to judge our souls first.
None of us are found worthy. I come closes, but am denied because of my pact to send 12 souls to Asmodeus.

Back outside, we get [[Galerin]] to help heal [[Krisius|Krisius']] hands - his fingertips were cut of in torture when in the Restless Mire.

Once back outside, I get a message from [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] to inquire to our status.
When we answer, he teleports to our location so we can have a larger conversation.

We tell him of our conversation with Illion and how he could prevent Hadak from dominating the Warforged.
He's hesitant to give full Warforged shells to Illion because he fears he'll populate them with devils.
After we explain he wants to use human souls to use as staff in his bar however, he's willing to set aside a few warforged frames with restricted combat capabilities.

Ecbert then tells us of the first warforged ever created, Weiss.
This one was the only one ever built with a complete soul. In shocking news to me, all subsequent Warforged are constructed with a flaw to ensure we remain loyal, a sort of backdoor into or very being which they could exploit when needed to keep us aligned.

This is the same loophole in our mental defenses that Hadak is now using to align the warforged with his goals.

They did this because Weiss soon rebelled after she was built.

[[Ecbert Dhaleus]] tells us that Weiss is still at the original location where it was built, simply called the Workshop.

With Weiss as a template, we could upgrade existing Warforged to close the backdoor.

This also means that they might abandon us if they weren't completely loyal to begin with and their aspirations were ever overridden.

The Workshop is located in the Allstein Bog, near a construction called The Spire, which should be a clear landmark.

We'll likely be placed at Ekholm, from which we can walk to the Spire in the course of a few days.

[[Ecbert Dhaleus]] will teleport us to Ekholm, so we task [[Krisius]] with safely taking the [[Dancing Hut]] to Amaroth.

We all leave that very evening, but take some damage from a less than perfect teleportation.
