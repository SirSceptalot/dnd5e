[Location:: [[Velgend]]]
[Mission:: [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]]]

Another day off, waiting for our drow contact to meet us here.
To pass the time we have a look at the signpost to see if any of the posted tasks could interest us, but there isn't really much worth our time.

The day comes and goes, and during diner an [[Eladrin]] (elven subrace) walks in while we're having a conversation with [[Elyn]] in the [[Topaz Wand]].
(This man will later turn out to be called [[Taliesin]])
He finds us and tells us that our escort won't be joining us, due to a sudden case of Owlbear.

We don't trust this man and send him on his way. When we have some privacy, [[Arinya]] uses her orb of scrying to find our contact and manages. She's sitting in the forest against a tree, tending to her wounds.
