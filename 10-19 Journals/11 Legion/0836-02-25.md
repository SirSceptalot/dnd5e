[Location:: [[Ekholm]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

We go to [[The Welcoming Gipsy]], where we notice new furniture and a new scar on [[Zuri|Zuri's]] face.

He explains that the scar is from Render, who also ensured the need for new furniture. It seems Zuri's haggling of illegal items, especially that tower of one of the Archmages, didn't sit very well with Paragon.

Ecbert informs Zuri that he will be providing us with free accommodation as part of his retribution for his illegal acts.

We start by taking a long rest, because even though it's early morning here, we actually didn't rest yet due to the time zone difference.

Afterwards, we meet up with the local guide [[Malek]] who will guide us past the Spire to the Workshop.

He tells us to stay out of the gas clouds, and to be careful with using magic in the bog, as unexpected things seem to happen when magic is used.

There are a people living there that think their god lives in the bog, and they kill trespassers on sight.
They have miniature cannons and can attack with poison clouds.

I summon a celestial horse to aid our advance, and we resume travel through a now more forested terrain.

After a few hours of travel we reach a body in the middle of the road.
[[Tiziano]] thinks it looks odd, the proportions are off and the texture makes it look like it's made of sand.

[[Arinya]] shoots it with her blast, and this confirms it's not an actual body, as grainy particulate matter is blasted away from the point of impact.

Knowing something is off, [[Tiziano]] takes another good look and spots several figures hidden, watching us. We make ourselves heard and declare we wish them no harm as we approach in what we hope is a non-threatening manner.

Either we were threatening anyway or they had no intentions to negotiate, because soon one of their mentioned miniature cannons is fired at the "sand" body which promptly explodes.

We soon figure out that these small cannons pack quite a wallop.

The captain, who initiated this attack, is certain to go to hell, so I reap his soul for my mission of taking  [[12 souls for Asmodeus]].

They have a peculiar gas mask which actually pumps poisonous gas INSIDE rather than preventing any from getting to them.
Whatever weird thing they have going on with the gas, they still appear to be human.

We take three longswords, two muskets and three bayonets from their bodies.

After setting up camp for the night and the other going to sleep, I notice a strange green glow in the distance.

I decide not to wake the others and see if we can figure it out in the morning.
