[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]

We head to the tower district to visit [[Balthazar Gelt|Weezard]] in his gold tower.
He's prepared to make the amulet we request, and to work on it with three servants in order to get it done in about three weeks.

This would normally cost 6000 gold though, but he'd rather ask for a counter favour and pay for it himself.
Metal deliveries from the mines of Avelund has ceased unexpectedly, and he would like us to investigate and resolve the issues.
Start of new mission: [[Restore productivity of Avelund mines]]

Arinya tries to score a number of freebies as usual, to no avail.

[[Dr. Kasimir]] is the next person we meet, and old friend, confidant and mentor of [[Arinya]].
He has a waiting room full of people, but I offer to take over for a while and tend to people via magical means, so him and Arinya can chat up. Full payment is still requested from the people, which I offer him when I've dealt with the most urgent cases.
There are a few people left over with minor maladies, but since they are now the first in line for the doctor there are no complaints.

After this we're off to find a cart, since we had to sell ours before getting on the boat. We pass by the [[Demon and Toad]] inn and book a room there for the night.

We're able to buy a cart and sufficient food for the horses to last us a good while.

By end of day, we go to the Metal Toad to meet up with [[Hammer]] and [[Reckoning]], who was already hammered when we got there.

In the course of the evening Reckoning talks about how he heard that one of the Arch Mages - a female one - is secretly giving out extracurricular work. From what he heard, the jobs are mostly information gathering.

This might be the elusive "V" that hired that the rogue Warforged murderer we took down a while back.
