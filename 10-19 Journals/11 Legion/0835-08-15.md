[Location:: [[Gribenwald]]]
We say our goodbyes and carry on through the forest.

After a few hours travel, events begin to play out as described yesterday by the villagers.
The forest starts to grow quiet, after which the flora tries to prevent our passage.

At a certain point, the [[White Woman]] appears. Despite the reputation of fright attributed to her, she is actually quite lovely looking.

I try to walk ahead of the others in order to great the apparition, but when [[Arinya]] breaks rank and steals my focus, the white lady is no longer where I left her when I look back.
Instead, we find ourselves suddenly assaulted by a number of Vine Blights.

After a short fight and short rest, we travel onwards with no more resistance or strange happenings.

We make some time for my lessons in Aquan, which works fine.

As dusk approaches, we set up camp and have an uneventful evening.
