[Location:: [[Avelund]]]
[Mission:: ]

[[Cleve Pabst]] is called upon again, this time to scry on the white young dragon we saw yesterday.
It's in some sort of cave, with different pools of water from which light emanates. Elterion is leading it through the cave, to a spot that's apparently to be the new home of the young dragon.
Elterion: "You will be part of us and fight with us. There is movement towards Aurocondor, and we must be ready."

We finish our business here with the purchase of a rugged coach, two horses plus food, and a number of rations for the journey.

After saying our goodbyes and confirming that [[Cleve Pabst]] can contact us if needed, we begin the long trek back north to Velgend and then Amaroth.
