Somewhere after midnight, the [[Bruxa]] pays us a visit and asks us if we know where the Shambling Lord went. We point in the right direction and asks if she knows where Krisius went, but she says she's going to give this information to the Shambling Lord as a gift.
Something something swamp politics, beyond me.

We scry on [[Krisius]]. He's been taken captive and is being transported on a wooden door.

Tiziano is able to find the trail of the board and we set off in pursuit.
As we near them, we see he's been captured by the [[Bruxa]], not by the Shambling lord.

We manage to convince the [[Bruxa]] - who's name is [[Verena]] by the way, to work with us.
She wanted to give [[Krisius]] as an offering to the Shambling Lord so they could rule the swamp together, but she's just as pleased with our offer to kill him instead.

In return, she'd give us [[Krisius]] and ensure the creatures in the swamp stay in the swamp.

The creatures she has dragging the board on which [[Krisius]] is tied are called [[Bone Ghast]], a more advanced type of ghoul with regeneration.

[[Verena]] tells here 2 [[Bone Ghast]] to take [[Krisius]] back to her lair, and we set off together towards the Shambling Lord.

A few fights and a long trek later we find his location, surrounded by HORDES of undead.

We devise a plan of attack, which consists of [[Tiziano]] dropping me from the air right on top of the [[Shambling Lord]] lord, after which he will retreat to [[Arinya|Arinya's]] position and occupy themselves with the zombies, while I deal the [[Shambling Lord]].

We prepare by coating my weapons and shields in holy water, casting Protection from Good and Evil on me, and me drinking a Potion of Haste.

We manage to take them by storm and the [[Shambling Lord]] falls in seconds to my sword. We decide to ignore the rest of the zombies and set off again at once.

Together with [[Verena]] we go back to her lair to get [[Krisius]], and then spend the night outside in the Tiny Hut after saying farewell to [[Verena]].
