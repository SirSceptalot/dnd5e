[Location:: [[Avandar]]]
Sixth day of travel, third day along the river.

Travel is uneventful, and there is time to work on my Aquan. Today marks the fourth day of descent progress.

Near the end of day we arrive at the majestic and illustrious city of [[Avandar]], which lay in between 2 impossibly large trees.
I would estimate their size at roughly 150 metres.
Besides their immense size, there is another oddity about them - they seem to be in different seasons.
When looking at the northern tree, one would wager spring has taken the land.
The southern tree, by contrast, would have you believe the lands are growing fertile in spring.

The city gates are built into a passage that runs beneath the roots of the towering trees.
A single envoy who introduces herself as Captain [[Sariel]] meets us some way before the gates, and agrees to let us stay in the city by the law of hospitality, but makes it clear we are to be accompanied by guards at all times.

There are a number of places that are off limits to us, but for the most part we are free to roam the city.
[[Tamunda's Alchemy]] draws our attention, but none of the items on sale meet our current requirements.
[[Eryn's Scriptorium]] also has nothing within our budget and needs.

Besides the shopping district, we also make a visit to the [[Grand Library of Avandar]], where they charge the exuberant sum of 20 gold coins for entrance, with an extra 5 gold coins for every hour past the first. This price is only for visitors, residents are not subjected to this offensive rate.

Needing some information, we decide to pay for entrance for [[Arinya]], and manage to convince one of the guards to assist in the search. Together, they do manage to dig up some information on the object Arinya seeks.

After this, we're guided to the [[Gypsy's lodge]], an inn where we can spend the night. We rent a room from the owner [[Anold]].

During the night a drow crawls on the balcony with a grappling hook, and introduces himself as [[Feyris]].
He speaks of the Morthïr, a political group that seeks to interrupt the political balance of [[Avandar]].

The matriarch of the Alfarin district does not want this to happen, and seeks a way to stop it without her involvement being known.

It sounds like they're trying to convince us to rob a shipment of legal wares, so we politely decline.
