[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: [[Defeat Hadak]]]

After finishing my rest, I start to [[Read Manual of Bodily Health]] until our departure.
We receive our 2 potions of flight beforehand, and we make our way to the forum where we belatedly pick up another 2 potions of haste for another 800 gold coins.

So far, I've read 2 hours today.

When we arrive at her tower, [[Verras]] asks us for the Erinyes feather, as she'll send a team to eliminate Tisiphone since we don't have the time.

We will be teleported to Vruwood, where we'll board the Dauntless Explorer, a ship able to move faster than Hadak currently is.

Travel will take an estimated 14 days, partly through Orc infested waters.

There is still the matter of my planned [[Leg upgrade]], and so we visit [[Balthazar Gelt|Weezard]] to ask if he'd be willing to accompany us on the boat to perform the modifications then.

Once there, his servant looks paler than usual, which is soon to be clarified by the appearance of my old friend. It turns out he has transformed himself to be made entirely of metal, and the surgery seems to have been quite bloody.
He has used multiple clones of himself to perform the procedure, and it would seem they have all met a grizzly end.

Regardless, he's willing to come along and do the modifications aboard. He'll teleport himself back home once he's done.

Once we've returned to [[Verras]], we ask for a status update on Ecbert Dhaleus, and are told that the cold war is turning hotter. Negotiations are still ongoing, although the armies of Esterna are ready to invade Ermedes.
All-out war seems imminent.

After this, we're teleported to [[Vruwood]], which seems to have a heavy [[Half-Orc]] population. We can see the river running to the ocean from where we are, and head for the docks.
The dock master points us to the [[Dauntless Explorer]], the fast ship we're to board which is captained by the [[Elf]] [[Filaurel]] and her [[Half-Elf]] First Mate [[Quyana]].

During the first day, someone reports a shortage of fruit aboard, which seems to have been stolen by someone aboard.

We offer our help, and [[Arinya]] find someone making moonshine from the fruit.
She promises the goblin not to tell the captain, but he must stop stealing fruit in return.

Without further interruptions, I manage to [[Read Manual of Bodily Health]] for 4 hours today.
