[Location:: at sea]
[Mission:: [[Expedition Guard Duty]]]

Day eleven.

[[Tiziano]] notices the smallest of island - basically just a rock roughly the size of a large room.
On it, a man stands that looks unnaturally still. Even his clothes don't blow in the wind, so it's likely some sort of ghost.

As we approach, he greets us and wants to play a game of Domalian poker for gold.
Tiziano picks him up on this and winds 306 coins. [[Arinya]] wants to play a bit as well but loses 113 gold.
They urge me to have a go as well, and I win 30 gold. There should be more, but he has no more gold so wants to compensate with a medallion.

We quickly figure out it's a cursed medallion and refuse further payment.
