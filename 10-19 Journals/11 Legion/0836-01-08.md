[Location:: [[Amaroth]]]
[Mission:: ]

Travel to [[Amaroth]], which we reach around midday.
First order of business is to pick up the 2 sets of armour which we commissioned for [[Fiona]] and [[Spyro]].
After that we go to [[Rebis Rumerach]]'s office to find out if they were able to sell the potion of longevity.
They have, and we receive 10238 gold for it.

[[Arinya]] still has that mysterious sphere which she purchased from Zuri in the Welcoming Gipsy, but the mage [[Jovanus]] we're with is unable to identify it, even with magic.

[[Tiziano]] has a conversation to find out what he needs to complete the enchantments he's doing to his shield, which turns out to be the essence of a Behir.

Since Arinya wants to know what she purchased back at the Welcoming Gipsy, we head over to Ecbert Dhaleus to see if he's willing to identify it, but he's not home. We can see him a bit later though, when he's back.

We head to the shops next to look at upgrading my armour, but we're displeased with the 32 week lead time it would take.

After that we figure [[Ecbert Dhaleus]] should be back in his tower and go back there.
He is able to identify the sphere, and confiscates it. It the shrunken tower of an archmage, who experimented with forbidden magic.

Ecbert Dhaleus gives us the opportunity - not an order - to carry out a covert mission.
In return he can have an adamantine full plate armour ready for us by the time we get back, in a few weeks' time.
Start of mission: [[Move the bell of Saint Solar Macharius]].

The item must be delivered to an elf called Finnaon in the Vanyarin district.

We do some more shopping, which should enhance Arinya's ability to fight.
