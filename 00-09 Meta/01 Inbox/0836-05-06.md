we hear the same voice greeting us again.

We reiterate the deal, and he once again agrees that he will fight [[Hadak]] alongside us.

[[Arinya]] asks him about the [[Agaros]], and he has the following to say:

When the agaros devil fails a saving throw he can choose to succeed instead, but when he does so he permanently removes one fire dice (die) from all his fire dmg.

He hands us a pair of [[Sending Stones#Changer of Ways]] to summon him when we fight Hadak.

Arinya tries to scry on [[Hadak's Horned Devil]] but fails.
Tiziano casts [[Find the path]] to calibrate our direction again, and off we go towards the [[Agaros]].

After some hours of travelling our companion warforged start getting exhausted.
The terrain smooths out slightly, and we start seeing purple hexagonal basalt formations.

We bespreken nog eens de scroll van [[Gate]].

[[Nivemera]] zegt dat we hem best naar de hel sturen ipv naar een andere plane of existence.
Devils en demons hebben vaak al tegen elkaar gevochten, bv tijdens de [[Blood Wars]], waar letterlijk de deuren van the abyss en hel naar elkaar gericht waren en legioenen elkaar hebben zitten afslachten

we kunnen hem ook naar een archfey zijn domein sturen, want feys en demons mixen meestal niet goed

Ik stel voor dat we hem de kans geven om vrijwillig naar hel te gaan in ruil voor het zwaard, of we dwingen hem naar de abyss te gaan en we NEMEN het zwaard.

We're going to higher ground. The [[Warforged]] travel along on a [[Tensor's Floating Disk]]. There are some impressive vista's in this purple dry wasteland.
At a certain point the temperature starts rising notably as we start descending.

As we descend further, we start to see more random magic effects occuring. Instead of little floating orbs of light, it turns into more aggressive magical sparks arcing and sparking into the ground or between rocks.

It starts becoming very dark, up to the point where the only light we see are the sparks and our own illumination.

Arinya casts the Light cantrip. It suddenly winks out and she feels the magic being sucked out of her in a painful way, but she can keep her composure and nothing else happens.
Tiziano thinks she's exaggerating and casts a cantrip of his own, and feels the same force tearing at his magic. They discuss it and conclude that this would have stripped multiple spells from them had they failed to resist.

We move forward away from the black mist, back into normal darkness.

Arinya sets up the hut.
