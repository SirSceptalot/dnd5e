# Dreadblade

> [!Summary]
> _Weapon (Fullblade) +1, rare (requires attunement)_
> Requirements: Strength 21
> - **Damage:** 3d8
> - **Damage type:** Slashing
> - **Properties:** Heavy, Two-Handed
> - **Weight:** 20 lbs

Creatures that do not meet the Requirements make every attack at disadvantage.

The touch of time was not kind to this massive sword, yet the magic still lingers as acid drips down from the corroded blade.

**Corrosive Strikes.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, it deals an additional 1d4 acid damage. With every consecutive hit against the same target, the acid damage die increases: 1d4 → 1d6 → 1d8 → 1d10 → 1d12.

- **Corrosive Burst.** On the sixth consecutive hit, the acid within the blade explodes. All creatures within 10 feet of the blade, including the wielder, take 6d6 acid damage. This damage bypasses resistance to acid. After the explosion, the acid damage resets to 1d4.
    
- **Missed Strikes.** If you miss an attack, the acid damage die decreases by one step, returning to 1d4 if reduced below that.
