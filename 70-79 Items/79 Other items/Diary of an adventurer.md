You are writing this for your own sanity and to continue to remember.

You came here by the calling of some cleric, the pay was good.

You went into a small town at the edge of a city. You and your group found some strange undead coming from a portal that only appeared at night. You fought them and at the recommendation of that cleric you went into the portal.

That is when things got fucked.

You entered a strange dimension, nobody could figure out where you ended up.

You followed a path to a mansion, it seemed the obvious thing to do. Inside the mansion there were cultists sacrificing people to some altar. You and your group killed them. From beyond the door you heard the sounds of something big and you didn’t know what to do.

You slept in this room trying to recuperate.

The dreams began then.

The cleric seemed the first to crack. The warlock in the group suggested that the cleric didn’t sense his god and that other things may be speaking to him now.

Your group then went ahead through the door.

The inner courtyard is guarded, do not go there anymore.

Its breath burned. You retreated. One died in the encounter.

You barricaded the door to strategize. Another day and night passed. The warlock disappeared. The cleric went to the guardian alone.

You think they are both dead.

In the night you also heard the voices. They said all the shards must be fed to the Child.

You do not want to do what the voices say.

You decided to return and find the portal. The portal was inactive.

You decided to find shelter and stay away from the mansion.

You slept in a cave and woke up in the mansion. The Child hungers.

You cannot do what the voices say. They do not care for you only for the Child.
