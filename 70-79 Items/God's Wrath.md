## Holy Avenger Greatsword

> [!Summary]
> _Weapon (Greatsword) +3, Legendary (requires attunement by a Paladin)_ 
> _Sentient magic item_
> - **Damage:** 2d6
> - **Damage Type:** Slashing
> - **Properties:** Heavy, Two-Handed
> - **Weight:** 6 lbs

This sacred weapon is a boon to those who champion the cause of righteousness. Forged in the celestial forges, it shines with divine power and serves as a symbol of ultimate justice.

**Magic Weapon.** You gain a **+3 bonus** to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with it, that creature takes an additional **2d10 radiant damage**.

**Divine Aura.** While you hold the drawn sword, it creates an **aura in a 10-foot radius** around you. You and all creatures friendly to you within the aura have **advantage on saving throws** against spells and other magical effects.

- If you have **17 or more levels in the Paladin class**, the radius of the aura increases to **30 feet**.

**Divine Transformation.** As an action, you can call upon the divine soul within the blade. For 10 minutes, you take on an aspect of an angelic being, gaining a radiant, otherworldly appearance. This ability can be used once per day and grants you two of the following effects of your choice:

- **Wings of Glory.** You gain glowing, golden wings that grant you a **flying speed equal to your walking speed**. If you are mounted, your mount also gains this flying speed.
- **Protection of Purity.** You gain the benefits of the **Protection from Evil and Good** spell.
- **Angelic Resilience.** You are immune to being **charmed, frightened**, and the effects of **exhaustion**.
- **Divine Fortitude.** You gain **resistance to necrotic and radiant damage**.
- **Eyes of the Heavens.** You gain **truesight out to a range of 120 feet**.
