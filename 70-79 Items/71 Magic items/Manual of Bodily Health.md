adventuring gear (wondrous item)

-   Category: Items

-   Item Rarity: Very Rare

-   Weight: 5

This book contains health and diet tips, and its words are charged with magic. If you spend 48 hours over a period of 6 days or fewer studying the book's [Contents](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/The%20Illrigger#h-Contents) and practicing its guidelines, your [Constitution](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Ability%20Scores#h-Constitution) score increases by 2, as does your maximum for that score. The manual then loses its magic, but regains it in a century.
