## Scion of War
Once per day, as a bonus action, you may mark a creature as your nemesis. This effect lasts for 10 minutes.
The following effects apply against that creature:
- You may add your proficiency bonus to damage
- Once per turn you may use your reaction to halve the damage received by a weapon attack from that creature.

## Avatar of War
Once per week, when the **Scion of War** ability is active, you gain the ability to become an unstoppable force on the battlefield.
You gain **immunity** to the following conditions:
- Paralysed
- Stunned
- Restrained
In addition, any effects that would reduce your movement speed do not affect you.

When a creature is marked to be your nemesis by the **Scion of War** ability, you may declare your superiority over this creature. For one round, this creature gains vulnerability to all damage. You may use this ability once per 7 days.

When brought to 0 hit points, you do not fall unconscious. You must still make Death Saving Throws and you suffer the normal effects of taking damage at 0 hit points.
