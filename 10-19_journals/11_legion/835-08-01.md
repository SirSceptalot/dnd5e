### Field Report: 835-08-01

**Location:** Village of Iglberg  
**Personnel Present:** [Arinya Vallin](/30-39_people/arinya_vallinn.md), [Tiziano Vecellio ](/30-39_people/tiziano_vecellio.md), Legion  
*Objective:* Investigation and neutralization of threats to local farmers

---

## Summary:

Under the guidance of [Arinya Vallina](/30-39_people/arinya_vallinn.md), we traveled to the village of [Iglberg](/50-59_locations/iglberg.md) to investigate reports of a deadly beast. Sightings suggested a stag-bird hybrid, matching the known descriptions of a [Peryton](/20-29_bestiary/peryton.md). The local administrator, [Emil](/30-39_people/emil.md), provided the names of witnesses and facilitated our investigation.

A dird party, [Tiziano Vecellio](/30-39_people/tiziano_vecellio.md), a Tortle, joined us voluntarily and proved competent in anatomical analysis and field tracking.

---

## Key Findings:

- **Victims and Witnesses**: All victims were adult males and personal acquaintances:
  - ***Kajetan***: Young widow, saw the attack. Describes the creature approaching from the forest.
  - **Waltraud**: Hostile witness. Described the creature lacking front limbs.
  - **Mina**: Did not witness the attack. Confirmed the victims' mutual acquaintance with one [Volker](/30-39_people/volker.md), husband to [Helmine](/30-39_people/helmine.md).

- **Autospy**: Performed by Tiziano. Confirmed that all victims had only their hearts removed. Injuries consistent with a large avian predator.

- **Gnoll Attack**: While tracking [Helmine](/30-39_people/helmine.md) into the forest, we were ambushed by a Gnoll pack. 
  - Loot recovered:
    * 3x hide armour
    * 3x wooden shield
    * 3x spear
    * 3x longbow
    * Coins totalling 22 gold

- **Helmine’S Cabin**: Unattended, entered via lockpick. Discovered ritual remnants and [Helmine's Notes](/70-79_items/helmine_notes.md), confirming transformation via dark ritual.

---

## Helmine's Notes (Evidence File):
Recovered document details:
- Infidelity by Volker prompted Helmine to seek guidance from a local entity called the "Lady of the Woods."
- Ritual required murder of Volker's lover and near-fatal injury to Volker.
- Ritual failed. Helmine transformed into a [Peryton](/20-29_bestiary/peryton.md).
- Entered period of predatory retaliation ainst the village.

---

## Final Confrontation:
- Returned to the tavern to await Helmine's return.
- She returned by morning in her beast form.
- Persuasion attempt failed. Combat initiated.
- [Helmine](/30-39_people/helmine.md) neutralized.
- Corpse delivered to [Emil](/30-39_people/emil.md).

---

## Outcome:
- Mission success. Threat confirmed and eliminated.
- Rewards:
  - 100 gold for beast
  - 15 gold bonus for Gnoll kills
  - Discount at [Heidelburg](/50-59_locations/heidelburg.md) armory negotiated
  - Lifetime access to Helmine’s cabin granted to [Redcap Boots](/30-39_people/redcap_boots.md), local tavern owner, for rental
- Redcap agrees to grant us free use of the cabin, or reserve two rooms in the Inn.

- He expresses interest in knowing what the "Lady of the Woods" is, and says he'll have some work for us in [Heidelburg](/50-59_locations/heidelburg.md).

- The new town wisdom, [Cerian Bedham](/30-39_people/cerian_bedham.md), meets us at the inn to tend to the injuries of Tiziano.
- Shortly thereafter, we end the day.
